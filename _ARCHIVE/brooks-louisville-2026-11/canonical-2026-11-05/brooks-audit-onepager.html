An outside read on your AI build, through one flavor decision

For Brooks Thomas, Louisville Brands

You asked where you are on the arc of your AI build, where it's least efficient, and which hypotheses are failing. I'd answer that through one real decision: Your criteria's call on Adobo, traced from its inputs to how it turns out, before the next 20,000-bag MOQ rides on a call like it.

Analyzing your own stuff from inside is hard: Confirmation and recency bias. An outside read checks the work you and your team have built; it doesn't replace it.

The test: Adobo

Three cases, set by where Adobo stands.
Not launched
Your criteria scores Adobo with me in the room. I write down the call, what it relies on, and the date you'll know, before the order goes in. The readout gives you that written call and the date to check it.
Launched
I put your criteria's call next to how Adobo actually sold, and trace where it held and where it didn't.
Shelved
The same test on the next flavor on the calendar.
