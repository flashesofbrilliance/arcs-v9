## BLUF (the lede)
You already hold the money and you just shipped the agent that acts on it (Command). The next layer isn't
*doing more financial work* faster: it's making every idle and every spent dollar *provably optimal*. Today
a startup's cash sits safe but unpriced, and spend is manual. I want to build the **Opportunity-Cost
Engine on Command**: a human-approved intelligence layer that prices the cost of every idle dollar in real
time, computes runway under scenarios as a first-class output, and opens the untouched spend-timing surface,
monetized by moving deposits into Mercury Treasury. I built this pitch by running that exact engine on its
own creation, and this page is the receipt.

## §1 · THE OPPORTUNITY — the residue Command doesn't yet reach
Credit where due, so you know I did the homework: **Command already** sends payments, sets auto-transfer
floor rules ("keep checking at $75k"), and rebalances Treasury funds; **Treasury already** auto-sweeps idle
cash to ~4.47% yield. The "park cash automatically" job is largely built. So I am not pitching that.
The residue, which is genuinely unbuilt, is the **intelligence** on top:
- **Opportunity-cost pricing**: "$412K sitting in checking is costing $1,540/mo vs. your Treasury rate",
  a live number, turned into a one-tap approved sweep.
- **Runway under scenarios**: burn, hiring, AR timing, computed as a first-class Command output, the thing
  every founder Slack-messages their VC about.
- **Spend-timing**: the untouched surface, an AP calendar that weighs early-pay discounts against runway.
  Command *executes* payments; it does not yet *optimize when and whether* to pay.

## §2 · WHY NOW — three converging windows
1. **Command / CLI / MCP just shipped** (June 2026). The execution rails exist; the intelligence layer that
   makes them *decide well* is the natural, now-possible next step, not a from-scratch bet.
2. **You are becoming the bank for AI-native startups**, and those founders expect their money to think.
   The $20B in deposits you hold is the largest untapped signal in startup finance.
3. **Ramp is racing the other way in**: it shipped autonomous finance agents, spend-first. The yield/runway
   half, on a $20B deposit base, is the flank Ramp does not own and you are uniquely placed to hold.

## §3 · WHY MERCURY — the unfair distribution and the aligned incentive
- **The distribution**: $20B deposits, ~300K customers (roughly one in three US startups), and Command as
  the surface. No standalone "AI CFO" startup can reach these founders on day one; you already have them.
- **The incentive is aligned, not bolted on**: the engine monetizes by **moving idle deposits into Mercury
  Treasury**, which is directly accretive to your core revenue. It makes the product better *and* the P&L
  better in the same motion.
- **The guardrail is respected**: everything is human-approved, inside Command's existing consent model and
  your read-only MCP posture. This is an analyst that recommends, not an agent that moves money on its own.

## §5 · WHAT I'D BUILD — the 0→1 layer
An **Opportunity-Cost + Runway + AP-timing intelligence layer on Command**, human-approved end to end:
- A continuously-running analyst that prices idle-dollar opportunity cost against Mercury Treasury and
  proposes one-tap approved sweeps.
- Runway-under-scenarios as a first-class Command surface.
- The spend-timing engine: an AP pay-calendar weighing early-pay discounts against runway, proposed for
  human approval, never auto-executed.

## §9 · PRE-MORTEM — how this fails (so we don't)
- **It's a thin wrapper**: if "rebalance Treasury funds" in Command already does the optimization, the
  wedge shrinks. *Phase 0 tests this first with a live product check; if it's already there, pivot to
  spend-timing, which is definitively unbuilt.*
- **Compliance says no to yield claims**: mitigated by building the framing with legal from day one, which
  is exactly the regulated-product motion I've run before.
- **Founders don't engage**: if the opportunity-cost nudge doesn't move deposits, kill it at the Phase-0
  gate, don't scale a dead metric.
