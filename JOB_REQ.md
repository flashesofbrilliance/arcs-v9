# Head of Growth Intelligence
**Fanatics Collectibles · West Village, NYC**

---

## The role

A new function that doesn't exist yet.

The growth org at Fanatics is doubling roughly every four months. What it needs — and doesn't have — is an institutional memory layer: a system that captures learning from every drop, campaign, and live event, cross-references it across five business units, and makes each subsequent decision sharper than the last.

This role builds that system.

Chris Lamontagne, SVP Growth, Fanatics Collectibles

## What you own

- **Pre-decision framing** — structured context before major growth calls (drops, campaigns, live events)
- **Post-decision capture** — what was predicted, what happened, why. Committed to record, not a Slack thread.
- **Cross-BU intelligence** — patterns across Instant Rips, Trading Cards, Live Commerce, VA (Paris), Fanatics Live
- **The workflow stack** — built on tooling already live at Fanatics: Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, Glean, Notion

## What you build

Not dashboards. Not reporting. Decision instruments — tools that sharpen what happens next, not summaries of what already happened.

## What success looks like

| Day | Output |
|-----|--------|
| 30 | Decision anatomy map for Instant Rips |
| 60 | First audit brief — a real tool Chase's team uses before the next drop |
| 90 | First cross-BU signal report |

---

## Who fits

- Has operated in high-velocity environments where decisions outpaced process
- Builds tools, not presentations
- Thinks in systems: what does the org learn, and how does that compound?
- Comfortable with ambiguity — this function doesn't have a template
- Player-coach: you're doing the work, not managing people who do it

## What this isn't

BI. Analytics. A CoE that sets policy. A function that slows GMs down.

The GMs run their businesses. This role makes the learning from those businesses work harder.
