Closed course concierge test

Before building any learning path, walk five people through one closed-course decision and one small real one by hand, and see whether they come back to close it.

§ The short version
What the funnel saysThe read-only count found 271 receipts but only 6 distinct owners, 4 users who were sent a first debrief, 0 who opted in to more, and 3 outcomes. You have not gone to market, so this is not a market funnel and says nothing about demand in either direction. It is the founder-and-tests baseline, and it shows the loop is rarely closed even by the people closest to it.
The testA first positioning test, not a demand verdict. Five people, two of whom owe you nothing. One closed-course case with a hidden outcome (immediate feedback), then one small reversible real decision with a written trigger, abort and rollback, then a check at day 7. No build, nothing published, nothing sent by me.
The kill lineFewer than 2 of the people who volunteer a real decision come back and close it unprompted: drop the learning-path framing and keep the readiness packet as an internal tool.
§ Decisions waiting on you

D1Run the five-person concierge test?

Recommended: Yes. You pick the five and send the invitations yourself; I draft the script and the log sheet only.

It costs hours, builds nothing and tests the one thing that matters: do people return to close.

D2Who counts as one of the five?

Recommended: At least two who owe you nothing and have a decision of their own pending. The rest can be people you know.

Friends are polite. A polite yes is the agreement without dissent the Shavasana check flags.

D3Which closed-course case first?

Recommended: Case file 001 (the six-week outage) at good.arcs.care/cases, after one check: the outcome must be hideable until the learner has logged a prediction.

Both case pages exist with evidence cards that reveal as fog clears. Neither is confirmed to ask for a prediction first.

D4Hold the fake-door page until at least three concierge cohorts are in?

Recommended: Yes. A public page that collects emails needs your go and a privacy line first, and one cohort cannot settle positioning.

Publishing and collecting addresses are outward-facing and cannot be taken back.

§ Heuristics: for you, and for me
For you (human)
  • Set the numbers in the signal table yourself before the first session. Do not change them after.
  • A person who says it was great but does not return at day 7 is a no. Count returns, not praise.
  • Ask at day 7 without a reminder from you. A reminder you send tests your nudge, not their demand.
For the agent (machine)
  • Send nothing. Draft only; Zach sends. No form, no page, no email collection from this proposal.
  • Log by participant code, never by name or address. Aggregate only in any file I write.
  • The signal thresholds are Zach's. The ones below are suggestions until he sets them.

§ Positioning arms, tested over time

You have not tested positioning systematically yet. Five people cannot rank framings; they can only tell you which words made someone act. So the same script runs in repeated small cohorts over several weeks, each cohort hearing one framing, and the results are read as a trend, never as a winner from one round.

ArmFraming in the two-minute briefWhat it is testing
A CalibrationLearn how well your confidence matches what happensDoes a measurement promise get people to close?
B ReadinessBe ready before a decision you cannot undoDoes irreversibility get people to prepare?
C Close the loopFinish what you decided and see what you learnedDoes the habit framing get people to return?
RuleRotate one arm per cohort, same script, same measures, 3 or more weeks apartRecord the arm per participant code. Do not mix arms inside a cohort.
Honest limitUNCONFIRMEDWith 5 people per cohort, differences between arms will look large and mean little. Read direction across 3 or more cohorts.
§ Concierge script (about 40 minutes plus a day-7 check)

Everything here is done by hand. Nothing is built or published.

StepWhat happensWhat is recorded
1 BriefTwo minutes: what a decision receipt is and what closing it means, in the framing of this cohort's armThe arm shown (A, B or C)
2 Closed courseThe learner reads a case with the outcome hidden, writes a prediction and a confidence from 0 to 100Prediction and confidence, saved before the reveal
3 RevealThe outcome is shown. The learner sees the gap between stated confidence and what happenedWhether they could say why they were off
4 PermitThe learner picks one small, reversible real decision due within 7 days and writes the six packet lines: door type, prepared state, what is unconfirmed, trigger, abort and rollback, confidence against felt readThe six lines, filled or marked unconfirmed
5 Day 7No reminder from you. Note whether they come back and report the outcomeReturned or not, matched or not, their stated confidence
6 LicenseA learner who closes one real decision gets their calibration line: stated confidence against outcomeThat line, nothing else

§ Proposed controls and how we will know each works

Each control is a guess until its test runs. A test has a known-bad case that must fail and a known-good case that must pass.

C1Hidden outcome stays hidden until the prediction is saved proposed

The closed-course case must not reveal the outcome before a prediction and confidence are recorded.

Must fail
A case page whose outcome is visible in the page text before any prediction field exists fails.
Must pass
A case where the outcome appears only after a prediction is logged passes.

Test status: specified; the two existing case pages have not been checked against this · line: 0 outcome strings visible before prediction

C2Permit needs all six lines proposed

A real decision is accepted into the test only if the six packet lines are present, each either filled or marked unconfirmed.

Must fail
A packet with no trigger or no abort and rollback fails.
Must pass
A packet with all six lines passes, even if some say unconfirmed.

Test status: specified · line: 6 of 6 lines present

C3Signal frozen before the first session proposed

The pass and kill lines are written and dated before the first learner, then not changed.

Must fail
Thresholds edited after a session has started fail.
Must pass
A dated file whose thresholds match the table passes.

Test status: specified · line: thresholds unchanged after first session date

§ Glossary
Closed course
A past decision whose outcome is already known, used so a learner gets feedback in minutes instead of weeks.
Permit and license
The permit is a first small real decision logged with its safeguards. The license is the first one closed with a calibration reading.
Calibration line
How far a person's stated confidence was from what actually happened.
Fake door
A page offering something that does not exist yet, used to count interest. Held here until the concierge result is in.
