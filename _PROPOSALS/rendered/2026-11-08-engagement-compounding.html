Engagement compounding

Five additions to the engagement record so every client leaves behind a measured cost of winning it, a measured gap between plan and reality, and parts the next client gets for free.

Markdown source: _PROPOSALS/2026-11-08-engagement-compounding-design.md

Why

Each client engagement so far was built by hand and kept nothing for the next one. My Ally shows the cost: about 30 to 45 unpaid hours (calls measured; work sessions estimated from 249 commits; emails at an assumed 15 minutes each) before a $1,500 first step. Counting Step 1's 12 hours, that is roughly $25 to $35 an hour. The number only exists because one agent dug it up once. PRL showed the same pattern earlier ($6,200 mostly on discovery). A lesson that lives in memory and not in a check repeats.

What

Five additions to the engagement record so that every engagement leaves three things behind: a measured cost of winning it, a measured gap between what I planned and what happened, and reusable parts (gift skills, a client page, a morning check) that the next client gets for free.

Why now

The pieces just landed or are in review: one record per engagement (merged), the honest-proposals v1 record format (PR 2072), and progress plus payment-released gifts (PR 2066). My Ally Step 1 starts within days, so its actual hours can be captured from the first one instead of reconstructed. The next engagement (Louisville or another) would otherwise copy My Ally's one-off scripts.

How

Before (now): this design. Nothing built. During (after your go): merge 2072, 2059, 2066 in that order, then build Move 1 and Move 2 during My Ally Step 1. After: Moves 3 to 5 start when a second engagement signs, not before. A claim that the calibration means ything waits for three finished steps.

How to read: Section 1 is the five moves in one table. Sections 2 to 6 are one move each. Section 7 is order and cost. Section 8 is what it will not do. Section 9 is the case against. Section 10 is what I need from you.

2. Move 1: hours ledger

  • Record. An investment block, append-only like progress:
    • calls[]: {date, minutes, source, basis} where basis is measured (Granola or calendar duration) or estimated.
    • work_paths[]: the repo paths that belong to this engagement (for My Ally: the hub, the record folder, the scaffold folder). Sessions are computed, not typed.
    • emails: {sent, minutes_each, basis: "assumed"}.
    • paid_from: the date the first paid step started. Hours before it are unpaid discovery.
  • Script. engagement_hours.py --dir <engagement> prints calls, sessions (commit clusters on work_paths, a gap over 90 minutes starts a new session, plus 30 minutes lead-in), emails, and a low/high total with each part's basis. It is the method the agent used on 2026-11-08, made repeatable. Stdlib, read-only.
  • Tripwire. A discovery_cap_hours field (you set it; I suggest 10). When unpaid hours pass it, the script exits non-zero, proposal_lint.py (PR 2072) warns on any new client-facing draft, and the session-start sentry names the engagement. It warns; it does not block, since sometimes free work is a choice.
  • Never client-facing by default. The number can go on a client page only by an explicit field, and then only as a range ("roughly 30 to 45 hours").

3. Move 2: planned vs actual

  • Progress entries already carry the date a row finished. Add an optional actual_hours to done entries. The session that does the work logs it (the standing rule already says sessions log progress).
  • engagement_calibrate.py reads every engagement and reports, by row kind (task, report, cost range, call): planned hours vs actual, planned day vs finished day, and how often a client gate (access, payment) was the delay.
  • No claim before three finished steps (rule of three). Until then the report prints counts and says "not enough data". After that, honest-proposals v1 ranges can cite it as their source instead of judgment.
  • Private always: calibration never renders into a client page.

4. Move 3: gift library

  • _ARTIFACTS/gift-skills/catalog.json: one entry per skill with id, repo, pinned_sha, path, license, fit tags (non-technical team, builder, founder, finance), and a one-line plain-language "what it does for you".
  • Rows in a record name gift: "<id>" instead of copying a file; the unlock rule from PR 2066 stays per row.
  • The copy served to a client is fetched at the pinned commit, and a --check fails if it drifts, so a fix in the public repo is a deliberate re-pin, not a silent change on a client page.
  • honest-proposals can then suggest gifts by fit when drafting the "included at no charge" section.

5. Move 4: one sync for every client page

  • Each record gets a hub block: page file, the marker block name, the download folder, the host.
  • scripts/engagement_sync.py does what myally_hub_sync.py does today for any record with a hub block. myally_hub_sync.py stays as a two-line wrapper so nothing that calls it breaks (house rule: never overwrite a script in place for a breaking change).
  • Routing stays one explicit line per host in src/proxy.ts, so the surface catalog check keeps seeing every host.

6. Move 5: morning evidence sweep

  • The existing weekday morning note (arcs-open-loops-morning-note) gains one section: for each engagement with an active step, new evidence since yesterday (a reply in the thread, a GitHub or Lovable invite, a payment notice), and the progress entries I propose from it, with the client-facing note already drafted.
  • It only proposes. Client-facing notes and payment releases go live when you say yes, in the session where you do.

8. What it will not do

  • Send, publish or bill anything. No invoicing system.
  • Put calibration or unpaid hours on a client page without an explicit field set by you.
  • Pool one client's data into another client's page.
  • Claim the estimates got better before three finished steps.

9. The case against

Building infrastructure for 1.5 clients is the same front-loading this design warns about. The answer is the trigger column: only Moves 1 and 2 are built now, because they cost a day, they start paying during My Ally Step 1, and they turn the unpaid-discovery lesson into a check that runs. Moves 3 to 5 wait for a second signed engagement; if none comes, they are never built, and nothing is lost.
