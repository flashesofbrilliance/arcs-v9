# Doors of perception: the first touch

## True north

**Keep your head, your heart, and your wits, in the age of AI.**

The purpose behind it: survive modern times with your soul intact, and use technology in a way that
is good for you and for society.

| | Path grouping | Factors |
|---|---|---|
| Head | Wisdom | Right view, right intention |
| Heart | Conduct | Right speech, right action, right livelihood |
| Wits | Practice | Right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration |

## The reframe

ARCS has been positioned on decision science and decision geometry. The thread's premise: most people
care more about the shift in perspective than the forensic receipt. The receipt does not go away; it
becomes the proof that the shift was real.

- People buy the view; the receipt proves the view is real.
- The truest second opinion is your first one, handed back.
- The model is Anton Ego in Ratatouille: braced to judge, served a peasant dish, carried back to his
  mother's table. The shift is recognition, not novelty. It regrounds you in what you already knew.
- His written review is the receipt: a public, costly account of a changed mind.

## Category

Not a digital brain (substitution, which NOTOMATION forbids). Not an exoskeleton alone (amplifies
wherever you push, mistakes included). A sparring partner: thinks with you, pushes back, never takes
the controls. Mechanism: an exoskeleton with resistance built in.

| | Goes along with you | Pushes back |
|---|---|---|
| Does the work for you | agents, automations | rare |
| Thinks with you | copilots, thought partners | ARCS |

The larger whitespace is a category, not a tagline: embodied feedback loops on judgment, learning
verified by what actually happened. Name it only after there is proof; enter through a job people
already know.

## The stack

1. Front door (individual): **A second opinion that pushes back.**
   Subline: *Bring it what you're stuck on. Leave with what you already knew.*
2. Premise: people want to do the pre-work; they lack the structure, not the will. Promise efficiency,
   never discipline. No shame anywhere.
3. Enterprise thesis: growth mindset by necessity, not choice. Upskilling fails on identity, not
   curriculum. When the role changes, the identity has to follow; ARCS keeps a record that it stuck.
   ("Shadow work" and "ego death" stay internal names; never on a front door.)
4. Engine (second page only): imprint method, receipts, decision geometry, L0 to L5.

Flow, without the word: *Every pull against it moves you forward.* A rower faces backward and steers
by the wake.

## The hallway of doors

Sequential stages, not a menu. Doors are never labelled with their pattern; the visitor chooses by
feel. Which door they reach and what they type is a first, thin register read.

1. **Grain door (PULL).** The only tool is a push hand. Push 1 (shudder): *Try pushing a little
   harder.* Push 2: *You can't push your way through a door marked pull.* The hand becomes a grip;
   pulling opens it. Lesson: *Read the grain before you push.* (Resistance still propels, as with
   oars; the skill is aiming effort.)
2. **Mirror door.** The door is a mirror; neither push nor pull works. *What are you sure of here?
   Could you show someone why?* It opens on an honest admission of one belief held without evidence.
   Lesson: *Sure isn't the same as shown.* Admission must feel like relief, never a gotcha.
   - Traversal: the loop becomes a spiral. Each small action nudges the circle a notch upward; haptics
     go from heavy drag to light lift. *Same circle. One notch higher.* Every step is a vote for who
     you're becoming (identity-based habits; do not name the source on the page).
3. Release door: open (the "already ajar" candidate).

Mobile: hold duration stands in for pressure (true pressure sensing is gone on modern iPhones).
Escalating vibration on Android; iOS Safari gets at most a light tick, so visual strain and sound carry
it. Pull is a swipe toward yourself. Desktop uses the cursor; keyboard holds Space; a skip link exists.

## The eightfold DAG

The path is a wheel practiced together, not a ladder. First touch opens a few doors; the rest unlock
on return.

| # | Factor | ARCS embodiment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Right view | Mirror door: sure isn't the same as shown |
| 2 | Right intention | People want to do the pre-work; supply structure |
| 3 | Right speech | Rhetorical resonance and the codex: facts invariant, delivery in their register; a "how I read you" toggle shows the instrument |
| 4 | Right action | Pull, not push: same strength, used the way the door works |
| 5 | Right livelihood | Workforce thesis: the identity follows the role |
| 6 | Right effort | Grain door: read the grain before you push |
| 7 | Right mindfulness | The detached gaze: atlas, ziggurat, HUD; rise to see the whole terrain, then descend to the one point that matters (red thread to needle) |
| 8 | Right concentration | Oars, flow, spiral: every pull moves you forward |

Groupings: wisdom (1, 2), conduct (3, 4, 5), practice (6, 7, 8).

## Guardrails

- Refuse to overclaim: the page shows the shape of compounding; receipts at 30 and 90 days prove it.
- Earned fidelity: first touch shows a foothill with the peak in fog, never a finished summit.
- One line is not a codex: label the register read "first read, will sharpen."
- Resonance never shades the facts toward what they want to hear; the pushback proves it.
- No borrowed brands on the page (Clear, Atomic Habits).

## Cheapest tests (none run)

   it does.

   gets quoted back.

   enterprise thesis.

   the premise.
