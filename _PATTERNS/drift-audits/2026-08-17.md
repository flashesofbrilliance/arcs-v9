  bypassing the exact protection the 2026-07-24 hardening built.
- One-line fix: delete the `kelly-interview.arcs.care` entry from
  `vercel.json`'s `rewrites` array. This is a one-line, zero-risk deletion
  that has now been recommended identically four times — flagging to a human
