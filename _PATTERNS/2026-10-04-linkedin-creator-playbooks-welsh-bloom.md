# LinkedIn creator playbooks: Welsh and Bloom, fitted to Notomation

All content below is paraphrase. No posts, newsletter text, or course material reproduced. Course content
(LinkedIn OS, Content OS) was not accessed; only public essays, interviews, and third-party reviews.

## TL;DR

- Both systems are distribution machines built on one move: **a repeatable format applied to a narrow topic, every
  weekday, with the newsletter as the place trust compounds.** Neither one wins on single brilliant posts.
- Zach's numbers (4,504 impressions, 68% out-of-network, 6 saves vs 1 comment, 8 link visits) say the reach problem
  is solved for now. The leak is **reach to conversation** and **conversation to compass run**. Both creators close
  that leak with comment-reply labor and an owned hop (profile Featured plus newsletter), not with better hooks.
- Adopt Welsh's **mechanics** (matrix, link-in-comment, profile as landing page, reply hour). Adopt Bloom's
  **artifact** (the named framework card, saveable and shareable). Reject both creators' certainty register.
- Highest-leverage change: every cut ends on **one runnable thing**, a compass question the reader can paste into
  app.arcs.care, not "read the essay." Saves are already telling us readers want a tool to keep.
- Run one A/B in the 10-12 week: framework-card exec image vs the current scorecard image, judged on saves plus
  comments per 1,000 impressions, rule written below before posting.

## Diagnosis of the current numbers

Data (Zach's screenshot, 2026-10-04): top post 2,359 impressions / 7 engagements. Period: 4,504 impressions,
2,676 members reached, 68% out-of-network, 7 reactions, 1 comment, 0 reposts, 6 saves, 0 sends, 8 link visits.
All diagnosis below is INFERRED; n is tiny and one post dominates.

1. **Saves 6, comments 1.** A save means "useful later, but I have nothing to add in public." Welsh's lens: the
   close asks for an opinion the reader does not feel qualified to give in front of colleagues. Bloom's lens: the
   content is reference material, and reference material gets kept, not discussed. Both point the same way:
   the posts read as tools. Give the reader the tool (a card, a question to run) and a lower-risk reply prompt
   ("which of these have you seen?" beats "do you agree?").
2. **68% out-of-network.** The feed is distributing to strangers on topic (Apple, AI) rather than through Zach's
   graph. Good for discovery, bad for comments: strangers rarely reply to someone they do not know. Welsh's
   reply hour and Bloom's early "reply under bigger accounts" habit both convert strangers into people who
   recognize the name. 0 sends also says nobody thought "my colleague needs this," which a named framework fixes
   better than a hot take.
3. **8 link visits on 4,504 impressions (about 0.18%).** Link visits are the only metric that reaches an owned
   surface. Nothing in the funnel currently asks the visitor to do one thing on arrival. Welsh's answer is a
   newsletter signup; Zach's equivalent is a pre-filled compass question. Without that, link visits end at reading.
4. **Compass runs: unmeasured from LinkedIn.** The real goal metric has no attribution. Until a UTM or a
   distinct sample-question landing exists, no queue change can be judged against it.

## Five changes for the next two weeks

1. **2026-10-05-apple-openai-ledger.json, tactical post 10-08:** replace the closing line with a two-option close:
   a low-stakes question ("Which Apple call would you score first?") and "or run your own call through the compass"
   pointing to app.arcs.care with a UTM (e.g. `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=2026-10-05-ledger`). Link in
   first comment, per the existing playbook.
2. **2026-10-12-four-dates-receipts.json, exec image 10-12:** recut as a named framework card, "The Falsifier
   Ledger": three rows (call, date, what would prove it wrong) plus an NCI line. This is the A/B treatment below.
3. **All three queues, poll cuts (10-06, 10-13, 10-20):** add a fifth-line "why I'm asking" with the result
   promise, and reply to every voter who comments within 2 hours. Polls are the only cut that earns participation
   from out-of-network strangers cheaply.
4. **2026-10-19-any-stranger-bar-raiser.json:** this queue has `evidence: []`. Its story is personal so it needs no
   metric, but the carousel (10-21) should end on a "bar raiser check" framework card (3 questions to ask before
   forwarding anyone's work), giving the save-prone reader a thing to keep. Leave the recruiter unnamed (counterpart
   pseudonym rule).
5. **Profile, before 10-08:** Featured slot 1 = app.arcs.care with one sample question in the description; slot 2 =
   good.arcs.care/series/receipts. Headline names the outcome (scored, falsifiable calls) rather than the product.
   Zero queue edits, all Zach clicks.

### A/B (pre-registered, written 2026-10-04 before either post exists)

- **Control:** 10-05 exec image (scorecard line, current format).
- **Treatment:** 10-12 exec image recut as the Falsifier Ledger framework card (change 2).
- Same weekday, same 9:00 AM ET slot, same hashtag count, link in first comment for both.
- **Metric:** (saves + comments + sends) per 1,000 impressions, read 72 hours after posting.
- **Success rule:** treatment wins only if its rate is at least 1.5x control AND it has at least 5 such
  engagements in absolute terms. Anything else is "no decision," not a loss and not a win. Impressions are reported
  but are not the judge.
- **Known confound:** different topics (Apple ledger vs prediction habit). One pair cannot separate format from
  topic; a win earns a second pair, not a rule.

## SEED CANDIDATES AND FORKABLE IDEAS

1. **`framework` cut type in `scripts/linkedin_cut.py`**: a sixth format (image card) with fields `name`,
   `rows` (3 to 5, under 12 words each), `falsifier` (required). Gate: no card without a falsifier line.
   Kill: if the A/B above and one follow-up pair both return "no decision" or a loss, drop the format.
2. **Post-to-compass funnel**: each tactical post carries a sample question that deep-links into the compass with a
   UTM, and `/api/compass` logs the campaign on the receipt. Kill: fewer than 3 attributed compass runs across 6
   posts with the link.
3. **Reply-hour receipt**: a tiny log (post id, replies sent in first 2 hours, comments received at 72 hours) so the
   reply-labor claim gets measured on Zach's own account. Kill: after 6 posts, no correlation visible between replies
   sent and comments received.
4. **Profile-as-landing-page check**: a quarterly checklist item (Featured slots point at live owned URLs; headline
   names an outcome). Kill: if Featured clicks are not visible in LinkedIn analytics after 30 days, retire the check.
5. **Content matrix as queue generator**: shows x 4 styles grid in the induced backlog, so idea selection is a
   lookup, not a blank page. Kill: if 2 consecutive weekly queues are still drafted from scratch instead of the grid.
6. **Library hop**: every post links the series index rather than the single essay (Bloom's rabbit hole). Kill: if
   index-page visits per link visit are not higher than single-essay visits over 2 weeks (needs analytics read).
