## The prime invariant — reverse-JTBD (the keel)

User0 is the **prime invariant**: the oddest tool for the job that has not yet found the rarest role
whose jobs-to-be-done it uniquely satisfies. The search arrow is INVERTED from normal JTBD — not
tool-for-the-job (which candidate fits this role) but **role-for-the-tool** (which JTBD is
unsatisfiable without this exact combination).

- **fit ≠ leverage.** Applicable-to-many = commodity; only-tool-for-one = moat. Magnetize for the
  only-tool role, not the applicable ones. Blacksmith was one instance of that rare role.
- **Oddness IS the selectivity — never sand it off to broaden reach.** The odd shape repels the
  common jobs (good; they were scatter) and resonates only with the rare JTBD. Full-fidelity oddness
  is what makes the rare role findable and unfillable-by-anyone-else. Normalizing to look applicable
  flattens the well and attracts tourists.
- **The invariant must survive every projection.** Role, gatekeeper, pitch are changes of basis; the
  tool's true shape is the lossless invariant (field-geometry canon). A pitch that preserves it is
  honest; one that strains to fit is deforming the tool for a job that was never its rare job.

### The gravity forge (blacksmith.arcs.care = reference implementation)

**The triad-closer is a PAIR of air-gapped objects, not one** (corrected against the blacksmith.arcs.care
reference, audited 2026-09-08):

- **The forge (founder-aimed)** — `public/blacksmith.html`. Aims PAST the external gatekeeper at the
  internal one (founders / role owner). Does three things: (1) **stands alone** — the external
  gatekeeper forwards the link and it emits without her re-transmitting (she carries the link, the
  object carries the technical fidelity she can't); (2) **proof of competency/efficacy** — the
  crossover made checkable (live MCP, status.arcs.care commit trail, co-authored provenance, the
  ship-clock, and the forge itself as a working demo of the hired skill); (3) **credits + protects
  the gatekeeper** ("reached me through Amy... no going around the front door"), which makes her look
  good to the founders without being in her register.
- **The enablement (gatekeeper-aimed, TWO-TIER)** — `recruithaus.arcs.care` (open re-sell script:
  two-hires-in-one, why-beats-a-specialist, "lines you can repeat," the sleeper/maturity-over-size
  framing) + gated `recruithaus.arcs.care/ammo` (comp logic = top-of-band-as-discount, archetype
  battle cards, best/base/worst + circuit breakers). The open tier is safe if a founder saw it; the
  gated tier reads as backchannel and must stay gated.

**Air-gap = argument-ownership-by-vertex, not just secrecy** (the generalizable principle, from the
blacksmith + recruithaus system, audited 2026-09-08). Each JTBD note is answered in the object whose
audience OWNS that argument: ownership / speed / engineer-is-the-end-user → the forge (founders);
maturity-over-size (the "sleeper" framing) + comp → the recruiter enablement (Amy's argument to make
to the team). Placing an argument on the wrong vertex is the defect — e.g. "why a non-big-co hire is
right" asserted on the founder page reads defensive; in Amy's re-sell it reads as her judgment.

**Structural job:** convert the unstable 2-body (candidate ↔ external gatekeeper — collapses if she
must carry the technical proof herself) into a load-bearing 3-body **triad** by pulling the internal
gatekeeper into the field. The forge is the third vertex / connective tissue holding the other two in
tension. This is HOW an external gatekeeper multiplies reach: not by her re-selling, but by her
setting down a self-emitting founder-aimed object in front of the people who decide, while she keeps
the air-gapped enablement for her own re-sell. Neither object alone closes the triad; the pair does.

**Template:** every external-gatekeeper triple earns the PAIR (built via the forge /
teardown-page-factory / recruithaus patterns). blacksmith.arcs.care + tear-sheet-amy.html is the
User0 reference: the founder-aimed magnet + the air-gapped gatekeeper enablement.

## Polarity (anti-scatter) — the load-bearing rule

A magnet that emits everything attracts noise: off-basin recruiters, generic "you're technical"
pulls, tourists. **Sharp polarity = emit ONE crossover signal, ONE basin register**, so the right
shape feels the pull and the wrong shape feels nothing. Diffuse emission = divergent inbound. This is
the same convergent/divergent test as the outbound atoms, now on the emission side. Resist the urge
to broaden the message to "reach more people" — broadening flattens the well.

## Field-strength readout (how you know it's working)

- **Inbound convergence ratio:** fraction of inbound (roles, gatekeeper DMs, intros) that is
  on-basin. Rising = field sharpening; falling = polarity diffusing, re-tighten.
- **Leading indicator:** basin-people engaging your emissions BEFORE any role exists — the field
  pulling ahead of any transaction. That is magnetism, distinct from applying.
- **Kairos:** when inbound convergence crosses threshold, outbound sourcing cost falls toward zero.
  The well pulls; you stop chasing.

## The flywheel (inbound + outbound are one system)

Outbound engagement (follow the basin, engage only convergent results) trains the recommender to
show YOUR emissions to the basin → emissions raise magnetism → inbound rises → outbound cost falls.
Projects-in-flight feed the yapping, which feeds the founder-led surface, which raises magnetism.
Self-reinforcing: the outbound atoms are the primer, magnetism is the steady state. You are not
building a job-search; you are building a gravity well and letting the convergent basin fall in.
