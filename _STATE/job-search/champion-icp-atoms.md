pitch.** Three legs; the pitch is the PROJECTION of the candidate into the role shape, rendered in the
gatekeeper's register — an output, not a leg.

- **Leg 2 — gatekeeper (internal | external):** who controls access; the flag is the ROUTING KEY for

  Resume+LinkedIn input, `_SPECS/self-serve-atlas-scan-v1.md`) and this is the ARCS talent-placement
  primitive for anyone — the champion machine is the User0 instance of a class.

|---|---|---|---|
| **External** | Retained/boutique search (Amy), fund talent partner | Arm them to re-sell you + justify comp to a third party (two-hires-in-one, de-risk). They carry it inside. Multiplies reach. | `_ARTIFACTS/resume-blacksmith/tear-sheet-amy.html` |

strain — divergent, pass. The A/B/C query families below FEED this triple (role shapes, gatekeepers,
basins); the triple is what you actually work.

**A1 — operator-turned-search (the direct Amy analog).** People-search keyword box:

Filters: Connections = **2nd** (warm path first) · Past company or title includes an operator role.

AND (founding OR "first hires" OR "0 to 1" OR "early team") NOT (agency OR "staffing")

> **Cut: VC/fund talent partners.** Per Khosla, most add negative value; a fund talent partner is a
> portfolio-referral volume seat, not someone who recognizes the pair or decides. Not a gatekeeper.
> The valuable external gatekeeper is the Amy shape (A1), not whoever wrote the check.

**A3 — the founder/Chief-of-Staff who hires generalists** (a champion need not be a recruiter):
```
(founder OR "chief of staff" OR "founding team") AND (generalist OR athlete OR "0 to 1" OR "zero to one" OR "wearer of many hats")
AND (agent OR "applied AI" OR devtools OR "developer tools")
```

**A4 — Amy's own adjacency (warm, one-hop).** Use LinkedIn's "People also viewed" / mutual-connection

**Alert:** LinkedIn does not alert on people-searches; set these as **saved searches** you re-run
weekly, and turn "follow" on the top 5 so their posts enter your feed (engagement trains the well).

## The convergence test (gravity well vs scatter)

Run the three streams for a week, then check overlap — this is the whole point of atomizing:

1. **Triangulation hits (the well is real):** a company in C that has a role in B and a person in A
   one hop from you. Rank these first; a triangulated node is a champion-backed, shape-fit, warm role.
2. **Convergence ratio:** of your A/B/C hits, what fraction co-locate on the same companies/nodes?
   Rising ratio = the well is deepening. Flat/falling = the queries are scattering across basins;
   re-tighten the domain gate (drop MSP-only, generic SWE, big-co PM).
3. **Feed discipline (you are training the attractor):** LinkedIn's recommender learns from what you
   engage. Only click/save/follow results that fit the basin. Convergent engagement deepens the well;
   engaging scattered results flattens it back to noise. This is the single cheapest lever and it is
   entirely in your hands.

**Left convergent, this constellates:** the right champions surface the right companies surface the
right roles, each reinforcing the others, and Amy-class people start appearing in your "People you
may know" unprompted. Left divergent, you get a busy feed that pulls in every direction and no well
forms. The domain gate + feed discipline are what keep it convergent.
