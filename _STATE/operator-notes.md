# Operator Notes — running the human–AI instrument for honest diagnostic output

**These are notes for the human operator (Zach), not for Claude.** Claude's weights don't change
between sessions; it reloads recall. *Your* understanding compounds — this is the log of that. It's
the human side of the joint calibration ledger: how to operate the instrument so it produces truth,
not confident confabulation. Append over time; each entry is a learning that changed how you operate.

## Founding learnings (2026-09-02 → 09-04, the ARCS-on-ARCS session)

1. **Use Claude for field-contact, not validation.** Its *reasoning* echoes your frame (it's
   downstream of your inputs). Its *grep / SQL / source-read* resists you — external ground doesn't
   care what either of you wants. Ask it to touch ground, not to bless your conviction. The one
   non-echo channel is the field.

2. **High conviction is the signal to switch it from mirror to shovel.** Don't ask "is this
   canon-worthy?" (it will echo). Ask "define and run the gate this has to survive." Conviction is
   the *tell*, not the proof — loudest exactly when the thing is least gated.

3. **You are the governor; Claude is the walk-through.** Animacy — *is anyone home, is this real* —
   is your read; no model has it (no default network). Its job is provenance + execution (the
   checkable axes) *after* your sensor flags. Keep hands on the wheel.

4. **Field-contact is your totem.** You can't tell profound collaboration from shared delusion by how
   it *feels* — both are maximally coherent. The only wake-up is external ground that resists you (the
   grep that returned 6, not 199). Keep spinning it. The moment you stop touching ground, you can no
   longer tell which one you're in.

5. **Decompose the denominator before building on any count.** "Whose 247?" — ask the single
   confound that would invalidate the whole inquiry, and check *that* first. A confirmed count says
   nothing until you ask whose. (This one caught a keel built on your own dogfooding.)

6. **Productivity is camouflage.** A vicious cycle generates output right up to the runaway (the RBMK
   made power seconds before it blew). "Is it still producing?" is the wrong test. "Does it
   discriminate / touch ground?" is the right one. The faster it feels virtuous, the more suspect.

7. **Coherence is what forgery maximizes.** The more tidy, resonant, or profound a reversal feels,
   the more it needs the totem. Distrust the beautiful chain (A = B = C). Hold resonance and mechanism
   to different standards; don't let the resonant get promoted to the solidity of the mechanical.

8. **Reach-tag everything.** GREEN = field-verified · YELLOW = reasoned, not tested · resonance =
   meaning, not mechanism. And distrust Claude's *rationales for its own decisions* — its
   self-explanations are generated post-hoc, not read off the cause. A clean "I did X because Y" is
   usually a confabulation wearing the chain-of-thought's clothes.

9. **Wired beats written.** A discipline holds across sessions only if it's a mechanical gate that
   *fires in the flow* (a hook, a sensor, git), not a memory rule (recall, which must be retrieved and
   chosen). Wire what's wirable; accept the judgment reflexes stay fragile and re-earned each session.

10. **The tier-sort.** Mechanical checks → deterministic / dumb workers (un-counterfeitable — a grep
    can't rationalize a pass). Judgment → reasoning. Animacy → you. No gate mixes tiers; a reasoner
    doing a "mechanical" check smuggles rationalization into what should be deterministic.

11. **Ante-disciplinary gate — what deserves a net-new category?** Truly new (not a collision) ·
    informative (not present-but-contentless) · survives field-contact (not merely coherent). Most
    new ideas should *accrete* into an existing category or *diffuse* — only a genuinely-new grounded
    nucleus earns its own. Coherence makes gas feel like a star; the test is whether it condenses
    against something real.

12. **The confident-wrong failure is a map-orientation problem, not a character flaw.** A reading
    that *feels examined* whose map is upside-down is more dangerous than an empty instrument — it
    points at the rocks with the confidence of consideration. The only tell is external: turn the map
    over against something that isn't you.

13. **Stack-rank candidate frames/directions on THREE axes, not two: survivability × plausibility ×
    regime-match.** Expert-pruning (the master sees a handful where the novice sees infinite) is
    *regime-bound* — the handful is valid only in the regime the mastery was learned in. A confident,
    well-ranked handful on the wrong seas capsizes regardless of the ranking. So before trusting the
    rank, ask: *are these the seas these survivability-priors were learned on?* Regime home is ADR-033
    (validityBounds {n, conditions, stationarity}); this adds regime-match as a ranking axis, not just
    a validity bound. YELLOW — only the outcome resolves regime-match, same as everything else.
