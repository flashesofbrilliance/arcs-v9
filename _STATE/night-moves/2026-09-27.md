- Read friction × write friction gives four quadrants; the pathology is cheap-write / expensive-read (RAM pretending to be ROM). Every cheap-write sink needs a curation path or it migrates there. — `seed-service-librarian-retrieval-policy`
- Etching RAM → ROM moves toward git, not away from it; the molted shell is the debit entry and cannot be deleted without unbalancing the books. — CLAUDE.md versioning policy, tao-257 lineage
- Cron asks "is it time?"; kairos asks "is it ripe?" Edge triggers can be missed, level triggers cannot; schedule the checks, capture at the moment. — `tao-257`
