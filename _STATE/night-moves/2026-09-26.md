## 🧠 What we learned
- Warmth does not corrupt what you ground; it changes whether you ground at all. Coldness's value is structural: a reader with no memory cannot narrate from memory, so the citing discipline is not optional. Witnessed GREEN. [[witness-2026-09-25]]
- Commit-as-you-go leaks: the most contemporaneous artifacts of an incident (the RCA, the witness stubs) were never tracked, so a cold reader of the committed record cannot see them. Pre-commit is the substrate that makes reconstruction lossless.
- A designed, technically-correct resume can read as AI slop to an allergic hiring manager and get a recruiter to refuse to send it. Slop-free is now a gate, not a hope. [[slop-free-rubric]]
- Specificity floors are mode-relative: forcing a reflective essay to hit a marketing page's specificity is Goodhart. The FE method-essays pass every hard gate and read human (burstiness 9 to 10.5) at 10 to 15% specificity, which is the genre, not slop.
