## 🧠 What we learned
- A red check on an old PR is evidence about the PR's base, not its diff. When main is green, rebase before diagnosing. _source: tonight's sweep vs main CI_
- Fix the generator, not its output: the font sweep had to be redone through the generators (`3d195fe2`) because hand-edited outputs get clobbered on the next render. Same lesson as [[regen-from-generator-can-clobber-postpublish-handfix]], now gated in CI by `e9085fd7`.
- A sweep that fixes "all" pages misses some until a gate enforces it: the lede fix took two passes (21, then 9 more), and only the second shipped with `page-gate` failing a short lede in CI. _source: `6f6544ea`, `2cecca39`; [[run-the-repos-own-gates-before-shipping]]_
- LinkedIn article promos earn reach but not replies when the hook is an abstract metaphor (2,126 impressions, 6 engagements; 722 and 0). Lead with a named consensus, a concrete scene, a public falsifier and an answerable question. _source: [[linkedin-promo-post-engagement-playbook]]_
