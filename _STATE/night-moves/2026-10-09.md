## 🧠 What we learned
- A control must sit above every section it governs; nothing it filters appears before it. — [[feedback-controls-above-controlled-sections]]
- Visual changes on a page with Reading options need the full page gate plus every text size at three widths before push; lint and spot screenshots missed overlaps a human caught by eye. — [[feedback-sweep-reading-options-and-run-page-gate-before-push]]
- Never rewrite an email draft the owner may be editing; a whole-body update erases their formatting. Ask, or write a new draft. — [[feedback-never-overwrite-a-draft-zach-may-be-editing]]
- Fixed breakpoints fail on user-scalable text; measure fit (overflow) and collapse, for nav and button grids alike. — myally commits `1ab4a9e5`, `4db9283f`
- Run a page's gate on the file that carries its own smoke test; CI does, and a hosted copy without it hides stale assertions. — `b15f5c87`
