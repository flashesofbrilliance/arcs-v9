## 🧠 What we learned
- **Do not assert a tool's negative as a fact about the world.** A failed measurement (MCP 404 on the analytics endpoint) is not evidence the thing is off; verify against the source before telling the user "it's not enabled." (recalibration booked live this session.)
- **Fact-check a JS-rendered SPA by grepping the raw file, not tag-stripping it.** VICO's site strips to ~23 chars of visible text; the real facts live in the baked JSON blob. Grep the raw HTML for the fact-strings directly. [[fact-check-js-spa-grep-raw-not-tagstrip]]
- **Gmail rewraps outbound link hrefs through google.com/url at send time.** A clean `<a href>` still ships wrapped. To get an unmangled URL in front of someone, paste the domain as PLAIN TEXT (no `<a>` tag) — nothing for Gmail to wrap, and it survives forwarding better. [[email-plain-text-url-defeats-gmail-href-wrap]]
