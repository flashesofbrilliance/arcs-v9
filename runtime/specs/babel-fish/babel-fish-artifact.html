        How it was checked

        1. ReadClaude lists the points that must survive.
        2. RewriteA version per listener. No new numbers or claims allowed.
        3. Blind readerA second call that never sees the points restates the version. Each point is marked kept, weakened or dropped, with a quote the page checks is in the text.
        4. Fear readerA third call reads as the listener's fear and quotes phrases that would make them stop. Quotes not found in the text are thrown out.
        Agreed before any run (2026-11-16): the checker must catch at least 90% of dropped or negated points and 80% of added or softened ones, over 10 test concepts (the Loki test).
        How would you hear it?a 5-minute self-check, then you are a listener too

        The Book of ARCS reads you through four channels, then takes their consensus as your Atomic Signature: a primary, a shadow, and the archetype you bridge with. You do the marking; the page only counts. No model decides who you are. Two channels are enough for a signature; four are better. The result becomes You, a listener Babel Fish can translate to.

        1. Heptagram: where your pull is

        The Book asks for a primary and a shadow on the star; the secondary is the archetype you lean on to cope. Marking it is optional: it counts as one vote for your Bridge, alongside the other channels.

        2. Somatic scan: what your body does

        Imagine each scene for a few breaths. Does your body open (lean in, breath deepens) or contract (pull back, breath shallows)?

        3. Mirror test: who you feel most alive with

        Think of 3 people. For each, tick the energies they carry, and say whether being with them energizes you or is a longing. Names stay on this device.

        4. Receipt inventory: why you left

        Your last 5 major decisions (jobs, cities, relationships, projects). For each, what made you leave? This channel reads a primary only; the Book gives it no shadow rule.

        For builders: stress-test this toolthe Loki test, about 90 Claude messages

        The transceiver is the whole lossless claim, so this attacks it. For each of 10 built-in concepts, a clean rendering runs once as a control, then four planted corruptions run through the same blind receive and compare: a claim dropped (sentence deleted mechanically), a fabricated number added (appended mechanically), a claim negated and a claim softened (edited by Claude, checked to have changed).
