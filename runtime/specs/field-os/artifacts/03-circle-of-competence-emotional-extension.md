# Circle of Competence — Emotional Dimension Extension

> Munger’s circle is the most humane boundary technology ever formalized. This session adds the dimension he left out.

## Munger’s Original Formulation

- The circle names what you know with genuine depth
- The perimeter matters more than the size
- “Too tough” is a valid and high-status output
- Staying inside = compounding. Straying outside = ruin

## The Missing Dimension

Munger’s circle protected **outward** (from incompetence) but not **inward** — from the weight of your own depth, the isolation of genuine mastery, the Prosōpon gap that forms when the inner universe has no translation protocol.

**The extension**: The circle guards in both directions.
- **Outward**: guards against overreach into incompetence
- **Inward**: guards against collapse under the weight of depth — the mikropsychia trap, the installed inversion

## The Contribution

> *The circle is not just an investment tool. It is a bidirectional humane boundary — naming what you have earned and what you haven’t, protecting both from overreach and from self-erasure.*
