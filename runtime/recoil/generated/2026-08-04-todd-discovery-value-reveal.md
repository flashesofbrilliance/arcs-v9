## Client-facing draft
_Keep tight and skimmable. Per the vision read, Todd absorbs this by ear, not by reading a ledger._

**Subject idea:** What the chatbot opened up

Hi Todd -

You brought me in for a simple thing: a chatbot so you stop losing website leads. To build it so it
actually fits how you sell, I had to follow a lead all the way through your business. The real value
turned out to be less in the chatbot itself and more in what it showed us it could unlock.

Four levers, all sitting inside what you already own:

- **Cost you can take out.** You are already paying for tools that are half-on. Your CRM, ZoomInfo,
  Unbounce, your analytics. Turn them on and get them talking, and things like the lead-gen service
  you are cutting become costs you can retire, not renew.
- **Time you can compress.** Almost none of your leads carry an email today, and the four-hour window
  you manage to is not on any report. Fix both and a lead reaches the right rep, complete, faster,
  and you can finally show the number.
- **Systems you can connect.** Right now about $26.5M of closed-won revenue has no source on it, so
  you cannot see which channels actually make you money. Tagging every lead closes that blind spot.
- **Revenue you can generate.** Your CRM already holds 231 deals tagged with the competitor grade the
  customer was replacing. That is a ready-made map of who you win against and where, and it drives
  both the bot and where you point your marketing.

Upstream, these change how demand gets captured and decisions get made. Downstream, they change how
fast and how well your team can act on it. None of it asks your reps to change how they work. No new
tool, no new login. A better lead just shows up where they already look. The whole point is to make
the technology invisible, so it feels like a friend that makes their day easier, not something
looking over their shoulder.

I am not sending a bill for any of this. I am showing it to you because it is yours, and because
doing these small, provable things well is how we build the shared language and the track record to
take on the bigger swings later, the facelift, the assistant for the reps, whatever comes when the
business is ready for it.

If any of it is worth walking through, I have it scoped and the risks worked out. No pressure. The
chatbot stands on its own either way.

With gratitude,
Zach
