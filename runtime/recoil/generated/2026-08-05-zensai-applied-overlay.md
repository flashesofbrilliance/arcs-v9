# ARCS × Zensai — Applied Overlay Map
Internal strategy. Built 2026-08-05 from 3 recon passes (market / competitive / product+asset), all web-sourced. Read the honesty flags at the bottom before using any number with Robin.

## The one-line thesis
Zensai's entire competitive set coaches *around* work (surveys, OKRs, reviews, course completions) and **never observes an actual conversation**. ARCS is the **resonance read** of the conversation itself — a net-new evidence source none of them can produce — delivered as a **thin read layer, not another recorder**. That is the white space, and it sits exactly inside Zensai's own "human success, in the flow of work, inside Microsoft" thesis.

## What Zensai is (grounding)
- **Human Success Platform**: Learn365 (LMS) + Engage365 (engagement/sentiment) + Perform365 (continuous performance / 1:1s), now also packaged as **Grow / Scale / Transform** tiers. Native in **Microsoft 365 / Teams / Viva**; May 2026 launched a Human Success Agent on Microsoft Agent 365.
- **AI story**: "agentic AI coaches employees and managers"; AI Skills Framework, sentiment analysis, AI coaching for learners, **manager coaching prompts via Copilot in Teams**, early-warning signals. Coaching is "included across the platform," not metered.
- **Robin's public thesis**: "AI is not the outcome. It's just another input." "Agentic AI shifts HR from reactive to proactive."
- **Category they're creating**: "human success." The coaching/conversation-intelligence market they're moving into is large and fast (sales-coaching ~$61B; conversation-intelligence ~$28B @ ~13% CAGR; HR-side coaching fastest-growing ~14.6% CAGR).

## Positioning & differentiation (honest, vs the field)
- **Read layer, not recorder.** Runs on recordings they already have (Teams); doesn't require ripping in Gong/Chorus or becoming the system of record.
- **Resonance, not compliance.** Where a rep was in sync vs lost the room — not MEDDIC-adherence scorecards. A different question than any incumbent answers.
- **Pre + post on the *real* call.** Bridges roleplay tools (pre, simulated) and CI tools (post, retrospective) that nobody stitches on a real relationship.
- **Non-invasive / air-gapped / on your own stack.** No always-on bot, no data migration. Credible vs the incumbents' all-or-nothing capture.
- **A layer that extends Zensai/Viva, not a suite that replaces it.** Lowers the displacement bar; fits the Microsoft "flow of work" thesis.
- **Honest about certainty** (earned fidelity) vs uniform confident scorecards.
- **Fit-or-fight on Microsoft**: an M365/Teams-native read rides their wedge; one outside it fights their whole thesis and stalls with the IT gatekeeper.
- **Sell to the outcome (CHRO / CHSO), instrument for the manager** — the same split that made revenue-enablement the buyer for Gong/Salesloft.
