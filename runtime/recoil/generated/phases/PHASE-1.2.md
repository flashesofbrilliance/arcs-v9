## Scope / what "done" looks like
Lead delivery is rock-solid; every lead lands complete with a business email; the prospect gets an
instant acknowledgment; your team is notified; and you can see time-to-first-touch (the four-hour
number) from day one, with a baseline to prove what the chatbot adds.

## Best / Base / Worst
| | Effort | Time | What drives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best** | ~12 hrs | few days | Your CRM already stamps first-touch; baseline pulls clean. |
| **Base** | ~20 hrs | ~1-1.5 wks | Normal setup + one data session + enrichment wiring. |
| **Worst** | ~30 hrs | ~2 wks | CRM doesn't capture first-touch (we switch to an automatic proxy), or the baseline data is messy. |

## Dependencies & what could delay it
- **Blocking:** the sandbox (shared with 1.1); a 30-min CRM Data Reality session with SM + reports.
- **Coordination overhead:** ZoomInfo + Mailgun account access; a sending subdomain set up.
- **Calendar risk:** the data session is the long pole — a holiday or a work trip that pushes it pushes
  the whole phase. Booking it early is the single best de-risk.
- **Out-of-scope:** rebuilding CRM reports or cleaning historical data beyond the baseline pull is a
  separate, quoted item.

## Payment
- **Base price: $4,500** (fixed for the phase). **Paid before Monday kickoff.**
- **Range:** best case is the same price, sooner. Worst case stays $4,500 — the fixed price is the cap;
  a bubble only happens via a named change order (below), never silently.
- Tool costs (ZoomInfo, email) are billed to PRL directly, outside this number.

## Contingency
- **Min-cost path:** if the historical data can't support the full retro-analysis, we ship the live
  four-hour number going forward (still the headline result) and skip the historical chart — no added
  spend, clearly flagged.
- **Where it could bubble:** a request to reconstruct missing lead-source history across the $26.5M, or
  to build custom dashboards, would be a change order (scoped + priced first).
- **Capital note:** ZoomInfo per-lookup cost is capped up front so enrichment can't run away.
