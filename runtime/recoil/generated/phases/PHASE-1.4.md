# Phase 1.4 — Your paid landing pages (SEM) + a smarter opening
_Todd-safe · LATER · 2026-08-04._

## Where this sits
**LATER.** This is where the chatbot starts working your paid-search dollars, not just your website.

## Scope / what "done" looks like
Each ad campaign page gets its own chatbot opening, tuned to why that visitor came, wired to your CRM.
The bot also starts showing each visitor what fits their job (a processor sees commercial and processing
info; a designer/OEM sees performance and spec info), without burying either in the other's concerns.

## Best / Base / Worst
| | Effort | Time | What drives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best** | ~16 hrs | ~1 wk | A few clear campaign pages; Google data points cleanly to what to fix. |
| **Base** | ~26 hrs | ~1.5-2 wks | Several pages + the persona-aware openings + wiring to the existing CRM zaps. |
| **Worst** | ~40 hrs | ~2.5-3 wks | Many pages, or the landing pages need real rebuilds, not just an opening. |

## Dependencies & what could delay it
- **Blocking:** Unbounce access (edit the landers) + Google Search Console / Google Ads access (to point
  it correctly). The persona-aware part depends on 1.5's AI handoff being proven first.
- **Coordination overhead:** whoever manages your ad campaigns and the agency for anything on the live site.
- **Calendar risk:** two access grants + an agency touch means more moving parts; holidays/trips compound.
- **Out-of-scope:** running or managing the ad campaigns themselves (media buying) is a separate,
  ongoing service, not this build.

## Payment
- **Base price: $6,000** (fixed for the phase). **Paid before kickoff.**
- **Range:** best case comes in faster at the same price. Worst case stays $6,000 unless a named change
  order applies (e.g. full page rebuilds vs openings).
- This is the phase most likely to open a **larger ongoing conversation** (a real SEM + campaign-
  management service), which would be its own separate agreement — flagged early so it is never a surprise.

## Contingency
- **Min-cost path:** start with the two or three worst-performing pages only; prove lift, then expand.
- **Where it could bubble:** if the pages need design rebuilds, or you want ongoing campaign management,
  those are separate scopes with their own numbers.
- **Capital note:** ad spend itself is yours and separate; this phase is the plumbing and the openings.
