## Picking up from Friday

In the weekend recap I flagged the one call that is genuinely yours: today the assistant quotes exact property
values and UL ratings straight from the catalog, and if any value is stale that becomes a customer-facing error
with your name on it. Option B (recommended) is that it recognizes what someone is asking, helps at the general
level, and routes the specifics to your specialist plus the official data sheet, capturing the lead either way.

This is just Option B spelled out so you can see exactly what it would and would not answer. Three settings per
kind of question:

- **Always** — the assistant answers directly from what it knows.
- **Sometimes** — it helps at the general level, then says a PRL specialist will confirm the specifics, and
  captures the lead so someone follows up. It never states an exact number.
- **Never** — it does not attempt it at all; it routes straight to a person and captures the lead.

The platform works at the level of these categories, not individual data points, so setting it here keeps
every visitor's experience consistent. Whatever we pick, it is a step up from today.
