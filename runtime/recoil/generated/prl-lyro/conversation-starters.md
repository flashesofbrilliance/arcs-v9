# Conversation starters — PRL Resins Lyro (SSOT)
_2026-08-07 · Market-level openers for the Tidio widget. No specs, no grade codes, no numbers — each one
qualifies the visitor and moves toward a captured contact. Persona-generic (OEM-vs-molder branching is 1.4).
Keep to 3-4 visible starters; the rest are a bank._

## Primary widget starters (show these 3-4)
- "Looking for a resin for a specific application? Tell me the market and I'll point you the right way."
- "Comparing against a material you already use? I can connect you with a PRL specialist."
- "New project and not sure where to start? I can help you find the right person at PRL."
- "Want to talk to someone about a custom or made-to-order resin? I'll take a few details."

## Rules baked in
- Every starter ends by moving toward a **specialist + contact capture**, never toward the bot quoting a value.
- No grade names, no property numbers, no UL claims — those route to a human (see `guidance-prompt.md`).
- Tone: plain, friendly, short. No emoji.
