## About Polymer Resources (PRL)
Polymer Resources Ltd (PRL) is a privately owned US manufacturer of specialty engineering resin compounds. PRL
custom-compounds engineering resins, blends, and alloys, and offers color-matching and related material
services. Manufacturing is in Farmington, Connecticut and Rochester, New York. PRL serves the electrical,
electronics, medical, consumer, industrial, building, and transportation markets, and has been in business for
about half a century. For anything specific to your project, a PRL specialist is the right next step.

## What PRL makes (material families)
PRL produces engineering resins and custom blends across common families, including polycarbonate (PC),
modified polyphenylene ether (PPE), ABS, PBT, ASA, nylon 6 and nylon 6/6, and polyetherimide (PEI), plus blends
and alloys such as PC/ABS and similar combinations. PRL also custom-compounds with additives, modifiers, and
reinforcing agents to meet an application's needs. To find the right material and the exact grade for your
part, a specialist will help — the specific grade, its properties, and any listings are confirmed by a person,
not quoted here.

## Custom compounding, color, and services
Custom formulation and precise color-matching are core PRL capabilities, along with product-selection support,
part and tool design support, process-optimization help, and guidance on regulatory requirements. PRL also
offers sustainable and recycled-content options. Order sizes are flexible, from small quantities up to
truckloads. For a custom formulation, a color match, or a selection question, a specialist can take your
details and follow up.

## Markets and applications
### Electrical & electronics
PRL makes resins developed for demanding electrical and electronics requirements, including needs like flame
retardance and tracking or arc resistance. Typical applications include wiring devices, circuit protection,
connectors and terminal blocks, cable ties, lighting controls and sensors, and electronics enclosures and
controls. PRL has grades listed for electrical use; which grade and which listing fit your part is confirmed by
a specialist.

### Medical devices and equipment
PRL supplies resins used in non-invasive medical devices and equipment, where consistency and documentation
matter. For device-specific requirements and any regulatory or grade questions, a specialist will follow up.

### Transportation
PRL serves automotive and transportation applications with engineering resins suited to under-hood, interior,
and exterior parts. The right grade for a given part is a specialist conversation.

### Building & construction
PRL makes resins for building-and-construction applications. A specialist can help match a material to your
application's requirements.

### Consumer & industrial goods
PRL supplies resins for a wide range of consumer and industrial products. Tell us the application and a
specialist can point you to the right material.

### Sustainability
Sustainable and recycled-content materials are a growing part of PRL's portfolio, including closed-loop
recycling and circular grades. A specialist can walk you through the sustainable options that fit your part.

### Structural foam
PRL offers materials for structural-foam applications. A specialist can help with the right selection.
