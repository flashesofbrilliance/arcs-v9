What a week looks like

Monday. 
A $400k deal with a REIT’s VP of Technology closes next month. You’re building a custom AI workspace with their actual portfolio data: their buildings, their leases, their numbers on screen for tomorrow’s demo.

Tuesday.
 Live demo. Their CTO asks how your AI evaluation framework compares to generic benchmarks. You walk through domain-specific evals, pull up accuracy metrics in real time, and show 98% lease abstraction accuracy on their documents. The room goes quiet.

Wednesday.
 Discovery call with a prospect who “already has ChatGPT Enterprise.” You show them what 65% accuracy looks like on a real lease abstract versus 98%, and why the gap matters when you’re managing a $2 billion portfolio. They ask for a follow-up with their data team.

Thursday. 
You draft the security response to their RFP. You map a competitor’s technical gaps on a battle card for the AE team. You know where the gaps are because you’ve been in the room with the buyer.

Friday.
 Pipeline review with the sales team. Three deals need technical depth next week; you’re prioritizing by deal size and close probability. One AE needs coaching on how to position the data integration story to a CFO.

This is pre-sales engineering at Fifth Dimension. You are the technical voice in every deal that matters. You build trust with CTOs. You drive unit economics, ensuring what’s good for our partners and what’s good for us align beautifully. Revenue depends on you.
