# Part B. Standard terms (these apply to both of us)

You asked for a standard agreement your counsel can review. These are the usual protective terms, and they work
both ways, including a cap on what either of us could owe the other.

## 9. Limits on what either of us can claim

- **Knock-on losses.** Neither of us can claim from the other for knock-on losses, such as lost revenue, a lost grant
  or lost data (in legal terms, indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages), even if told they
  were possible.
- **Cap.** Each side's total liability for everything arising out of or related to this agreement is limited to the
  greater of $1,500 or the fees paid and payable under it, including any 2-hour blocks. This limit applies to all
  claims, whether in contract, in tort (including negligence and negligent misrepresentation), under statute or
  otherwise.
- **Where these limits do not apply:** [a breach of the NDA, see Part C choice 3]; fraud, or reckless or intentional
  misconduct; amounts either of us owes the other under sections 4, 15 and 16, including refunds; and anything the
  law does not allow to be limited.
- **Time to raise a claim.** Any claim must be brought within one year after the readout, or after this agreement ends
  if there is no readout.

## 10. What this work is, and what it is not

- The deliverables are opinions and estimates, based on the code, spec and information you give me, as they stand on
  the days I review them. Ranges are not quotes, and nothing in them is a guarantee of cost, timing or outcome.
- The data governance plan describes common controls and how teams build them. It does not decide which laws apply
  to My Ally LD or whether it meets them; that is for your counsel, and the plan lists those questions for them.
  Where a law may matter, for example student privacy, children's privacy, health or disability information, or
  state privacy law, the plan names the question. I do not give legal, tax, accounting or security certification
  advice.
- I report what I find in the code on the days I review it, including whether each family's data is kept apart. This
  is a reading of the code, not a full security test of the running app, so it can miss problems a full test would
  catch.
- I do the work with professional care. If something I deliver falls short of that care, tell me within 30 days of
  the readout. I redo that part at no charge, or if I cannot, I refund what you paid for it. That is your only remedy
  for the quality of the work. Apart from that promise, the deliverables are provided as is, and to the extent the
  law allows I give no other promises, stated or implied.
- Decisions about what to build, fund, hire and launch, and how to comply with the law, stay with you.

## 11. Your materials and data

- You confirm you have the right to share with me the code, spec, bills and other materials you send.
- You confirm the workspace and its database hold no real family or child records, or you tell me where they are
  before the invite. I read code, settings and table structure, not rows of user records, and I do not copy or export
  any. If any real family data reaches me anyway, I tell you, do not use it, delete it and confirm in writing. If I
  see or suspect any exposure of family or child data, I tell you within 72 hours.
- In this step I do not receive student or family records for My Ally LD or any school. If a later step needs that,
  we sign a short data addendum first.
- I make no changes to your code, data or settings, and I do not use the AI chat inside your workspace unless you
  ask. When Step 1 ends I leave the workspace myself and confirm it by email, unless you choose more detail or Step 2
  on the readout call. You can remove my access at any time.
- I keep your materials only in password-protected accounts I control and in the tools named in section 13, share
  them with no one, and list where they are when I confirm return or deletion, as NDA section [5.1, to confirm
  against the signed NDA] requires.

## 12. Who owns what

- Once the fee is paid, I assign to you all rights in the deliverables written for you.
- You, and any company you form for, or that takes over, My Ally LD, may use anything of mine that appears in the
  deliverables, including anything I shared earlier at no charge, for the My Ally LD platform, free and for good, and
  may change it and share it with a developer working on it for you. You may not sell it on its own.
- I keep my own tools, methods, templates and know-how (NDA sections 9.2 and 9.3) and may use them with other
  clients, so long as they carry none of your confidential information.
- Nothing here gives me any right in your code, spec, data or brand.
- The deliverables are written for My Ally LD, and my duties are to you only. You are welcome to share them whole,
  with their first page, with your team, investors, schools, counsel and developers for their information. Each
  deliverable carries this line on its first page: "Planning estimates for My Ally LD by Zach Harris, based on the
  assumptions listed. Not a quote, a guarantee, a compliance certification or investment advice."

## 13. AI tools and recordings

I use [named tools, see Part C choice 1], each under business terms that do not train on your material, as NDA
section 4.1 requires. I add a tool only after telling you by email. I record or transcribe our calls only with your
OK at the start of each call, and recordings, transcripts and AI tool histories count as your confidential material
under the NDA. I check everything before it reaches you, and I am responsible for what I deliver.

## 14. Claims from others

Each of us covers the other for claims by others caused by our own negligence or breach of this agreement or the
NDA. I also cover you if anything I deliver copies someone else's work. You cover me for claims about the My Ally LD
app, its users and data, and about how you or anyone you share the deliverables with uses them, except where I
caused the problem. Both covers sit inside the limits in section 9.

## 15. How we work together, and ending early

- I am an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of yours. I am responsible for my own taxes and
  tools.
- Either of us may end this agreement at any time by email. I then deliver what is done, marked as an unfinished
  draft not for decisions, and the fee becomes $125 for each hour worked, never more than $1,500. I refund anything
  paid above that within 10 days, and section 8's return or destroy applies. If you end it before the sprint starts,
  I refund the full fee.

## 17. The rest

- **Whole agreement.** This agreement and the NDA are the whole deal for Step 1, and replace earlier drafts of it.
  The October 29 plan for Steps 2 and 3, including the $1,500 credit toward the build, still stands as a plan; each
  step is confirmed in its own written agreement. If this agreement and the NDA conflict, the NDA governs
  confidentiality and this agreement governs everything else, except that section 9 sets the limits on liability and
  section 12 decides who owns my tools and know-how.
- **Nothing outside it.** Neither of us relies on any statement not written in this agreement or the NDA, including
  the client page and earlier emails.
- **Changes** count only in writing that both of us accept; an email from each side is enough.
- **Notices** go by email to the addresses each of us uses for this work.
- **Law and courts.** Connecticut law applies, and the state or federal courts in Connecticut decide disputes, as in
  the NDA. Before going to court, each of us first talks it through in good faith for 30 days, except to stop a
  breach of the NDA.
- **Delays outside anyone's control** (illness, outages, natural events) move the dates rather than breach the
  agreement.
- **If a part is invalid,** the rest still stands. Not enforcing a term once is not giving it up.
- **Assignment.** Neither of us may transfer this agreement without the other's written OK, except to a company that
  takes over My Ally LD (section 8) or that takes over my business.
- **Signing.** Electronic signatures and separate signed copies count as one original.
- **Survival.** Sections 8 to 14 and 17, and section 5 for blocks already approved, continue after the step ends.
