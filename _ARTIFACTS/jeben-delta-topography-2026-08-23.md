## 2. What the new instruments saw that the original pass could not

**misattributed**. Verbatim record: "disturbance in the force" is Zach's own Feb 13
annotation; Jeben's actual end-user line is the Apr 21 Spock passage ("I'm smelling arcs

two months and two speakers. The 2026 instrument caught it before it entered canon or an
outbound note; the 2026-02 toolchain had no mechanism that could have. Filed with full
fact-check trail in `_CANON/great-moments.jsonl`.

requesting the Drift Sentinel role — all unfiled at the time, all now first-class canon

### 2d. Expected-vs-actual on the Jul 15 ex-ante scenarios (recoil close-loop)
The Jul 15 SSOT priced three scenarios: A (both threads advance, 40%), B (PE stalls,
Louisville stays paused, 40%), C (Louisville closes for real, 20%). Ground truth as of
Aug 23 (per Zach): **Jeben never replied to either heavy send. Scenario B materialized,
via exactly the mechanism the SSOT flagged** — "bundled asks dilute; he closes loops on
whatever he raises himself" (riskFlags.BUNDLED_ASKS_DILUTE) and the Jul 10 "champion has
stepped back" read. The instrument's causal model was right ex ante; the behavior that
contradicted it (two heavy sends) happened anyway. Lesson class: prediction without a
wired gate does not stop the operator — matches lesson-banked-when-it-can-STOP-you
(prose-tier lessons don't block; only gates do). The cold-loop refresher draft
(`_ARTIFACTS/jeben-refresher-note-DRAFT.md`) is the corrective, built to the SSOT's own
prescription: one narrow touch, his thread, zero asks.

### 2e. Stale-artifact detection (live-culture doctrine, ADR-066)

still frames the Louisville factory as underused capacity; Jeben's own update (Wisconsin

doctrine this is a feed-the-artifact moment — but it is DOWNSTREAM of the refresher: do
not touch or re-link the Thread Book unless Jeben reopens the channel. Flagged, not fixed,
by design.
