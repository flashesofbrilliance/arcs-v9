Hi Peloton team,

I build operator software, then become the operator it was built for. That is the short version of why this role fits.

Here is the longer one. The commercial software problem at Peloton is not a features problem. It is a trust problem. A gym owner or hotel fitness director has Peloton equipment on their floor, members or guests expecting it to work, and a support ticket as their only recourse when it does not. Operator NPS lives or dies in the gap between what the equipment does and what the operator can see, control, and fix without calling anyone. The 0-to-90-day issue rate you named as a core metric tells me Peloton already knows where that trust breaks: in the first weeks, before the operator has built any confidence in the system. I have spent the better part of a decade closing exactly that kind of gap, most directly at Twill Health, where I built evaluation frameworks and quality loops for three AI-enabled B2B product lines, defined operator-facing success metrics and API instrumentation, and ran market-viability testing that killed one product line before it consumed further engineering time. Knowing when to stop is the same skill as knowing what to build.

The scrappy-builder requirement is the one I want to address directly. I founded and built ARCS from scratch, roadmap through production, prototyping and pressure-testing ideas with AI tools before committing engineering time. I hold my own in technical tradeoff conversations with engineering on interface design and capability scoping. I do not hand a spec to a team and wait. I am in the weeds with them.

The hardware-plus-software dimension is one I find genuinely interesting. At the WTA I built real-time fan-behavior scoring at under 120ms, serving personalized offers inside a live, high-stakes usage window, which is the same shape of problem: software that has to be right when the physical experience is already in motion. A Peloton bike mid-class is not a moment for latency or ambiguity in the operator layer.

I do not just write the deck. I am in the discovery sessions, the instrumentation design, and the tradeoff calls. Receipts, not adjectives: the bundle at zachharris.xyz has the live commit trail and the fuller picture of how I work.
