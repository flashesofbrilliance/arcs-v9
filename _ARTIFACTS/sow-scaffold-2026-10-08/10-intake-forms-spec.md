# Intake: what I would like to see, and how to share it

Four short sections, one each for the technical, money, day-to-day and business sides. You can answer in any order, skip anything you do not know, and say "not sure" as often as you need. Nothing here is a test. Each answer helps me look at the same picture you do.

**Before you answer.** Please share links, file names and short descriptions only. Do not paste or upload any family's information, any child's information, or any passwords or keys. Anything sensitive travels another way, agreed after the NDA is signed. Intake opens once the NDA is in place.

## 1. The build (for whoever builds)

| Question | How to answer | Why I ask |
|---|---|---|
| Where does the app live today? | Link to the project in your build tool | So I look at the real thing |
| Is the code saved in a code repository? Which one? | Yes, no or not sure, plus the name | Tells me how changes are tracked |
| Which services does the app use (build tool, database, AI provider, search, email, analytics)? | A list, with the plan name if you know it | I map where information travels |
| Which AI provider or providers have you tried or considered? Is any agreement already signed? | Short text | The provider choice is a decision to make with the facts |
| Where is the build plan? Who wrote it, who reviewed it, and when was it last changed? | Link or file name, plus names and a date | So I can read it in context |
| How do people log in today, and who can see what? | A few sentences | A first look at who can see what |
| Is there a separate copy of the app for practising, apart from the real one? | Yes, no or not sure | Practice should never touch real use |
| Have real documents ever been used while testing? | Yes, no or not sure (no details needed) | Tells me how careful to be |
| Do you know if backups exist? | Yes, no or not sure | A backup nobody has tested is a hope |

## 2. The money (for whoever keeps the numbers)

| Question | How to answer | Why I ask |
|---|---|---|
| Business plan and financial projections | Link or file name, date, who wrote it | The plan and the build should say the same thing |
| The usage-cost model for light, typical and heavy families | Link or file name, date, and where each assumption came from | I want to check it against how Alice will really work |
| Funding plan and the timing you expect | Short text | Dates shape the order of the work |
| How much cash runway do you have? | Your own figure, or "prefer to discuss" | A failed test should never threaten the whole plan |
| Which services do you pay for each month, who pays, and on which card or account? | A list | Hidden recurring costs are common |
| Which entity pays for this work and who signs? | Short text | So the paperwork matches |
| Roughly what range feels comfortable for this work? | Optional | Helps me offer options that fit |

## 3. Day to day (for whoever runs things)

| Question | How to answer | Why I ask |
|---|---|---|
| Who makes the final call on each area (product, money, legal, build)? | A name per area | A decision with no owner is not a decision |
| How many hours a week can each of you give this? | A number per person | The plan has to fit real time |
| Who are your lawyers, and where is the list of questions you have asked them? | Names and a link | Their answers come first for some things |
| Policies you already have (terms, privacy notice, consent wording) | Links | I do not rewrite what you have |
| Key dates and limits (launch hopes, funding dates, holidays) | A list | So the roadmap respects them |
| Who would be contacted if something went wrong with families' information? | A name and a way to reach them | Better to name them before it matters |
| Tools you already use to work together | A list | I work where you work |

## 4. The business (for whoever holds the vision)

| Question | How to answer | Why I ask |
|---|---|---|
| In two sentences, what is My Ally LD and who is it for first? | Your own words | Everyone describing it the same way is the goal |
| Rank the five parts (vault, directory of providers, strengths questionnaire, opportunities, checklist and dashboard) by how much they matter to the first families | A ranked list | The roadmap follows what matters most |
| What must be true by the start of next year? | Short text | Sets the checkpoint |
| What have early testers told you, even informally? | Short text | Real feedback beats guesses |
| Partners or grants in play, and what they require | A list | Their rules may shape the build |
| What would make you stop or change course? | Short text | Decided in advance is cheaper |
| What have you already decided that you do not want reopened? | A list | I respect settled decisions |
