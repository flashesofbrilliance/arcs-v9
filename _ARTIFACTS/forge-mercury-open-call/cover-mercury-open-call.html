Mercury Founding Team,

You already hold the money, and you just shipped the agent that acts on it. Command sends payments, sets auto-transfer floor rules, and rebalances Treasury today. The layer, plainly. An Opportunity-Cost Engine on Command: a human-approved analyst that prices the cost of every idle dollar live ("$412K in checking is costing $1,540/mo" versus your Treasury rate), computes runway under scenarios as a first-class Command output, and opens the untouched spend-timing surface, an AP calendar that weighs early-pay discounts against runway. Command executes payments today. It does not yet optimize when and whether to pay. The incentive is aligned rather than bolted on: the engine monetizes by moving idle deposits into Mercury Treasury, accretive to your core revenue in the same motion that makes the product better.

I build AI products with AI, on your stack. I built ARCS from scratch, an AI-native decision platform on Claude with a custom MCP server and agentic orchestration, in production. Regulated-capital fluency is the moat on this one. At Grayscale I led go-to-market for $7B in SEC/FINRA-regulated product launches across a 10-product portfolio. The moment an agent touches yield, every claim has to survive compliance. Making true statements about money under a partner-bank or broker-dealer model is the scarce skill here, and it is one I have shipped before, not one I would be learning on your deposit base.
