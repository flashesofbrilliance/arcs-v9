Mercury Founding Team,

You already hold the money, and you just shipped the agent that acts on it. Command sends payments, sets auto-transfer floor rules, and rebalances Treasury today. The residue is not doing that work faster. It is making every idle and every spent dollar provably optimal. I want to build that layer, and I built this pitch by running it on its own creation.

The layer, plainly. An Opportunity-Cost Engine on Command: a human-approved analyst that prices the cost of every idle dollar live ("$412K in checking is costing $1,540/mo versus your Treasury rate"), computes runway under scenarios as a first-class Command output, and opens the untouched spend-timing surface, an AP calendar that weighs early-pay discounts against runway. Command executes payments today. It does not yet optimize when and whether to pay. The incentive is aligned rather than bolted on: the engine monetizes by moving idle deposits into Mercury Treasury, accretive to your core revenue in the same motion that makes the product better.

I build AI products with AI, on your stack. I built ARCS from scratch, an AI-native decision platform on Claude with a custom MCP server and agentic orchestration, in production. Your call asks for people who can prototype agent harnesses, evals, and RAG rather than only spec them. That is the work I already did, in the open, and you can open it in a browser.

Regulated-capital fluency is the moat on this one. At Grayscale I led go-to-market for $7B in SEC/FINRA-regulated product launches across a 10-product portfolio. The moment an agent touches yield, every claim has to survive compliance. Making true statements about money under a partner-bank or broker-dealer model is the scarce skill here, and it is one I have shipped before, not one I would be learning on your deposit base.

What I have not done, so you hear it from me first. I have not run a bank treasury desk or held a broker-dealer license. I am not pitching myself as a career treasurer, and this layer is deliberately an analyst that recommends, human-approved, inside Command's existing consent model and your read-only MCP posture, never an agent that moves money on its own. What I bring is the build and the regulated-product operating reps, working alongside the people who hold the licenses.

The deeper fit is architectural. This is a build-and-own job across product, compliance, and go-to-market at once, phased with go/no-go gates where no phase advances without its metric.
