About Crosby

Crosby is built on a simple conviction: a great legal system is the watermark of a great society — and the best legal work comes from combining human expertise with the right technology, not replacing one with the other.

Crosby is the first AI-native law firm, helping ambitious companies like Cursor, Ramp, and Cognition sign commercial contracts faster.

Legal work is both art and science, and we're mapping the frontier between the two — codifying what can be systematized, amplifying human judgment where it matters most. That's why the right way to bring AI into law isn't to sell software and walk away. It's to own the outcome together: higher-quality work, delivered faster. We build proprietary tools and human-in-the-loop workflows that change what's possible for corporate legal teams.

Crosby was founded by Ryan (Stanford Law, Cooley, startup GC) and John (Penn, first 15 engineers at Ramp). Help us transform one of society's most important industries.

About The Role

As an AI Developer Experience Engineer at Crosby, you'll play a critical role in building the internal systems that enable our team to develop, evaluate, and ship AI features quickly and reliably. You’ll focus on improving the developer experience around LLMs, data, and experimentation — creating tools and infrastructure that accelerate iteration while maintaining quality.

You’ll partner closely with engineers, data scientists, and researchers to streamline workflows across prompting, evaluation, fine-tuning, and deployment. This role is for someone who is obsessed with leverage — building systems that make other engineers dramatically more productive.

What You'll Do

Build AI developer tooling: Design and implement internal tools for prompt management, evaluation, experimentation, and model iteration. 

Improve iteration speed: Create systems that enable rapid testing, debugging, and comparison of model outputs across different approaches. 

Own evaluation infrastructure: Develop frameworks for benchmarking, regression testing, and monitoring AI system performance. 

Streamline data workflows: Build pipelines and tooling to support data collection, labeling, and feedback loops for model improvement. 

Support production systems: Partner with engineering teams to ensure AI features are reliable, observable, and easy to maintain in production. 

Enable the team: Identify bottlenecks in the development process and build systems that increase developer velocity and confidence. 

Who You Are

3+ years of software engineering experience, ideally working on developer tools, infrastructure, or platform teams

Strong proficiency in Python and experience building backend systems and internal tooling

Hands-on experience working with LLMs, APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic), and modern AI workflows

Experience building systems for experimentation, evaluation, or data pipelines

Strong systems thinking with the ability to design tools that scale across teams

Highly pragmatic with a focus on improving developer velocity and real-world outcomes

Strong communicator who can work closely with engineers, researchers, and product teams

Proactive, resourceful, and excited about building foundational systems in a fast-moving environment
