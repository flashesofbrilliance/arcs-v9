- Drive AI Project Deployment: own end-to-end PM for AI integrations — scoping, vendor evaluation,

- Build an AI Culture & Community: cultivate a network of AI champions across departments.
- Manage Vendor & Technical Partnerships: oversee AI tool providers; coordinate IT, data, legal,
  compliance for secure, responsible, compliant deployments.
- Track & Measure Business Impact: define success metrics, measure outcomes, communicate to senior

COMPETENCIES
