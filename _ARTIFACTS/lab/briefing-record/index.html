How this works, and why the receipt holds up
1

Record

Speak the brief or paste it verbatim. The rawness is the evidence — don't tidy it.

2

Receipt

It reads the work-shape — effort, outcome, risk — and drafts a receipt: scope, assumptions, clarifications. Opens in your review queue.

3

Seed

Attest to seal it. Share the open receipt as a seed — a link that onramps the next person into their own record.

Coordinate vector per receipt:  specificity · #assumptions · #clarifications · work-shape (effort/outcome/risk) · status · t-stamp.  Each briefing is one point in the field; the trajectory from stated to revealed is what the diff surface reads. The inferred fields are a first guess you review — never the seal.
Attestation is one-way — once sealed, the verbatim, intent and specificity lock; assumptions and clarifications stay editable. Inferred fields are a first guess you review, never the seal. Saved locally in this browser; a shared seed carries only what's in that one receipt.
