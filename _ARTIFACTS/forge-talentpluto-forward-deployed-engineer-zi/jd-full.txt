About the Company

TalentPluto is an early-stage company building the infrastructure that connects great operators to the startups that need them. Our platform gives professionals an AI agent that learns their real career story and controls how they are discovered by people, companies, and other AI agents, and gives hiring teams a matched, evidence-ranked pipeline instead of a resume pile.

We work with fast-moving venture-backed startups across go-to-market and engineering. The team is small and technical, we ship daily, and the product surface spans a candidate agent, voice and text onboarding, matching models, and deep integrations with the applicant tracking systems our clients already run.

The Opportunity

We are hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer to sit between our product and the companies using it. This is a highly technical, customer-facing role for someone who would rather solve a customer's problem in their environment than file a ticket about it.

You will own integrations, pilots, and technical onboarding for new clients, and you will be the person who makes the first weeks work. Because you will see exactly where the product breaks in the real world, your input will shape what engineering builds next.

Responsibilities

Own technical onboarding for new clients, from first integration through a working pipeline

Build and maintain integrations with client applicant tracking systems and hiring workflows

Debug data, webhook, and matching issues end to end, across our stack and the client's

Translate what clients actually need into concrete product and engineering requirements

Partner with go-to-market on technical conversations during evaluation and rollout

Build internal tooling that removes recurring manual work from the deployment process

Requirements

3+ years in software engineering, solutions engineering, forward deployed engineering, or technical consulting

Strong engineering fundamentals and comfort reading and writing production code, not just scripts

Experience debugging integrations, webhooks, and data pipelines across systems you do not control

Ability to work directly with customers and hold a credible technical conversation with senior engineers

Comfort operating with ambiguity and little structure at an early-stage company

Judgment about when to fix something for one customer and when to fix it in the product
