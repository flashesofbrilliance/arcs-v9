What the controls mean

Epochs (e1–e8) — the corpus's eight serializability epochs (2026-05-20 → 07-10). Every edge is stamped with the epoch its evidentiary road appeared; scrubbing replays the web's growth.
Metronome — five timekeepings: CHRONOS even ticks · KAIROS dwells on significant epochs · DILATION continuous time (fractional epochs, interpolated) · SUSPENSION holds time still so you can move through structure (axes, nodes) while nothing ages · SUPERPOSITION renders all 8 epochs at once.
Layers (each owns one visual channel): trails = colored history paths · entangle = violet dashes · invariants = ring grammar (dash/solid/dot) · satoshi = gold nested geometry.
Lens (changes what is emphasized, adds nothing): superposed dims every collapsed node.
