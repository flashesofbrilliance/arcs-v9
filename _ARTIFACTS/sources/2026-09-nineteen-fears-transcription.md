## Method applied on the page (one question per item)

"What would prove this wrong, and by when?" The page does NOT judge whether any fear is right.
Classes (form only, never truth):
- **checkable**: states or implies an observation that could count against it, as written.
- **sharpenable**: becomes checkable with one missing term (a metric or a date); the page shows the sharpened form.
- **fog**: the load-bearing term has no observable referent (simulation, "feel", circular "wins"), or it is hedged with may/might so no outcome can count against it.
- **value**: a statement of what is good, not a claim about the world; receipts do not apply (NOTOMATION: never overrule a preference).
- **missing**: not in the record.

Classification is the operator's read, shown with its reasoning on every card, and contestable.
