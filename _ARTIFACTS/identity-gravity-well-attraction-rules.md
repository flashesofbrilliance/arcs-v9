## What this surface is

identity.arcs.care is not a portfolio (a portfolio pushes: here is what I offer). It is a gravity
well (it pulls: here are my rules of resonance; if you fit, you constellate; if you do not, you pass
through, and that is the surface working, not failing). Its power is honesty: a well that states its
polarity truthfully attracts the right bodies and repels the wrong ones. State it falsely and you
pull noise and lose the fits.

The laws below are that polarity. Each has a pull (what it draws in) and a pass (what it lets go by).

## The laws

**1. Resonance over reach.**
I do not broadcast to everyone. I polarize. I would rather be a hard yes for a few than a soft maybe
for many.
- Pulls: people who felt something specific click when they read this.
- Passes: volume-seekers, anyone looking for a generalist to blanket a problem.
- Basis: [[zach-north-star-resonance-gradient-field]], [[arcs-purpose-honest-resonance-engine-not-propaganda]], [[icp-is-a-state-not-a-segment-catch-in-caught-breath]].

**2. Proof over promises.**
I show the shipped thing. I distrust the deck. If I cannot point at something running, I have not
earned the claim yet.
- Pulls: buyers burned by advisors who never shipped; people who want receipts.
- Passes: those who want a confident story more than a working artifact.
- Basis: the blessed flag "proof over promises, one operator accountable end to end"; [[brand-promise-refusal-to-overclaim]].

**3. Into the weeds, so you can get out of them.**
I am drawn to the tangled, high-stakes, regulated problem most people route around. The harder the
terrain and the more expensive a wrong move, the more the pull.
- Pulls: leaders standing at a fork where a mistake is costly and the path is genuinely unclear.
- Passes: low-stakes work, or anyone wanting a body to run a known playbook.
- Basis: zachharris.xyz signature line; WORLD.md BAR/ruin edge; [[project_todd_hotes_prl_resins]] (the consulting archetype).

**4. One operator, accountable end to end.**
The person you talk to is the person doing the work. No middle layer, no hand-off, no account manager
relaying messages.
- Pulls: those who want a single accountable throat, not a team to manage.
- Passes: buyers who want scale-by-headcount or a vendor to diffuse responsibility across.
- Basis: the flag; [[zach-personal-brand-and-job-search-system]].

**5. The human decides; I scaffold, never overrule.**
I am magnetized to human-in-the-loop. I sequence and sharpen cognition; I do not replace judgment or
launder a machine's guess as your revealed preference.
- Pulls: people who want leverage without surrendering the call; the human-on-the-bike stance.
- Passes: anyone chasing full automation that removes the human from the decision.
- Basis: NOTOMATION (constitutional); [[arcs-is-an-active-listening-aid]].

**6. Cross-domain echo.**
I am pulled to patterns that rhyme across unrelated fields. The best move is usually an isomorphism
carried in from somewhere that looks nothing like the problem.
- Pulls: people working a problem that has a hidden twin elsewhere; the orthogonally curious.
- Passes: strictly in-domain specialists who want only a same-field reference.
- Basis: ORTHOGONAL ECHO DETECTOR (constitutional); [[orthogonal-echo-proof-point]].

**7. Receipts or it did not happen.**
I attract people who want their claims checkable, and I repel spin. Provenance travels with the work,
written at the moment, not retrofitted as lore.
- Pulls: the evidence-first, the ones who ask "show me."
- Passes: those who prefer a polished narrative to a verifiable one.
- Basis: "Receipts or it didn't happen" (AGENTS.md); [[temporal-chain-of-custody-fact-vs-fiction]].

## The two poles, stated plainly

**Who constellates (the fit).** A decision-maker at a high-stakes fork in regulated or tangled
terrain, who wants one accountable operator that ships proof, keeps them in the driver's seat, and
leaves receipts. They value resonance over reach and evidence over story.

**Who passes through (the honest anti-fit).** Anyone wanting cheap volume, a confident deck without a
running artifact, a team to diffuse accountability, or full automation that takes the human out of
the call. Not a failure of the surface. The polarity doing its job.

## The pull (the invitation, not a contact form)

One line, resonance-first, for the body that felt the field: *If these are your rules too, we should
be in orbit.* Then the lightest possible affordance to constellate.
