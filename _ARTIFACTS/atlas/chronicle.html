The first commit on main is 471d5739, at 11:39 on the morning of 9 April 2026. Its subject calls it Day 1. The status page counts differently: on 29 September it reads DAY 173, which only works if 9 April is Day 0. The world began on a disagreement about where to start counting, and both counts are in the record.

What the scaffold contains is plain. Next.js 16, Prisma 7, Auth.js with a magic link. A schema with a User, a Receipt, and an Outcome. A proxy that guards two routes and sends anyone without a session on them to /compass. A package manifest that pulls in Tailwind, which the next book will have to carry out. The commit message ends with a done-state rather than a boast: fill the environment file, migrate, click the link, arrive at /dashboard with a session.

Then the record goes quiet. Between 471d5739 and a61d492e there are no commits on main at all. Thirty-nine days. It would be easy to fill that silence with a story. The Chronicle does not, and the reason is written into the second commit itself.

a61d492e lands on 18 May at 13:32. It carries two database migrations whose own folder names are timestamps: 20260410143234 and 20260410143652, the afternoon of 10 April, the day after Day 0. The work was being done. It was not being committed. The pipeline, the Y-gate, the brief, the guide, and a 188-line WORLD.md all arrive together, thirty-one files and 5,762 lines in one breath, authored from a different address than the first commit.

Before any of it, there is an older book. ADR-071 and the apex-spine canon cite a Book of ARCS from November 2025 as the earliest artifact, the place where three was first named the minimum for coherence. It is not in this repository's history, so this Chronicle cannot prove it. It is cited here as what came before, and marked as such.

Book II · Svadhisthana (flow)

Amend the source of truth first, then the pages that derive from it, so the promise stays literally true and not merely presented as true.

tao-150 · _CANON/tao-proverbs.jsonl
Left behind
A single machine and a single database file. And, at the last commit, Tailwind: a Day 1 dependency that no component had ever used, carried for ninety-three days.
Taken up
Production. Postgres on Supabase with every table denied by default, deploys that run from main only, and a run of counterpart briefs on their own subdomains.
The seam
The first successful deploy is dated 28 May in the handoff record. Git holds the fix that made it possible, not the deploy itself.
1,000 ft · 1080p · Operational

The leaving starts small. On 25 May, 6c03d396 adds one line so that Vercel generates the Prisma client when it installs, because the build server is not the laptop. The next day cb21d0fe moves the datasource to Postgres. On 27 May f4aa8336 teaches the database layer to tell the two worlds apart, SQLite at home and Postgres on the server, and 91b746c0 closes every one of the nine Supabase tables to the public until a rule opens it. The house was built with its doors locked.

Then the book fills up. Thirty-two ADRs are added in this range, and the surfaces multiply faster than the rules: briefs for people on the other side of a call, each on its own subdomain, some behind a password. By 7 July the deploy script has to learn to refuse any worktree but main (3f2c2c9f), and the subdomain registry has to be restored after it was lost (3b2cd16a). Motion was high. Not all of it was direction.

The commit tags tell the same story from inside. The NCI score in the subject line climbs until one commit reads 227 (e6b590e8), on a rubric whose thresholds sit at 45 and 50, and 213 commits in this book skip the score entirely. A number that only goes up has stopped measuring.

The book ends on 11 July with 445ebf08, and its subject is the plainest sentence in the whole history: pay down false-claim debt. Tailwind goes, 'unused Day-1 scaffold violating the inline-styles rule since the first commit.' A README that called the operator library production-ready canonical is relabeled a reference implementation. A stdlib-only claim is corrected, since 12 of 34 files import numpy or scipy. A guide that cited five files that do not exist gets a provenance notice. The exodus was out of a stack, and it was also out of a way of describing the stack.

10,000 ft · 480p · Strategic

A gap between dev and prod stays hidden until the crossing; put the gate at the crossing, not after it.

tao-099 · _CANON/tao-proverbs.jsonl
Left behind

Taken up
Law and gates: enforcement parity, zero trust, a secrets inventory, a split between public and private, and a smoke gate that rolls a bad deploy back on its own.
The seam

1,000 ft · 1080p · Operational

Leviticus opens the day the Tailwind commit lands and runs to the first of August. Twenty-one days, 503 commits, and only six pull-request merges. Most of the law was written straight onto main.

On 15 July 2b69043c puts three machines at the crossing between a branch and the public: a drift check that compares stated state with real state, a front-end smoke gate, and a rollback that fires after a deploy goes bad. The same day c3835253 takes a phone number, an email, and a location off a public resume page. Both are the same instinct, a gate placed where the harm would cross, not after.

Nine ADRs are added, and they read like a code of conduct for a system that had just caught itself overclaiming: enforcement parity (e6c72e3e), a commit NCI rubric (f32d1323), secrets rotation (06ca51b0), a zero-trust posture (1e608106), a public and private repo split (70bf93ec), a stateless anonymous share (4ab01066).

And here is the seam, shown rather than smoothed. Enforcement parity, the NCI rubric, and the serialization pathogen family were all filed as ADR-033, on 16 and 19 July. Anonymous receipt remediation and zero trust were both ADR-032. Each author inferred the next number from what they could see, and parallel sessions saw the same thing. The law was sound. The ledger of laws was not, yet.

The seam

1,000 ft · 1080p · Operational

Deuteronomy runs from 16 September to the render, and in thirteen days it adds a single ADR, 8595ed3e, on 26 September. Everything else is restatement. On 26 September the belief log from August finally reaches main (2492e535). On 27 September 373fc292 binds the operator suite to a registry that is generated from the periodic table and refuses any binding it cannot find in the code, and 42135091 prints the Accountant's scoreboard from real data.

The same day the law turns outward. 06ca7c8e stamps the count of falsified beliefs onto the public audit page on good.arcs.care, and merges as PR #591 (740aa55f). The number is never typed. A script computes it from the ledger, and the build fails if the page falls behind. This is the throat, Vishuddha: the system saying in public what it got wrong.

On 28 September PR #613 (150beb19) teaches Operator 21, the Librarian, to retrieve by expected value instead of by recency. On 27 September ac3163cc defines Operator 25, Shavasana, the pause before any irreversible act that adds no new claim, and 64d4f362 puts it at the handoff door. It is the same pause that closes each book of this Chronicle.

The book ends at aef6180a, which promotes a DAY 173 card to the front of the status page. Genesis began with a Receipt table and no decisions in it. Deuteronomy ends with the receipts spoken aloud and most of their outcomes still unrecorded. Ajna, the seat of the fixed gaze, is not reached. The record says so.
