About Pylon

America’s $13T mortgage market is one of the most important financial systems in the world. It underwrites the middle class and is the mechanism through which millions of families build wealth. But while every other financial instrument has been simplified to an API call, mortgages are still assembled by hand.

We started from zero and created the first vertically integrated mortgage platform that turns origination into a single API.

Publicly traded companies and the country’s largest originators are already building on Pylon. Revenue is compounding monthly. We’re backed by Peter Thiel, Conversion Capital, QED, Citi, Fifth Wall, and the founders of Ramp, Blend, and Mercury.

Working at Pylon isn’t for those seeking comfort. The people who thrive here have high agency, strong opinions, and a track record of delivering outcomes without direction. Many of us are former founders. We move quickly, challenge each other directly, and take full ownership of results. It’s hard work, but it will be worth it.

Join us in building America’s mortgage rails.


ABOUT THE ROLE

Every customer runs their mortgage business a bit differently. They have their own systems, teams, and ways of serving borrowers, and Pylon only works if it fits into how they actually operate.

We’re hiring a Lead Forward Deployed PM to embed with our most strategic customers, learn their business from the inside, and build Pylon into the way they work. You’ll spend time with their loan officers, operations teams, and engineers, figure out how loans really move through the organization, and map out how to put Pylon at the center of it.

This is a senior role. You’ll work directly with customer executives and technical leaders, take charge of complicated, high-stakes projects, and push them to real results, even when that means asking customers to change how they work.

You’ll also be the link between our customers and our product team. What you learn in the field will shape what we build, and how quickly you get customers live will have a direct impact on Pylon’s growth.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

 - Embed with strategic customers, on site and remotely, to understand their teams, systems, and full mortgage workflow

 - Map how each customer works today, figure out where Pylon adds the most value, and put together a plan that gets them live quickly

 - Own each integration from scoping and solution design through data mapping, configuration, testing, and launch, working with their engineers and ours

 - Find the manual steps, workarounds, and bottlenecks in each customer’s process and replace them with Pylon

 - Write and ship code yourself, including integrations, scripts, configurations, internal tools, and agents on our platform, and commit changes to our core codebase when a customer needs them

 - Bring what you learn back to the product team. You’ll figure out which requests are one-offs and which should become part of the core platform, and work with product to build them

 - Be the person accountable for each engagement. You’ll work directly with customer executives and technical leaders to set goals, get everyone aligned, make the hard calls, and hold both sides to the results

 - Advise customer leadership on how to modernize their mortgage operations, and work with GTM and Account Management to support deals, grow relationships, and make sure customers succeed

 - Define and track what success looks like for each customer, including time to go live, adoption, loan volume, and efficiency gains

 - Build playbooks and reusable tools so every integration goes faster than the last

 - Set the standard for how Pylon deploys, and help hire, mentor, and grow the forward deployed team

 - Use AI in your day-to-day work
