Law 1 · Earned fidelity

A surface renders only as sharply as its evidence deserves, and never lies about how certain it is. Confidence is a visual channel, not a claim.

Law 2 · Instrument, not dashboard

Surface the reading first, detail on demand. Every hue, glow, and motion carries exactly one signal. Cut what does not earn its place.
