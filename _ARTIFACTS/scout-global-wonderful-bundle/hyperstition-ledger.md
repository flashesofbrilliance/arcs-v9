## Decomposition rehearsal (illustrative, not a Wonderful fact)
Use Wonderful's own six steps (identify workflow, connect systems, build agent, deploy, monitor, improve) against a workflow from their stated verticals, for example inbound collections calls at a bank. Rehearse out loud, 20 minutes:
1. **Workflow and success metric:** contact-to-resolution rate, promise-to-pay kept, handle time, escalation rate; name the baseline you would ask for.
2. **Systems and data:** core banking, CRM, call recording, payments, identity checks; access, latency and consent per system.
3. **Agent design:** voice first; tools it may call; what it must never do; hand-off rules to a human.
4. **Guardrails and regulation:** disclosure, call-time rules, vulnerable-customer handling, audit trail.
5. **Pilot to production:** a bounded pilot slice, eval set from real calls, go/no-go thresholds agreed in advance.
6. **Monitor and expand:** drift, failure review, the next use case. State what you do not know and what you would ask in week one.
This maps to Strategist (scoping, plan, stakeholders) and shows the build depth FDE needs (tools, evals, failure modes). Deliberately includes unknowns; do not invent Wonderful internals.
