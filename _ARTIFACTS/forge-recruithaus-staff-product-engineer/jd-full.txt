About Our Client

The company started by building infrastructure to run CI workloads substantially faster and cheaper than standard cloud offerings — by owning and operating its own global fleet of bare-metal machines rather than renting generic cloud VMs.

Today, it orchestrates tens of millions of lightweight virtual machines each month, running CI for 3,000+ companies, and reached roughly $10M in ARR in under two years. Revenue has more than tripled since the start of 2026.

It operates thousands of bare-metal machines across multiple regions, regularly schedules 100k+ vCPUs concurrently, and runs and manages its own petabyte-scale distributed storage cluster.

The company has raised $13.5M across Seed and Series A rounds, led by a top-tier venture capital firm, and is intentionally building a small but exceptional team.

It was founded by a team with deep systems and scaling experience, including building search/ads infrastructure at a well-known e-commerce marketplace and operating large-scale distributed systems at a prominent distributed-database company. Go-to-market is led by an experienced sales leader who previously headed sales at a major workflow-automation software company.

The company is now extending its core infrastructure into a broader platform — running agent sandboxes at scale and building its own background coding agent.
