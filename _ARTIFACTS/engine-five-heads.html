Composability

Every surface is an atom with a socket: same envelope in (id · type · tenant · provenance · updated · associations[]), same heads out. That is why five of them consolidate into one monolith in an afternoon — composition was designed in on instance #1, not retrofitted.

Interoperability

The performers don’t know about each other — and don’t need to. They interoperate through the shared invariants: one score, one grammar, one fidelity law, one registry. Swap any performer’s internals and the ensemble still plays, because the contract lives in the envelope, not the implementation.

Socialize & self-reticulate

Standalone microservices that socialize: each publishes its associations into the registry (artifact-registry.jsonl), and the links knit themselves into a lattice — sibling, renders, echo — without a central planner. The web self-reticulates: add a sixth performer and it finds its family by conforming, not by being wired in.

the invariant beneath the polyform

Five bodies, four heads, one engine. Parameterize the invariant and you get a family for free; conform to the envelope and the family finds you. That is the whole idea — and this page is its working proof.
