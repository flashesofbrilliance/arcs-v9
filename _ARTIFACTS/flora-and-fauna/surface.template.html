Succession

From pioneer fog to climax crystal

Early: heavy fog, coarse briefs, yellow gates firing. Mid: patterns surface, the compass gets faster, NCI climbs. Late: navigation from the clarity ridge, the receipt history a personal atlas. Superionic: structure of clarity, fluidity of instinct.

Graduation is not extinction. A healthy ecosystem needs its keystone species to need it less over time. One that must keep its operator dependent to survive is a monoculture.
