 Who Is Flip? 

 There are 3 runaway categories in B2B AI — coding, legal, and customer support. We're at the center of the third, with a one-of-one market position and hundreds of diehard fan clients, from Under Armour to Tory Burch to some of the largest healthcare institutions in the country.

 The market is dividing into two camps: generic tooling-and-FDE players building custom agents from scratch, and industry-specific solutions winning on client love due to speed of ROI. We're firmly the latter. Retail and healthcare alone represent $5B+ TAMs each, and we're still early.

 We built the company on a simple idea: the best way to grow is to have a reference list as long as your customer list. We're a team of 100+ now, with offices in NYC, LA, and the UK. Many people here will tell you it's the best place they've worked and the hardest they've worked. Both are true.

 What You’ll Do 

 You’ll work on problems like:

 Automating complex customer calls (subscriptions, billing, healthcare workflows)

 Designing AI agent behavior in ambiguous, multi-turn conversations

 Building systems that combine voice, AI, APIs, integrations, and real time decisioning

 Moving client outcomes: automation rate, resolution rate, revenue collected, retention, and ROI

 Day-to-Day 

 Own one or more of Flip’s most complex product areas end to end, from the client problem through measurable outcome

 Work directly with enterprise clients, from executives to frontline agents; watch real workflows, find the underlying problem, and push back when the requested solution isn’t the right one

 Get your own answers: write SQL, call APIs directly, and use scripts, notebooks, and AI coding tools to prototype before asking engineering to productize

 Run tight loops of problem → prototype → client feedback → production, measured in days, not weeks

 Partner with engineers as a technical peer on architecture, integrations, evals, failure modes, latency, and reliability; debug in production alongside them

 Ship as part of our WEEKLY product releases (https://flipcx.com/product-releases/)

 Turn one-off client solutions into scalable product used across hundreds of brands

 Build and lead a team: hire, manage, and develop PMs and Product Owners, sharpen how we make decisions, and model the builder mentality
