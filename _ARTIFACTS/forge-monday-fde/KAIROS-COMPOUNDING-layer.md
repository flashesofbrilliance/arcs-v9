# Kairos and compounding layer (private; added to the chorus)
Adds the "time signature" questions every voice is asking underneath their objections, and defines what to build so value compounds. Run it after the epode as a final pass on the outline and the draft.

## The five time-signature questions (every persona asks these, in their own words)
1. **Why now (kairos)?** What about this moment makes this the right time?
2. **Why this instrument?** Why are you the right tool for this job?
3. **Can you actually play it?** Where is the proof of mastery across the instruments the job needs?
4. **Does this make my life easier?** Less time, less energy, less frustration, more trust and clarity.
5. **What will compound?** What do we keep after this engagement that makes the next one faster?

## Honest answers, and what to avoid
**1. Why now.** Evidence-based timing, from public facts only: monday relaunched as an AI work platform in May 2026; the strategic CS and FDE practice formed in February 2026; deployment-strategy roles appeared in Sept and Oct 2026. Compounding is cheapest while a practice is young. Say what you know and keep it light. Do not claim insight into internal plans.

**2 and 3. Right tool, can you play the instruments.** Do not claim "better than any other candidate": you cannot know the field, and the claim invites a fight you cannot win. Prove range with receipts instead. Each instrument has a proof, an honest tempo, and a limit.

| Instrument | Proof you can show | Honest limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive and outcome framing | Grayscale gates and results; the outcome-first structure of the deliverable | Few post-launch adoption measures in your record |
| Delivery and scoping | Squat sole ownership; WTA phased rollout | Results for some stories not yet filled in |
| Integration and APIs | The take-home build; your MCP tool layer (private) | About a year and a half of daily TypeScript; no customer on the MCP layer yet |
| Agents and evals | AI-use log; evals with a pass bar; public ARCS surfaces | Outcome capture on ARCS is low; say so |
| Governance in regulated settings | Evidence, PII, audit, change control, one-way doors in your own words | Not certified; a design discipline, not a compliance claim |
| The monday platform | A tenant you set up with role-based access (about 20 users) | Agents, vibe apps and MCP in a customer setting not yet done |

**Adaptive time signature** means shifting altitude and tempo to the audience: 60 seconds for an executive, 30 minutes for a surgical reader. That is the skill Sam noticed ("zooming out and in"). Show it in how the deliverable is layered.

**4. Make my life easier.** Say it in each altitude's currency.
| Altitude | What it saves them |
| --- | --- |
| Executive | Decision time and risk: one sentence, one sourced number |
| Strategic | Coherence: a plan that fits the rest of the strategy |
| Operational | Estimation and staffing effort: a ready estimate, RACI and handoff |
| Tactical | Teaching effort: steps and templates they can reuse |
| Surgical | Verification effort: tests, failure modes, honest limits |
Across all of them: fewer surprises, shared language, and a log that shows how decisions were made (trust).

**5. What compounds.** Candidate assets, each reusable by the practice and across client scopes. Choose one or two after reading the brief; do not promise all.
| Asset | What it compresses | How you would measure it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engagement playbook (scope, success measure, build, evals, change control, adoption, feedback to product) | Time from kickoff to first value | Days to first value, engagement one versus engagement two |
| Discovery and friction-audit template | Discovery effort and rework | Hours to a ranked friction list |
| Connector pattern library with read-after-write checks | Integration defects and support load | Defects per integration |
| Agent qualification harness that fixes known gaps (fixtures reach agent and judge, run metadata, hard-fail checks) | Time to trust a new agent | Runs to a stable pass rate |
| Receipts (one decision record per engagement) | Re-explaining and audit effort | Time to answer "why did we do this?" |
| Governance pack for regulated clients (data classes, model tiers, approvals) | Security review cycles | Days in review |
| Land-and-expand map (first value, then expansion triggers) | Time from first win to the next scope | Time to second scope |

**Compounding without hype.** Say "compress time to value," not "exponentially." Treat compounding as a hypothesis with a falsifier: if engagement two with the kit is not faster than engagement one on days to first value, the asset is not earning its place. Do not quote a percentage until you have a baseline.

## Add to the chorus

- Add a "Why now, why you, what we keep" box to the one-page executive summary (three short lines).
- In the walkthrough, show the compounding asset you chose and the single metric that will tell you if it works.
