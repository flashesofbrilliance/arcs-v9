### Skills-based
- **Wiring an MCP integration into a customer system:** list the tools the agent needs, scope each to least privilege, require approval on irreversible actions, test on real workflows, then ship.
- **Evaluating an agent:** golden cases from the customer's real work, a pass bar agreed in advance, human checkpoint on risky steps, trace review, and never trust the agent's own report of success.
- **Platform versus custom code:** configure first, then an agent or vibe app, then a script only where the platform ends; turn repeats into reusable assets.

### Scenario
- **Week 6, executive sponsor sees no adoption:** meet the sponsor first, show usage by role, name the gating step, fix the workflow before adding features, and reset the success measure honestly.
- **Customer asks for something the platform cannot do:** clarify the underlying outcome, offer a workaround, and file it to product with evidence from the account.
- **An agent errs in production:** contain it, find the root cause, add the case to the eval set, tell the customer plainly, and report it to product.
- **Two strategic accounts need you at once:** protect the committed outcome dates, communicate early, and bring in help rather than quietly dropping one.

### Take-home or case-study method
Restate the goal and the success measure, list assumptions and constraints, give a three-step plan, name the top risks, say what you would measure, and time-box it. The team walkthrough tests your reasoning, so you must be able to explain every choice. Their AI policy means disclose any AI use and be able to defend all of it.
