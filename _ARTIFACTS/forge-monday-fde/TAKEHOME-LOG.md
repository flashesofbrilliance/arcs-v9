## Rules
1. **Log before you act** on any material choice, then record the result after.
2. **AI use is disclosed here** (monday's interview policy): what tool, what for, what you changed or rejected. Be able to explain every line.

4. **Sources:** every factual claim or number cites where it came from.
5. **No retroactive rationale:** if you decided something by feel, say so.
