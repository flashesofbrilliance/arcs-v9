# Cover letter: Upside · Staff Forward Deployed Engineer, GTM Platform

Hi Upside team,

I build the system, then I stay on the hook for what it changes in the business.

Your posting asks for the first engineer on a platform meant to take over work your go-to-market teams do today across legacy tooling. I have done the embedded half of this before: learn how the work actually runs, own the build, and leave a system the team can change without me. At Squat New York, a $350M-ARR behavioral health company, I was the sole product and technology partner and tech lead on a seven-figure embedded engagement from scoping through QA and handoff. I owned the technical direction and the architecture, rebuilt their front-end and back-end platform end to end, and stayed accountable through production. At Grayscale I sat with revenue and operations teams across 10 regulated launches and learned how that work actually runs. At the Children's Tumor Foundation I built the first data layer the fundraising, marketing and clinical teams had, and net-new revenue rose 20%.

The platform side is what I build now. I founded ARCS and build it myself: TypeScript and Next.js on Postgres, a Python operator layer that coding agents reach through an MCP server, and agentic orchestration on Claude. I make the build-versus-buy calls, set the patterns it grows on, and write down the decisions and tradeoffs so the next engineer can build on them.

On the model side, every response is checked and gated before it is saved, and new prompts are scored against the current one on fixed cases before they ship. The harder lesson was on the business side. I built outcome capture so ARCS could score its calls against what actually happened, but people rarely come back to log how a decision turned out, so I keep calibration switched off until there are enough outcomes to tune on. For an internal GTM platform the same question decides everything: what does the revenue team do after the system answers, and how do you find out. So I would start by sitting with the people acquiring merchants and serving partners, and build the first thing so the outcome gets captured inside work they already do, not in a form they have to come back to.

I am in New York Metro. Happy to go deep on any of it.

With gratitude,
