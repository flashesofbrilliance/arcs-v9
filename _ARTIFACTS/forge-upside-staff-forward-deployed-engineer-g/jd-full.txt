Meet Upside:

We created Upside to transform brick-and-mortar commerce. Our technology uses the sophistication of online retail—profit measurement, attribution, and incrementality—to provide users with more value on their everyday purchases and brick-and-mortar businesses with new, profitable customers. About the role

This is the first engineering hire on a new internal platform effort inside Upside's Revenue Operations organization. You'll be building a zero-to-one, AI-native system for our go-to-market teams; a substrate for agentic orchestration that is meant to take over the work our teams do today across cumbersome legacy tooling.

Reporting to the Sr. Director of Revenue Operations, you'll operate as a forward deployed engineer: embedded directly with the business, close to the people whose problems you're solving, and measured on outcomes rather than output. You'll sit with revenue teams, understand how they actually work, and build the systems that change it. You will define the architecture and technical direction of this platform.

We're deliberate about the title. The forward deployed model means deep business alignment, tight feedback loops, and a bias toward shipping something real. The difference here is that your users are internal: the teams that acquire merchants, serve partners, and drive Upside's revenue.

What you'll do

Build the first version of an AI-native platform for Upside's go-to-market teams, working across agentic orchestration, automation, and the interfaces our revenue teams use every day

Embed with revenue teams to learn their workflows firsthand, then design and ship systems that meaningfully change how that work gets done

Own technical direction. You'll make the architecture calls, choose the tools, and set the patterns this platform grows on

Move from prototype to production quickly, then keep iterating as AI capabilities change underneath you

Anchor your work to business outcomes. Success here is measured in what the platform unlocks for the business, not in tickets closed

Work with our security and infrastructure partners to make sure what you build is sound and supportable

Document decisions and tradeoffs clearly so the team that follows you can build on your work

What we're looking for

Staff-level engineering experience — roughly 8 years or comparable depth built through another path with a history of owning systems end to end

Experience taking something from zero to one: greenfield systems, early-stage products, or internal platforms you built from nothing

Real experience building with LLMs and AI APIs in production, including prompt design, output evaluation, and iterating toward reliability

Fluency with AI-assisted development tools as part of how you work, not as an experiment

Strong judgment about technical tradeoffs, and the ability to refactor and change direction as requirements shift

Genuine interest in the business side. You'll be spending time with revenue and operations teams, and curiosity about how they work is a real requirement of this role

The ability to work with a high degree of independence and ambiguity, and to bring stakeholders along as you go

Clear communication with both engineers and non-engineers

We're language agnostic. We care much more about how you solve problems, make decisions, and adapt than about which languages are on your résumé. Our current prototypes lean on React, TypeScript, and Python, but you'll have real input into what this platform is built with.

Nice to have

Understanding of CRM and revenue data models — Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar. Note that this role is about building what comes after those tools, not integrating deeply with them

Experience as an early or founding engineer at a startup, or as the person who built an internal tooling function

Familiarity with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, and embedding-based search

Experience in marketplace or high-growth environments

Experience with modern data platforms such as Snowflake
