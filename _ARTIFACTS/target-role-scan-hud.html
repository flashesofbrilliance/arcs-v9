03Fit Filterscreen-in · screen-out
Must-haves: the deal shape that fits
  • Build-and-embed role: real building, real ownership; not maintenance or pure management
  • AI-native surface: LLMs/agents core to the product or the work, not a bolt-on
  • Senior IC / lead / staff / founding scope with decision latitude (can decide without six approvals)
  • Sustainable, experience-valuing culture: positive-screen signals (GPTW/Comparably, family-friendly, tenure mix in senior roles)
  • Comp band at senior/staff level
  • Remote or NY-metro friendly (Fairfield, CT)
Disqualifiers: screen out
  • Pure people-management with no building
  • "AI" as costume: nothing real for it to do
  • Junior/entry leveling or "young & scrappy only" framing (risk-register)
  • 996 / always-on grind; wide/loose comp banding across locations for one role (risk-register)
  • Keep-the-lights-on maintenance with no decision authority
  • Domain he has no interest in or can't add real value to

06Key Assertionthe load-bearing sentence

The search is not "apply widely." It is size ruthlessly, invest only where the genome compounds: forward-deployed, AI-native, high-stakes, breadth-rewarding roles. The rubric is the external brake/throttle: it decides go/no-go before the maximizer redlines on every green light, so full effort goes to the PURSUE handful, not to every alert in the inbox. The strongest résumé in the world tailored to a role that doesn't fit is wasted motion the sizing step exists to prevent.
