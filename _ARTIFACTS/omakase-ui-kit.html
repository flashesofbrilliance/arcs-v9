Law 1 · Color is never ornament

Gold means credit and value. Vermillion means the cut: the adversary, the checkpoint, the thing that earns the spend. If a color is not carrying meaning, it is not on the page.

Law 2 · Coursed, not scrolled

The work is plated in sequence and paced. Each course lands whole before the next. Restraint is the luxury: white space, one idea per plate, nothing hurried.

Do's and don'ts

Do give gold and vermillion a job every time they appear (credit / the cut). Two accents, no more.
Do course the content: sequence, pace, one idea per plate, generous restraint.
Do credit the evidence every time it travels.
Don't use color as ornament, or add a third accent. If it carries no meaning, remove it.
Don't import the Field OS cockpit (cyan, bright gold, Inter, glow). This world is warm, editorial, Helvetica.
Don't use em-dashes; prefer commas, colons, or the interpunct (project rule).
