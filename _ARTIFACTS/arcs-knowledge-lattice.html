The 720° skeptic-led pressure test

Every node in this lattice carries a burden of proof. Two full sweeps — the first circles the claim from outside before commitment; the second circles it again after evidence, then turns the instrument on the examiner. 360° of skeptic + 360° of self-audit = 720°.

First 360° — anterior (before commitment)
  • LAB / Loki — strongest objection stated in its strongest form, not a strawman.
  • BAR — ruin bound: what's the worst survivable loss if this claim is wrong?
  • Bayesian prior — base rate before our evidence; wishful deltas flagged.
  • Premortem war-game — it's 12 months later and this concept failed: write the obituary first.
  • COURT — axiom collision check (NOTOMATION, honesty, consistency).
  • Burden assigned — who bears proof, and what observation would falsify.
Second 360° — posterior (after evidence)
  • Receipts — what did we actually record, with outcomes where they exist?
  • Recoil — reverse-listen the history: did it land where it was heading?
  • Bayesian update — posterior moved by evidence, not by attachment.
  • Orthogonal echo — did uncorrelated domains independently confirm? (This is the hardness metric.)
  • KKL — is the window still open, or are we defending a stale claim?
  • Fun House — audit the auditor: anchoring, sycophancy, self-confirmation, canon written faster than loops closed.
Diamond rule (highest burden of proof): a node crystallizes only when it survives both sweeps AND holds ≥4 in-edges from distinct uncorrelated domains. Counter-forces are constitutional, not optional: edge-decay, contrarian-injection, and calcification-guard (lattice-pressure ROM, L7-pinned) exist precisely so the lattice cannot harden by enthusiasm alone.
