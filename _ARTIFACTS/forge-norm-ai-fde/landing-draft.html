The forge

How this bundle was built, and what it refused to say.

An agentic pipeline read the posting, pulled the master résumé as the record of record, graded every claim by how checkable it is, red-teamed the draft, rendered the pages, and ran an integrity check at the end. The stages are a real sequence, so they are numbered. Nothing below is marketing: it is the method, shown.

GatherGradeRed-teamRenderKintsugi
  1. Gather
    Read the full posting from the Ashby board (not a scraper summary), fetched the master résumé, and extracted its text. Titles, dates, and employers reconciled line by line before anything else moved.
    GateNothing advances that could fail a background check.PrintedThe posting text and the record of record, side by side.
  2. Grade
    Every lead claim got an evidence grade. Documented means a public URL, a public figure, or inspectable code. Self-reported means the candidate's own figure, plausible but not independently checkable from outside.
    Documented · ARCS live on Claude · $7B AUM · open-source toolsSelf-reported · seven-figure engagement · 20% lift · 60% noise reduction
    GateNo ungraded claim reaches the page.PrintedThe tailoring notes: each posting hook mapped to what the record honestly puts against it.
  3. Red-team
    An adversarial pass over the draft, subtractive only. It may remove or soften; it may not add.
    Cut: "then teach their people to run it." Enablement sessions are in the posting, not in the record. The record supports handoff, so it says handoff.
    Cut: "twenty years in regulated settings." The regulated work is recent; the twenty years are product. Rewritten to say exactly that.
    Cut: "translating a rule into working software." The record supports translating rules into product requirements. Softened to match.
    Never claimed: FastAPI, Dagster, Airflow, and a "4+ years of Python" count. None of them are in the record, so none of them are on the résumé, even though the posting names each one.
    GateEvery claim that fails the evidence bar is removed to a defend-or-drop list.PrintedThe red-team log, with each fix applied to the cut.
  4. Render
    Print-safe HTML to PDF through headless Chrome, then each page rasterized and looked at: overflow, orphaned headings, bad breaks. A plain-text ATS copy extracted from the same PDF.
    GatePages are inspected as pixels. A page count is not a pass.PrintedRésumé PDF (2 pages, US letter), page images, ATS text.
  5. Kintsugi
    A final integrity receipt: what checked healthy, and every seam that had to be repaired along the way, recorded rather than hidden. Repairs are shown in gold, not painted over.
    GateThe receipt names each break, what caught it, and the fix.PrintedThe kintsugi receipt for this bundle.
