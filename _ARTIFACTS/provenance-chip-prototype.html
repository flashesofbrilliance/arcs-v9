Provenance Chips

Utilities that ship with their literature

Each chip reads simple, then rich, then deep. Simple is the tool. Rich is how it works. Deep is its provenance: the notes from the trenches on the conditions it was born in. Use it shallow, or sound its origin.

Utilityproven
resume-tailor
Turn one record into a role-tuned resume, from a single profile-scan source of truth.
open ↗github
Rich · how it works◇
Reads a LinkedIn export plus a profile scan, tailors against one job description, and emits HTML, PDF, and an ATS-plain text. Field-tested at v0.2. The record stays the source of truth; each output is a re-fold of it, never a rewrite.
Deep · provenance◇
The literature
Born in the 2026-08 job-search sprint, from one lesson: read the JD, not the title. An old batch scored zero clean fits because the tool matched titles; the fix was to tailor against the actual job to be done.
