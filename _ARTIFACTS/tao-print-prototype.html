Tao print prototype

One mold, repeated until the ink gives out

The ARCS Möbius, pulled nine times in four flat inks. The two faces of the strip print in different inks, so you can see it has one surface. Plates slip, ink drops out and the horizon breaks up in proportion to (1 minus fidelity). Fidelity is declared in the recipe, not measured. The last panel of the strip turns over with its inks swapped.

Reproducible from proverb id, recipe and seed. Not an edition: no scarcity, no registry.
