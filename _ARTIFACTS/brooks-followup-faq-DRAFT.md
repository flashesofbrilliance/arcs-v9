Subject: what this is, in three answers

Brooks,

Good to finally put a voice to the name. In case I buried the lede, here it is in three answers.

**What is it?** Before you spend real money on a bet, you write it on one page: what you think will happen, the number that would prove you wrong, and the date you'll check. Then you check. That's the whole thing.

**How could it help Louisville Brands?** You're taking three brands to ten. Every launch, drop, agency, and package is a bet. Today those bets live in memory, and memory rewrites itself after the fact. A page doesn't. A year of them is something few companies your size own: a record of what you believed, what you bet, and what actually happened. It sharpens the next bet, and it reads as discipline to anyone looking in from outside.

**Why should you care?** Because the cost of a wrong bet at a holding company is uncapped, and the cost of writing it down is an hour. The first one is a working session with you, on a call you're already about to make. You'll know by the end whether it earned a second.

If there's one bet in your lane you'd want on paper before the money moves, name it and I'll bring the page.
