Title: Senior Product Manager, Post Sales
Location: Austin; New York City; Palo Alto; Seattle
Updated: 2026-09-11T15:37:10-04:00

The IT Go-to-Market (GTM) organization builds and operates the technology platforms that enable the end-to-end customer lifecycle, spanning Sales, Revenue Operations, Customer Support, Customer Success, and Professional Services.

 As a Senior Product Manager on the IT GTM team, you will own the product strategy and roadmap for post-sales technology platforms, including CRM, Service Cloud, support operations, customer portals, AI-enabled support capabilities, and enterprise integrations.

 You will partner with business and engineering leaders to define product vision, prioritize investments, and deliver scalable solutions that improve customer and agent experiences, drive operational efficiency, and support business growth.

 We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Palo Alto, New York City, Seattle, or Austin for our hybrid working model.

 Responsibilities

 Domain Expertise

 Proven experience modernizing Technical Support platforms, including workforce planning, workflow automation and optimization, customer and partner collaboration and i, and knowledge-sharing solutions

 Strong understanding of end-to-end support user journey, including case creation and management, omni-channel case routing, prioritization, system integrations, and AI-driven proactive customer engagement

 Extensive knowledge of integration patterns across Salesforce Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, external support portals, and analytics platforms to deliver seamless customer and agent experiences

 Broad understanding of the post-sales ecosystem spanning Professional Services, Technical Support, and Customer Success, with the ability to design solutions that optimize cross-functional workflows

 Understanding of expertise in customer and support agent journeys, support processes, and operational workflows, with a track record of leading large-scale digital transformations and delivering custom solutions that address complex business challenges

 Expertise in building and scaling technology solutions for Customer Support, Customer Success, and Professional Services organizations

 Product Strategy & Vision

 Translate business strategy, customer needs, and operational priorities into scalable, platform-based product capabilities

 Partner with business and engineering leaders to align product investments with enterprise architecture, security, governance, and platform standards

 Develop clear product requirements (PRDs), prioritize roadmaps, define measurable success metrics, and drive adoption through data-driven decision making

 Balance short-term business priorities with long-term platform strategy to maximize customer value, operational efficiency, and business impact

 Champion a product-led mindset by continuously incorporating customer feedback, operational insights, and market trends into product evolution

 Stakeholder Engagement

 Partner with Customer Org and identify opportunities

 Represent the voice of the user, ensuring solutions address real operational challenges

 Communicate priorities, trade-offs, timelines, and progress clearly to stakeholders and leadership

 Build strong partnerships with engineering teams, architects, and external vendors

 Roadmap & Delivery

 Own and maintain the product backlog and roadmap, prioritizing work based on business impact and technical feasibility

 Collaborate closely with engineering teams in an Agile environment to plan and deliver product releases

 Balance new feature development with platform improvements and technical debt

 Ensure smooth product delivery by managing cross-team dependencies and aligning stakeholders

 Adoption & Change Management

 Partner with enablement and change management teams to drive adoption of new GTM capabilities

 Communicate product changes and new features across impacted teams

 Monitor KPIs and support incident or problem management when issues arise

 Requirements

 6+ years of Product Management experience in enterprise SaaS or cloud platforms

 Strong domain expertise in Customer Support tooling and Post-Sales solutions

 Experience building custom workflow applications, automation platforms, or AI-powered tools

 Deep knowledge of Sales and Service Cloud and customer lifecycle processes

 Experience working with telemetry, analytics, and customer health data

 Ability to partner effectively with engineering, operations, and business stakeholders

 Familiarity with AI-enabled Post Sales, focused on Customer Support workflows or automation tools

 Experience using product metrics and analytics to guide decisions

 Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience)
