DepthHow it works+Analysis, determinism, the ladder, and what stays on your device.

The page decodes your file with the browser, mixes it to mono, and measures it once: a 2048-point FFT at 30 frames a second, 64 bands from 40 Hz to 16 kHz on a log scale, a loudness value, and a measure of how much the spectrum changes between frames. A novelty curve compares the 8 seconds before each moment with the 8 seconds after; its peaks are the chapters.

After that, every picture is a pure function of those measurements and the time. Nothing is stored in the picture, so scrubbing back gives exactly the same frame. Playback follows the audio clock, so sound and picture stay together.

The ladder runs from a single pixel, which is the average of the frame, through 256 by 144 (the 8-bit rung) up to 7680 by 4320, and ends at vector, where the scene is drawn analytically with no pixel grid. While playing, the picture is drawn at 1920 by 1080 at most; when you pause, it is redrawn once at the rung you chose, if your device can make a canvas that large.

Your file is read in this page and nowhere else. It is not uploaded, and no audio is stored in this page.

The default demo signal is generated in this page from sine waves and noise. It is not a recording.

Not a MilkDrop-style shader engine: these are four drawing modes over the measured spectrum.
