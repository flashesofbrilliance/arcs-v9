What a right swipe fires

  1. Resume cut. resume-tailor against the JD into _ARTIFACTS/resume-<target>/: HTML, PDF, PNG, ATS text, tailor notes. Runs the moment you swipe right, in a live session watching the board. The cap only bounds how many run at once.
  2. Hiring-manager pull. The JD's reports-to line, then a LinkedIn people search on company and team. Labeled explicit, implicit, or unknown. Never a guessed name. Records the apply path too: ATS portal, a form on their site, a human's email, or a referral, as an operating note under the name.
  3. ATS resolve. One probe per company, cached. Sets the next-step template: Workday means re-key once and reuse, Ashby or Greenhouse means one upload.
  4. Forge stub. <company>.arcs.care reserved, not built.
Gate. The full counterpart forge (reverse-listen, audience model, persona gauntlet, hosted page) never fires on a swipe. It fires when a human on their side responds. Same rule as the held Kinexys cut.

What a finished forge looks like, live: blacksmith.arcs.care (the forge) · blacksmith.arcs.care/resume (the tailored resume it printed) · recruithaus.arcs.care (recruiter tier). Every cut derives from resume.arcs.care/resume.pdf.
