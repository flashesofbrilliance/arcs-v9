About Genius AI

Genius AI is building technology to help local businesses run themselves. Genius AI products do the admin work that keeps practitioners from the work they love and the people they serve. From GlossGenius to Reception, its products work 24/7 on busywork like booking, marketing, client management, finances, staff and inventory management, and more.

Genius AI technology has saved businesses 106M in admin hours, earned them $2.0B in additional revenue, and facilitated more than 624M client touch points.

About the Role

AI is at the heart of everything we build. More than 130,000 service businesses count on us to keep their day-to-day operations running, and our AI teammates, including Reception, are already doing meaningful work for them. Now we’re moving into new territory: reaching new markets, building smarter teammates, and finding better ways to help customers grow and stay with us. We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager to take on one of these opportunities and make it their own. You’ll join a deliberately small product team, where everyone takes on broad ownership, and AI shapes how we research, prototype, and ship. Decisions happen in days, not quarters. You won’t inherit a playbook, you’ll write one.

You will report to our Head of Core Product. You must be commutable to our NYC headquarters and will operate in an in-person environment. We default to being in-office 3-4 days per week with required attendance on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

What You’ll Do

 - Own end-to-end product execution from zero to one, taking new ideas from concept to launch with urgency and precision

 - Design and ship AI-native experiences, including agentic teammates that do real work for customers, and define what “agentic” means in your part of the product

 - Shape the roadmap with bold, well-reasoned bets grounded in market insight, customer needs, and business impact

 - Ship fast and correct fast: iterate on a weekly cadence, analyze results, and pivot based on what the data tells you

 - Go deep on the market, the competition, and customer pain points so Genius AI stays ahead as we move into new categories and customer segments

 - Align Design, Research, Engineering, Marketing, and Analytics around ambitious goals and keep them moving at high velocity

 - Build systems, not just features, using AI to automate research synthesis, analysis, and delivery so your impact scales beyond your own capacity

 - Tell the story of where we’re going by communicating strategy, priorities, and learnings clearly to everyone from execs to frontline teams

What We’re Looking For

 - 5+ years of product management experience, ideally at high-growth technology companies building multi-user or collaborative products, with a record of taking products from inception to launch

 - Shipped AI or ML-powered features or redesigned your team’s research and decision-making around AI tooling, with a clear point of view on what AI makes possible and proof that you’ve built something with it

 - Default to action: you set a direction, ship something, and iterate rather than waiting for perfect information

 - Strong product instincts, with the ability to understand user problems and business opportunities and define a vision for solving them

 - Fluency in technical and design conversations, and comfort working shoulder to shoulder with engineers and designers

 - An AI-forward profile, staying current on emerging tools and championing experimentation across your team

 - Genuine empathy for small business owners and a drive to understand their needs deeply
