I run the system, then I build the tool that runs it. That is the short version of why this role fits.

Here is the longer one. Most GTM business systems reqs are really a question about who owns the seams: between Customer Success and the CRM, between the data contract and what the automation actually fires, between what the process says and what the post-sales team actually does. I have spent two decades on those seams. At Grayscale I led go-to-market for ten SEC/FINRA-regulated products and owned the data contracts and field-mapping logic that kept systems telling the same story across product, engineering, legal, and compliance. At a three-business-unit nonprofit I built the enterprise data layer from nothing, CDP selection through Salesforce Lightning modernization, and net-new revenue moved twenty percent because the segmentation finally matched the lifecycle.

A few things I noticed that made me want to write.

Ramp is running $200B in annualized spend through a transaction layer that has to be right every time. The post-sales systems that sit on top of that, Customer Success, Account Management, Solutions, are where retention and expansion either compound or leak. The seam between Salesforce and the automation layer you describe is exactly where I work: requirements analysis, specifications, end-to-end implementation, and documentation that the next person can actually use. I have done that across regulated environments where a bad field-mapping decision has compliance consequences, which is a useful instinct to bring into a finance infrastructure company.

The quieter overlap: I built ARCS, an LLM-native decision pipeline on Claude with a live MCP server, and I use it the way a good operator uses any tool: to take the enrichment, scoring, and pipeline-visibility work that eats a team's week and make it run on its own. I am not applying as an AI engineer. I am applying as the operator who can bring that tooling into your Workato flows and Salesforce automation without waiting on a platform team.

On me: I do not just draw the process map. I am in the Salesforce admin panel, the data contract, the integration spec, and the compliance sign-off. SQL, Python, Snowflake, Segment, Salesforce. I have shipped zero to one under SEC/FINRA and HIPAA, which is the part of the job that usually gets underestimated.
