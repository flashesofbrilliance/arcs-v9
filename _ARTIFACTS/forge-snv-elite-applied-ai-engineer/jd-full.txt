## About the Role

We’re looking for a talented **Applied AI Engineer** to help shape how AI is transforming institutional finance. You’ll build production-grade AI-powered applications, infrastructure, and workflows used by sophisticated hedge funds and asset managers.

This is a highly hands-on **0→1 engineering role** where you’ll work across the full stack, partner directly with investment teams, and turn complex financial workflows into elegant, intelligent software.

You’ll have significant ownership and the opportunity to work with rapidly evolving AI technologies, including LLMs, agentic systems, MCP integrations, and modern AI development frameworks.
