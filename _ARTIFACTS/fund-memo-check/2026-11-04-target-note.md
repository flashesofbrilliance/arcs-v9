## Where it fits against the existing offer

The target list has one offer for everyone: the boxed AI Audit + First Slice. The memo trace is a candidate
**first slice** for a family office or fund, not a second pitch. It is narrow, it has a dry run built in, and it
ends in something the principal sees at the next committee meeting. Lead with the audit framing on a first call;
bring the memo trace out when they name an IC or board process.

Who actually buys it: a family office that **makes direct deals or commits to funds** and has a written IC memo
step. A multi-family office that mostly allocates to managers sees LPAs and side letters (fund documents, the
harder reader per the fit check). One that does direct deals sees term sheets and purchase agreements (closer to
the RFP shape the engine already reads). Ask which on the first call.
