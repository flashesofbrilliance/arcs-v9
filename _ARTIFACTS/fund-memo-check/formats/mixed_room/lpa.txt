LIMITED PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT (EXCERPT)
Issued: January 15, 2026 | Fictional sample document written for this test
================================================================
SECTION 3 - TERM (pp. 5-6)
================================================================
3.1 Term. The Partnership shall continue for ten years from the Final
Closing, subject to two one-year extensions with Advisory Committee consent.
3.2 Commitment Period. The Commitment Period ends on the fifth
anniversary of the Initial Closing, which occurred on March 1, 2026.
================================================================
SECTION 8 - FEES AND EXPENSES (pp. 41-44)
================================================================
8.1 Management Fee. During the Commitment Period the Partnership shall
pay the Manager an annual Management Fee equal to 2.0% of aggregate
Capital Commitments.
8.2 Fee Offset. 80% of Transaction Fees received by the Manager shall
offset the Management Fee.
8.3 Organizational Expenses. The Partnership shall bear Organizational
Expenses up to $1,500,000; any excess is borne by the Manager.
================================================================
SECTION 9 - DISTRIBUTIONS (pp. 45-48)
================================================================
9.1 Preferred Return. Limited Partners first receive a Preferred Return
of 8% per annum, compounded annually, on contributed capital.
9.2 Carried Interest. After the Preferred Return, the General Partner
receives 20% of profits, with a 100% catch-up.
