04Root cause · separatedA sufficient alone · B raised the price of a yes
A · req reality · out of Zach's control

There was no req. A recruiter cannot mint a VP function on a candidate's behalf.

  • Chris's post was, per CJ, a probe to "see what type of response he would get."
  • The forming role was a growth-operator exec. The pitched role was a new function: "A new function that doesn't exist yet."
  • Chris asked in writing for a Chase meeting, then went silent. The block was upstream of CJ.
  • Verdict: sufficient on its own. No execution change turns "JD being defined" into a hire inside a Talent screen.
B · execution and register · in Zach's control

"Cards hot" did not cause the no. It raised the price of a yes.

  • Wrong axis. CJ conceded the AI axis in minute eight ("that's incredible"). Every further demonstration on it bought nothing. The unconceded axis was the mandate.
  • Negotiating before there was a counterparty. Offers, band, process, passes. Each met with "not baked yet."
  • Self-authoring the req and the rubric for the person whose job is to write them.
  • Maximal ask, no scoped fallback. The Day-60 pilot was in the plan as a reward, never offered as the entry.
  • Stop-loss unexecuted. Warmth mis-scored as fit. Rule unbanked for 60 days.
