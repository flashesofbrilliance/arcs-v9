Altitude 2The map: capture, recipe, render+Which parts of raw photography are mechanism in ARCS, and which are only a picture.

The raw file is the receipt. It holds what the sensor saw, before anyone chose how to show it. The edit is a recipe: parameters, not pixels. A render is what you get when you apply a recipe to the capture.

Ad Astra

Promoted to canon. Fuel cost must not exceed demonstrated escape velocity.

This idea sits at the first stage. It has partial mechanical support and no adversarial review or second context.

  • Mechanism shown for the deterministic layer: stored raw intact, parsed columns match.
  • Loki challenge: not run. Someone should argue the brief prose layer is the baked image and the idea fails there.
  • Receipts in a second context: none. Needs a decision receipt from a different surface or domain.
  • Kill test 1: dry-run backfill. If the column cannot be rebuilt from the raw on every row, the raw is not the source.
  • Kill test 2: any rendered claim not traceable to a receipt field means one description is not enough.
