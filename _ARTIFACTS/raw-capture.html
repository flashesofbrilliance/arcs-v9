Altitude 2The map: capture, recipe, render+Which parts of raw photography are mechanism in ARCS, and which are only a picture.

The raw file is the receipt. It holds what the sensor saw, before anyone chose how to show it. The edit is a recipe: parameters, not pixels. A render is what you get when you apply a recipe to the capture.

Altitude 5The sparkplug: potential to kinetic+The one action the stored evidence is waiting on, and the gates that keep it from firing early.

Potential energy here is 197 receipts with an empty column and a proven way to fill it. Nothing changes until one write runs. A spark plug does not make the energy. It sets the gap and the moment. Close the gap too early and it misfires, and leave it open and the engine idles.

  • Tier 1: mechanical. Hard gates. Any failure stops here and nobody is asked anything.
  • Rebuildable: 197 of 197 empty rows, from the stored raw reply (dry run, read-only).
  • Rule reproduces the stored column on 73 of 74 filled rows.
  • Scope is exact: only rows where the column is empty, ids saved first.
  • Tier 2: judgment. A person fills it in; nothing here is scored by the machine. It opens only when tier 1 is green, and then asks for craft and aesthetic input, not facts.

Capture the snap

The sharpest event in this data is not a level, it is a change of slope. Count the filled column cumulatively across the 271 receipts. Flat at one for 169 receipts, then the slope jumps to one per receipt from receipt 199 on. Nobody recorded that moment. It was found afterward. The same shape marks every conversion: a gate flips, a state changes, a rate changes. Each one deserves a capture at the instant it happens: the before state, the after state, and what caused it. A mechanical detector can flag it, because it only needs the first difference of a series that was flat and is not.
