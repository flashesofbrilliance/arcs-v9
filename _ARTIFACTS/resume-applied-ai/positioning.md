## The one-line bet
A senior operator-builder who **turns AI capability into shipped product inside the customer's
weeds** — strongest where the stakes are high, the domain is regulated, and breadth
(product × engineering × GTM) is the edge, not a liability.

## The four load-bearing differentiators (rank per JD)
1. **AI-native builder, for real.** Ships LLM-native, agentic systems end to end — not demos.
   Evidence: ARCS, a decision-intelligence platform on Claude with a **custom MCP server, agentic
   pipelines, tool calling, and human-in-the-loop control by design**; a family of MIT-licensed
   OSS developer tools (good.arcs.care), each with its own conformance suite. Application/orchestration
   layer of LLMs, not model training.
2. **Forward-deployed operator.** Embeds with the customer, aligns eng · design · clinical · ops ·
   commercial. Evidence: Squat NY — sole product+tech partner on a seven-figure embedded engagement
   ($350M-ARR client), discovery → QA → handoff, on schedule.
3. **Regulated / high-stakes fluency.** Ships where getting it wrong is expensive. Evidence: Grayscale
   (Product Lead for the customer-facing surfaces, institutional and retail, on the team that launched
   $7B in digital assets to capital markets; SEC/FINRA; RBAC + policy-at-the-glass publishing controls);
   HIPAA digital therapeutics; Axon (gov). Twenty years turning the constraint into the product.
4. **Product × GTM breadth.** Decision → revenue in one head. Evidence: Grayscale +20% net-new revenue
   via data personalization; WTA first commercial data partnerships (Twitter/Facebook), $850K+ y1.

## Screen-in / screen-out (from the rubric)
- **IN:** build-and-embed role · AI-native surface (LLMs/agents core, not bolt-on) · senior IC/lead/
  staff/founding with decision latitude · sustainable culture · senior/staff comp · remote or NY-metro.
- **OUT (hard gates):** pure people-management with no build · "AI" as costume · junior/entry leveling ·
  996/grind · keep-the-lights-on maintenance · a domain he can't add real value to.

## Per-archetype emphasis knob
- **Forward Deployed Engineer / Deployment Strategist** → lead diff #2 (embed) + #1 (build); Squat NY headline.
- **Applied AI Engineer / Lead** → lead #1 (MCP/agents in production) + #3 (regulated); ARCS headline.
- **AI-Native Product Lead / Founding PM** → lead #1 + #4 (breadth is the product); ARCS + Grayscale.
- **AI Solutions / Deployment Architect** → lead #2 + #4 (customer-facing technical + cross-functional glue).
- **Platform / API / Agent-Ecosystem PM** (e.g. Amex MCP role) → lead #1 (MCP/agents) + developer-platform
  framing + #3 governance (RBAC/auth/responsible-AI). See `_ARTIFACTS/resume-amex-mcp/`.

## Honesty policy (inherited, non-negotiable)
Nothing appears on a public artifact unless verifiability is **documented** (public/inspectable URL,
AUM, cert, repo) or **self-reported** (subject's plausible figure). Impressive-but-false is a ruin
vector (background/reference checks). Defensibly-true beats impressive. Do not manufacture JD-specific
claims (e.g. don't assert OAuth/consent-management experience unless the real record supports it —
RBAC/authorization + Auth.js identity in ARCS is the honest mapping).
