Every company is building a context layer for its agents. We build the infrastructure it runs on: Omnigraph, our open-source graph database, and the engineering to put it to work inside customers&#x27; teams.

We keep the team small. Each person owns whole systems, from design to production, and agents handle the busywork, so time goes to the hard problems.
