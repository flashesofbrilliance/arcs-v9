Kroll AI Transformation

The AI Transformation team partners with practice leaders across Kroll to redesign end-to-end workflows and build AI-enabled tools that our professionals use every day.

Our Work Spans

Mapping and redesigning core service delivery processes

Embedding generative AI, automation, and analytics into workflows

Reducing cycle times and manual effort in repeatable activities

Standardizing data capture, knowledge reuse, and review frameworks

Building internal tools and assistive applications for practitioners

Supporting governance, risk, and control frameworks for AI usage

Driving change management and adoption across practices

We are outcome-driven: every initiative must tie to measurable impact on productivity, quality, risk, or revenue.
