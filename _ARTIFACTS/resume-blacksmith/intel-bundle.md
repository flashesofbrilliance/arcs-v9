## Air-gap boundary (the load-bearing rule)
The **persuasion craft** — reverse-listening, per-persona resonance, the recon *method* — stays in
this bundle and **never reaches a public surface**. The public Forge *names* these stages so the
machinery reads as rigorous, but their contents are offstage. Only the **honest-method** half
(gather, grade, red-team, render, the CI gates) is published. The one deliberate public exception is
the recruiter credit ("Amy at RecruitHaus"), attested by the candidate. Enforced mechanically: a
Playwright air-gap test fails the build if a forbidden token reaches the served HTML.

## Reuse
This whole bundle is a **template instance**. For the next company: re-run stage 1–4 of the blueprint
with new params (company, role, counterpart transcript, decision-makers), and this SSOT regenerates —
recoil seed, recon, gauntlet, critique — feeding a fresh public three-surface family. The intel bundle
is what makes the forge *fast the second time*: the shape is fixed, only the evidence changes.
