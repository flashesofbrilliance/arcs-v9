Hi Blacksmith team,

I build the tool, then I become its most demanding user. That is the short version of why this role fits.

Here is the longer one. I would be building Blacksmith's agent tooling for a developer I already am. I spend my days building ARCS, an LLM-native platform on Claude with a live MCP server, and I delegate to coding agents at scale to ship it in days, not months. When I read about Sandboxes and [code]smith, I did not read them as a candidate. I read them as the person who would use them. That is a rare thing a forward-deployed engineer can bring: I am inside the customer you are building for.

A few things I noticed that made me want to write.

Your moat is not the faster runner. It is that you own the bare metal, the co-located storage, and the utilization curve, and each layer pays for the next. The agent bet is coherent because an agent's inner loop is build, test, iterate, and you already run that loop faster and more faithfully than a generic sandbox. The question I would want to argue with you about is whether the win is the coding agent or being the execution substrate under everyone's agents. My instinct is the substrate. Your edge is infrastructure, not model IQ.

The part of your product I respect most is the one-line diff. Hiding that much hard engineering behind a change a developer makes in three seconds is a real design choice, and it is the kind I like to own: clean surface, hard interior.

There is also a quieter overlap. A CI system validates code before it merges. ARCS validates a decision before you commit to it: state the bet, keep the receipt, check whether it panned out. I built a CI for decisions, so I understand the inner-loop obsession from the inside. It is the same instinct as a pre-deployment check suite that enforces integrity before a change ships.

On me: I do not just write the deck. I am in the code reviews, sprint planning, and incident response. Framework-agnostic, TypeScript, React, Tailwind, Python, SQL, Node, Prisma. Army of one for the better part of a decade, punching at a heavier weight class. I have shipped zero-to-one under SEC/FINRA and HIPAA, which happens to describe a good slice of your customer base.

One note in the spirit of no surprises: I tore my ACL and meniscus and have surgery scheduled shortly, so I would need a little flexibility on the on-site start. Everything else, I am ready to move fast on.

The bundle linked here pulls the proof together, including the live commit trail and the open-source tools. Happy to go deep on any of it.

Zach Harris
zachharris.xyz · github.com/flashesofbrilliance
