# Blacksmith Founder Gauntlet — interview objection-handling prep

Internal prep. Persona-based gauntlet across the three founders, with steelmanned objections, sharp honest replies (say the concession first, then the strength), and an ATG reframe per objection. Air-gapped: never shown externally.

## The through-line (say this, honestly)
"I'm not a distributed-systems engineer today, and I won't pretend to be one in this room. What I am is a builder-who-ships with a verification reflex from regulated domains and a year of adversarial experience inside coding agents, which is the exact surface you're about to productize. Hire me for the ramp speed and the guardrail instinct, not for a Ceph résumé I don't have." Every answer below is a facet of that sentence. With these three, the concession IS the credibility.

## Top-3 landmines (ranked) + best defuse
1. **"Consultant/advisor, not an engineer who ships"** (Aayush's core doubt; JP's PM-who-codes worry rhymes). Category rejection, interview-ender. Defuse: get them into the ARCS git history early — "a running system I own end-to-end, solo, public commit trail; read it tonight, then tell me I'm an advisor." Concrete artifact beats résumé shape.
2. **"Agent-driven velocity is a corruption risk in infra"** (Aayush + Maru). Dangerous because it's correct on its face. Defuse (the flip): "the failure mode you fear in me is the product you're building; I've spent a year as the adversarial user of coding agents, living in where they lie. My velocity is guarded by mechanical gates; I refuse to merge on a plausible green." Concession first, then flip. Turns the biggest liability into the most specific qualification for THIS company.
3. **"Too corporate / not startup-native"** (JP tripped this on the ex-AWS candidate). Cultural, pre-conscious. Defuse: name it and separate the species — "the corporate worry is 'needs a big org to function'; I'm the inverse, I function best as the whole stack in one head with nobody to delegate the hard part to. Test me on what I built when it was only me."

## Aayush Shah (Co-CTO, ex-Cockroach allocator; the skeptic)
- **"You've never written systems code."** Concede the ramp is real (months). Push back on "only above the line": regulated finance forced reasoning about consistency, idempotency, replay, RBAC-as-correctness. "I close depth gaps fast and I don't fake understanding — the trait that keeps you from shipping a plausible-looking corruption bug." ATG: he's screening for bluff-vs-dig. Offer: "give me your gnarliest recent incident, let me reason through it out loud."
- **"Consultant who codes, not an engineer who ships."** Concede some years were influence/de-risk. Counter with ARCS as checkable running system (auth, Prisma migrations, MCP, env-fails-closed), solo, public trail. ATG: 20 years next to hard problems = failure-mode intuition a pure IC lacks (the Spartan-Award instinct). Only after conceding the pager point.
- **"'Power user of coding agents' is a liability in infra."** Right fear, held too. Practice = agent drafts, I own verification, mechanical wherever possible (grep, set-diff, schema check, migration dry-run); treat agent output as false until a test draws blood. ATG (strongest move): the thing he fears in you IS Blacksmith's next product — you've been the adversarial power-user of exactly that surface.

## JP Jayaprakash (CEO, ex-Faire; theory + product + business)
- **"Is ARCS a product or a personal tool? No users."** Concede: no external user base; "built a thing I love" ≠ "market pulled on it." Counter: 0-to-revenue rep is in the client/advisory work; ARCS proves I can take an ambiguous no-spec problem to a running opinionated system. ATG: he's screening for market humility — volunteer what you'd cut from ARCS if a real user showed up tomorrow ("the fact that I have a list is the answer").
- **"Where's the rigor under the polish?"** Concede: not algorithmic, no complexity result. The rigor is in the invariants (provenance can't be fabricated; a claim without a falsifier is propaganda; no rendering above the evidence). Systems-invariant model, not a theory-paper model. ATG: his home turf is delightful product built WITH design/product — the bilingual translation layer held by someone who can read the code is your best positive note with him.
- **"PM who codes, or engineer who owns product?"** "Engineer who owns product" — make it checkable: when there's a hard technical decision under the surface, do I make it or route it? In ARCS I make it (schema, auth, pipeline) because there's no one to route to. ATG: name the AWS-candidate wire and separate the species.

## Aditya Maru (Co-CTO, ex-Cockroach; the gate-builder — your strongest alignment)
- **"Velocity vs pre-deployment integrity?"** Converge, don't defend. From regulated finance: a change isn't done when it works, it's done when a mechanical check proves it (schema check, set-diff vs SSOT, migration dry-run, build-fails-closed on missing env). "Cheap gates get run; slow gates get skipped." ATG: his Google check suite is your favorite design pattern — "where do your Blacksmith surfaces not have that gate yet? A background coding agent shipping unreviewed changes is exactly the surface that needs a schema-integrity checkpoint." A day-one project conversation.
- **"Solo builder — can you lead/raise a bar?"** Concede: ARCS is solo, not leadership evidence; leadership rep is a layer back (mentoring, standing up practices in regulated teams). "The best way to lift a team's quality is to encode the standard so it enforces itself." ATG: the highest technical leadership is building the guardrail that makes everyone safe without you in the room — same as his check suite.
- **"Your domains aren't infra-as-a-product."** Concede: haven't built dev-infra-as-a-product; engineer-as-buyer is unforgiving. Counter: "I AM that buyer, right now, at intensity — I've made those switch decisions this year." ATG: a buyer who reads your source and benchmarks you is the buyer your build-in-public, refusal-to-overclaim posture is built for. "Grep-survivable or it's a pretty sentence."

- **JP:** prove a real model under the polish + market humility (volunteer ARCS cuts); land engineer-owns-product as checkable, not asserted.
- **Maru:** converge — his integrity gate is your favorite pattern; verification discipline and leadership-through-gates are the same story, and the truest thing you can say all day.
