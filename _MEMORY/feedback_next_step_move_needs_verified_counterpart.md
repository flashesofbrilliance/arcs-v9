The Room Playbook experiment (name one next step out loud: date, owner, action) is good advice with a verified counterpart and a gift to an attacker. In the one impersonation on record (2026-11-02), every "advance" signal (same-day CV request, a quick next meeting) was the hook, and the named next step was the attacker's ask. Zach's ex-ante hard lines caught it at the verification step; the counting pipeline saw nothing wrong.

**Why:** transcript counts are blind to bad faith; trust has to be its own stream (the precall brief's T2 gate).
**How to apply:** before the next-step move, confirm identity on the company's own channels. A fast, urgent ask from an unverified party is the signal to stop. Pairs with [[unverified-inbound-starts-at-bad-faith-reference-class]].
