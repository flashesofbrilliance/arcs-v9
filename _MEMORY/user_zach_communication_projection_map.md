## How Zach projects on calls (counted, not judged; ledger: tools/projection-map/ACCUMULATION.md)

**Solid (114 calls, stable across 9 months, no drift):**
- Holds the floor about two-thirds of the time everywhere, and asks 1.6 to 3.2x more questions than the other side. "Monologue" is the wrong label: high-volume talker AND asker.
- Floor share follows his authority: recruiters 56%, interviews 63%, peers 66%, clients 71%.
- Expert mode is plain mode: with clients he talks most and goes mid-curve least (0.2 per 10 turns vs 0.9 with peers).
- Code-switches under stakes: filler 14 per 1k words with peers, 5.5 in interviews. "We" doubles with clients.
- Goes deep in response to depth: before a mid-curve turn the listener's replies run about 1.6x their usual length. He answers engagement; he does not fill silence.
- Density alone does not cost him the room (998 long turns, null; the Grug count matches what blind readers perceive).
- Almost never checks in ("does that make sense"): 0.1 to 0.5 per 1k words.

**Home registers (two blind coders, kappa 0.69, 60 turns):** Sage, Shapeshifter, Heart make up about three-quarters of his long turns. Heart lands most with peers and recruiters; Shapeshifter with clients and interviews. No register reliably loses the room: misses are rare and spread out. The old "shadow Heart" read does not hold.

**Leaning (one stream, p near 0.05 to 0.10; promote only on calls after 2026-11-16):**
- More mid-curve in calls that went nowhere; the cost, if real, is in the end-of-call impression, not in-call cooling.
- Looser talk (more filler) went with calls that advanced, against prediction.
- In interviews, dense answers land better.
