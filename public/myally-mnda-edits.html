Mutual NDA: a few small edits

Thank you for sending the NDA, and for making it mutual. It is clear and fair. I would like to suggest six small edits before signing. A clean copy with the edits applied is at the bottom. Each one has a short reason: tap Why? to read it.

struck text is the current wordingshaded text is what I suggest

  1. Section 3.2

    Who each of us may share with

    Section 3.1 lets your team share my information with your advisors and counsel. This makes it two-way, so I can ask my own lawyer or accountant a question, and so the ordinary tools I work in (like the intake form you will use) are covered rather than technically off-limits.

    Original: Advisor will not share Company's Confidential Information with any employee, subcontractor or other third party without Company's prior written consent.

    Proposed: Advisor may share Company's Confidential Information with its own legal and financial advisors, and with service providers it uses for the Purpose (for example document storage, form and hosting tools), each bound by confidentiality at least as protective as this Agreement and used consistently with Section 4. Any other sharing needs Company's prior written consent.

  2. Section 8.1(c)

    When materials are returned or destroyed

    Sixty days runs out quickly once you add review time, counsel and holidays. Ninety gives the proposal room without either of us having to remember to extend it. Return or destruction on your request still applies at any time.

    Original: (c) 60 days after the Effective Date, unless the Parties have signed a services agreement or extended this period in writing.

    Proposed: (c) 90 days after the Effective Date, unless the Parties have signed a services agreement or extended this period in writing.

  3. Section 8.3

    Copies that cannot be cleanly erased

    I keep working files under version control, where history cannot always be erased line by line. This keeps any such copy fully covered by the agreement, the same way the clause already treats backups.

    Original: Copies held in automatic backups that cannot reasonably be deleted may be kept until overwritten in the ordinary course, and remain subject to this Agreement.

    Proposed: Copies held in automatic backups or in version-control history that cannot reasonably be deleted may be kept until overwritten or archived in the ordinary course, and remain subject to this Agreement.

  4. Section 6.2

    Derivative works

    Read literally, a proposal or analysis written about your spec is a derivative work, which the agreement exists to let me prepare. This carves out exactly that, and keeps it confidential.

    Original: The Receiving Party will not reverse engineer, decompile or create derivative works from the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

    Proposed: The Receiving Party will not reverse engineer, decompile or create derivative works from the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except materials prepared for the Purpose, such as proposals, analyses and notes, which remain subject to this Agreement.

  5. Section 6.4 (new)

    My own methods stay usable

    Some of my existing work sits near your space (for example strengths-based profiling and decision tools). Section 1.5(d) already allows independent development; this simply says it out loud so neither of us has to argue about it later. It does not loosen anything about your information.

    Original: (no existing text)

    Proposed: Nothing in this Agreement restricts Advisor's use of ARCS or of Advisor's pre-existing or independently developed systems, methods and concepts, including strengths-based and decision-support methods, provided Advisor does not use Company's Confidential Information in doing so.

  6. Section Recital B

    How my services are described

    I do not give legal or compliance advice; your counsel does. Dropping the word keeps the description accurate.

    Original: Advisor provides strategic, technical and compliance advisory services, fractional technology leadership and product development services...

    Proposed: Advisor provides strategic and technical advisory services, fractional technology leadership and product development services...

Jump to the clean copy

These are requests, not conditions. If your counsel prefers different wording that does the same job, that works for me. Everything else in the agreement I am happy to sign as written. I am not a lawyer, and nothing here is legal advice.
