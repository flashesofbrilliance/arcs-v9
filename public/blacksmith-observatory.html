GATE → RED-TEAMRendered pixels verified in CI: Playwright, zero horizontal overflow at four widths, and the PDF is reachable. Page-count is not a pass.

Not one gauntlet, several. The draft went through an honest-grade pass, an independent constitutional court (air-gap and overclaim), a founder-persona gauntlet, and a responsive sweep at 27 width combinations. Every hit is checked against the evidence; only true ones change a word. Subtractive, never puffed.
