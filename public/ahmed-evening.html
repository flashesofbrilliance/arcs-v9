The shared bet

Under both our companies is one wager: structured, portable context beats more compute. Your one activity table and my one receipt table are the same bet: that the bottleneck isn't model quality, it's context that can move. Everything below is that bet, broken into the pieces that would matter to your book of customers.

Course I

The Recoil 録

What you already saw on the report page: a conversation turned into a time-ordered model of what each side believed and how those beliefs moved as new information arrived. Every claim is anchored to a line someone actually said, no paraphrase, no invented certainty. When a read turns out wrong, the correction is kept, not smoothed over.

Run on any recorded call: a sales call, a renewal call, a churn-risk conversation. The output is the same shape every time, which is what makes it a pipeline and not a one-off analysis.

Course II

Canon Forge 鍛

One call is a data point. A relationship is a corpus. Canon Forge is the operator that takes everything known about an account, every call, every note, every deck, and forges it into one evidence-graded record: what's confirmed, what's inferred, what's still open, each with a source it traces back to.

For a customer of yours running a lot of AI-assisted sales or support motion, that's the difference between "we think this account is at risk" and a record that shows exactly which conversation, which line, and how confident that read actually is.

Course III

The Seed & the Serialized Recap 種

Every engagement compresses to one load-bearing sentence, the seed. The trick isn't finding it once; it's carrying it losslessly as the same material gets summarized for different audiences: the full internal record, a team-facing recap, an exec one-liner. Nothing that matters gets dropped along the way, and what can't be carried at a given altitude is logged, never silently cut.

That's parameterized: the same operator, pointed at a different account or a different call, produces the same shape of output on a schedule, not a bespoke write-up each time.
