Honest framing first

What this actually is

"Fan out every primitive" isn't a real operation on the material available — there's no hidden dataset to orchestrate, just three documents: two of your sites and two conversations. What's genuinely useful is the oldest trick in behavioral analysis: compare what someone says they want against what their choices actually optimized for, across enough time and enough surfaces that the pattern isn't noise. That's what follows.

The delta

Three places stated and revealed pull apart

1 · Health & Wellness is the revealed center of gravity, not the stated one

Largest delta

It's one line among five to seven on every stated list, but it's the sector with your longest tenure (Twill, full-time CBO), your current fractional role (Aescape), and your longest-running advisory relationship (Fisher Wallace Labs). The stated framing spreads attention across AI/Public Impact/Media; the revealed record concentrates in health.

2 · The Mar 20 conviction sectors haven't produced a case study yet

Forward-leaning, not yet revealed

Authenticity/provenance and fashion tech — named with real energy on the call — don't map onto any of the twelve named engagements across both sites. AI-and-displacement is closer, given the Twill-era material and the Apple tenure, but still isn't a labeled case study anywhere. This is the one delta that reads as genuine forward interest rather than an unresolved framing problem.

3 · The stated ask ("single ambitious mission") sits next to a revealed pattern of parallel fractional roles

Behavioral, not sector-based

scottwitt.cc states readiness to "commit to a single ambitious mission." The revealed record — Aescape (Fractional CBO) concurrent with advisory work at Fisher Wallace Labs, plus the mentorship/office-hours funnel on parlance.cc — shows a portfolio operating mode maintained even while the stated preference points toward single-mission focus.

The pattern

What actually pulls, versus what gets said

The revealed field points to health/wellness brand leadership at senior-operator altitude, run in parallel across two or three relationships at once — not the single, sector-diversified mission the stated language describes.synthesis, not a quote from you — flag if this reads wrong
