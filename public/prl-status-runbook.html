The whole system in one paragraph: a visitor chats on the site, the bot captures the lead, and it flows automatically into your CRM, deduplicated and enriched with job title and industry, routed to the right person, with follow-up reminders set. You can run all of it yourself, no one else needed to keep it moving. This page is the end to end tour plus the exact steps to flip it on for real.

The lead's journey, start to finish

No jargon version. Each step below is a real, working hop, proven in a copy of your system before anything touches the live site or CRM.

1
Visitor chats on the siteThe chat widget opens, greets them, and asks a short set of guided questions.
2
Lead is capturedName, contact info, and their answers are packaged up the moment the chat ends.
3
It flows into the automationThe captured lead is handed straight to the background automation that does the busywork for you.
4
Deduplicated and enrichedThe automation checks whether this person is already a record, then adds job title and industry so the lead arrives more complete than a typical web form.
5
Written to the CRM, tagged "Chatbot"A clean lead record is created or updated, always labeled with its source so you can always tell where it came from.
6
Routed by territoryThe lead is pointed at the person who owns that account or region.
7
Follow-up reminders fireA short-window nudge and a longer-window reminder are set automatically so a lead does not go cold unnoticed.
One more thing every lead carries: the ticket includes the full chat transcript plus a link to the relevant product or competitor spec sheet, so whoever picks it up has the full picture without asking the prospect to repeat themselves.
Go-live, three steps

Everything above has been proven in a sandbox copy of your CRM and site. Going live is three deliberate changes, each one small and reversible.

A
Point lead delivery at your live CRMSwap the destination from the sandbox copy to your production system. One setting, one place.
B
Add the "Chatbot" lead source in the live CRMA short, one-time setup step so live leads carry the same clean source tag the sandbox leads do.
C
Install the chat plugin on the live siteAdd the same widget, using the same credentials already proven in the sandbox, to the production site.
Before you flip it: prove it once with a single test lead through the live destinations, exactly the way the sandbox has already been proven. Then pick your own window to go live. Nothing here needs to happen on our clock.

How we know it works

We do not call anything done on a mechanical pass alone. Two levels, in order.

1
A step testSynthetic data proves the chain runs end to end. Necessary, not sufficient on its own.
2
A real testSubmit one real lead, then read the resulting CRM record directly, not the automation's own report of itself, and confirm: the name splits correctly, the state is a real code or is honestly flagged when it cannot be parsed (never a wrong but plausible value), the lead source tag is right, and a long company name is not silently cut.
✓
Duplicate-submission checkA double click on submit must not create two leads.
The bar for "done": one real, non-synthetic lead with its CRM record ID in hand, and a plain statement of what was tested versus what was assumed, independently read back before sign-off.
What the chatbot knows

Its knowledge base, and the deliberate limits we put around it.

✓
Guides and resourcesIt serves live links to your own site: the product selection guide, the polymer catalog, spec and data sheets.
✓
Competitor-grade recognitionIt recognizes named competitor grades (for example Lexan, Makrolon, Ultem, Noryl, Lustran, Valox, Zytel) so an inquiry reaches the right person faster. It never guesses a spec, a certification, or a price.
✓
Quick FAQ answersFor example, "do you have a data sheet or spec guide?"
A deliberate safety choice: only public material already on your website is used as its knowledge. Nothing proprietary, such as the internal color-match and product-development forms or the Good/Better/Best selection matrix, is ever uploaded, so nothing confidential sits inside the bot.
