A checkup on your AI build

Where is your AI build on its arc, where is it least efficient, which hypotheses are failing?

I'd answer by following two real decisions, one technical and one business, from what went in to how they turned out, before the next 20,000-bag MOQ rides on it.

Questions worth putting to your team

Useful whether or not I'm involved. Each comes from something you raised.

What you raised

Worth asking

Volume indicators can miss a rising signal; every day without a call costs time to market.
How often was your criteria's peaked-or-rising read right, and what would have flagged it wrong before the MOQ?
You've built partner-risk alarms by hand; analysis before signing is critical.
Before the next collab, what runs on the partner, and would it catch trouble early?
A missed simulation means bad data or a wrong hypothesis.
For the last miss, which was it, and can your setup tell next time?
No 20,000-bag MOQ without an early indicator.
Which flavor calls carry a number that says stop, agreed before the order?

If you want the checkup

I read your build through what it decides.

Each decision gets three marks: What it relied on, what it couldn't see, and when you'll know if it worked.

The two decisions

You pick. My suggestions:
Technical
How the build works, such as how the warehouse and MCP feed your criteria, or what stays on-prem. Followed from the data in to the answer out: Where it held, slowed or broke.
Business
A call the build informed, such as Filipino Adobo, a partner check before a collab, or the number that says stop before an MOQ.

What's included

Every deliverable, and the time it takes.
  1. 01Each decision written up with its three marks and the number that would tell you to stop, agreed up front. If one has played out, it sits beside the result.
  2. 02One page: Where the build stands, what's least efficient, which hypotheses aren't holding.
  3. 03A read against the six things you said matter, in your order: Accuracy, efficiency, speed, cost savings, on-prem, no data bloat.
  4. 04Next steps with dates, each with a number that shows it worked.
  5. 05Honest marks: What's unchecked, what's unsure, and your pushback, all on the page.
  6. 06A one-page field guide so your team can rerun the checks.

Two weeks. About 3.5 hours of your team's time, a little over 4 with the optional session.
