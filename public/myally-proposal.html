Step 1: the audit

Two weeks, fixed at $8,400. The goal is simple: find what must be true at the foundation before each later step can safely start, and in what order.

How it works

  • Looking out: what Alyce must serve. Your parent journeys and user stories, what Alyce must and must never say, the questions for your counsel, and the dates that set the clock. No interviews with families; made-up data only until launch.
  • Looking in: what exists today. The spec, the code, how data is stored, the cost model, and whether the Ages+ stub shares Alyce's code.
  • Bringing it together: a short list of open questions, answered with you in one joint session.

Conversations, all inside the fee

  • 45 minutes each with Chelsea and Kelly.
  • 45 minutes with Leah on what Alyce must never say to a family, and the legal questions that matter most to her. Her answers become page one of Alyce's guardrails.
  • A 60-minute joint session in week 2, and a 30-minute readout at the end.

What you receive

  • A list of first steps every later phase depends on, each with what "done" looks like. I own every technical step.
  • For each area of the app: keep, adjust or rebuild, with the reason.
  • A roadmap sketch, a short findings report, and a map of how each family's data is kept separate.
  • A recommendation on the AI provider.
  • Which case we are in (below) and a firm quote for the build.

During the audit I test with made-up accounts only and never download or copy documents. If I see real family data, I stop and tell Chelsea the same day. At the end my access is removed and my notes are deleted, confirmed in writing.

Steps 2 to 5: the build

Foundation plus a first Alyce slice
The first steps from the audit: who can read which records, where keys live, a separate test copy, each family's data kept apart. Then a thin Alyce you can click: one parent, one child, a Vault document, a safe answer.
Alyce core
The AI connected behind one swappable layer, so changing provider later is not a rebuild, and an Ages+ Alyce can plug in. Replies short, plain and strengths-first, checked for reading load and read-aloud.
Guardrails
Three lists written with your counsel: always, only with a check, never. Tested against fixed sample questions before every release: no diagnosis, no medical or legal advice, crisis handling, tone. You get a record of what was tested. It is not a certification.
Launch
Full desktop and mobile accessibility pass, deploy, and a runbook any developer can follow.

Data, ownership and the law

I'm not a lawyer, and none of this is legal advice. Your counsel decides; I bring them short, specific questions.

  • Children's data: each family's data is kept strictly apart, with parent and child permissions. Made-up data only until launch.
  • Questions for counsel: does HIPAA apply, and what must Supabase and the AI provider sign? Do children ever use the app directly (COPPA)? What must Alyce say about being an AI under Connecticut law? Is a data protection assessment needed before launch?
  • Ownership: everything I make for MyAlly is yours. Methods I had before stay mine, and you get a free, permanent licence to any of them inside your app.
  • AI tools: the tools I use on your materials are named here, set so they never train on your data.
  • Calls: I record or transcribe only if you say yes at the start of that call.
  • Liability and insurance are set in the services agreement with your counsel.
  • On our first call I mentioned a $2 million figure from memory. Please disregard it; it is a question for your counsel.
