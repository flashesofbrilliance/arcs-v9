Same discipline in public: dated predictions with the falsifier written first, then scored and corrected in the open, including the calls scored down and left on the record (the calibration receipts).
  • Human-in-the-loop by design: the system scaffolds judgment without overruling it: evidence-gating and structured decision scoring as the quality layer, never an autonomous verdict.
