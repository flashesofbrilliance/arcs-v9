02
Assessment · what it's doing on its own terms

scottwitt.cc is a person; parlance.cc is a firm

Read alone, scottwitt.cc succeeds at something parlance.cc doesn't attempt: it makes you feel like you know Scott before you've talked to him. The Enneagram typing, the denim and camera list, the live-updating "things on" section — these aren't padding, they're doing real trust-building work fast, at the cost of looking unfinished (a May 2026 status note still live in August signals the personal pages update on a slower cadence than the professional ones).

The ask is narrower and more current here than on parlance.cc

Notable

parlance.cc's homepage presents seven sectors at equal weight with no ranked ask. scottwitt.cc states a specific, dated ask: venture studio, PE portfolio work, or brand+creative lead at an early-stage AI company in four named verticals (consumer, health & longevity, media, creator economy). That's the sharper of the two — and it doesn't fully match either the seven-sector Parlance homepage or the three-sector thesis from the Mar 20 call.

03
Evaluation · judged against the standard the sites set for themselves

Where each site fails its own stated promise

scottwitt.cc: "Creative Capitalist" has no capital evidence attached

Internal contradiction

The framing claims creativity is deployed to "de-risk the enterprise" and cites "raised capital" and "created billions in enterprise value" — but none of the seven /work/* case studies visibly show a number, a round size, or an outcome metric as scanned. The claim is capital-flavored; the evidence is craft-flavored.

parlance.cc: "Narrative Clarity" has the same evidentiary gap

Same failure mode, different site

Flagged in the prior teardown: nine case studies indexed by category and role only, no narrative. This is the identical pattern showing up on both properties — the pitch is proof-by-assertion, the case studies are proof-by-omission.

Overlap and divergence in the project lists

Structural

Constellation, Twill, Fisher Wallace Labs, Naming, and Aescape appear on both sites. The Marque Longevity Lab and SPKR. appear only on scottwitt.cc. WelcomePAC, Reporter, UpTrade, Pedal, and Cars & Bids appear only on parlance.cc's case-study index. Neither site cross-links to the other's project list, so a visitor to one has no way of knowing the fuller record exists on the other.

The serialization surfaces one finding neither teardown alone would: you're running two credibility engines that don't reinforce each other. parlance.cc sells the studio and its method; scottwitt.cc sells you as a person and collector of taste.
