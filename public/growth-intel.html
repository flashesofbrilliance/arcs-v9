02 / 08 · The problem
Every drop generates learning.
Most of it dies.
It lives in someone's head, a Slack thread, or a post-launch debrief nobody reads before the next one. At the speed Fanatics is growing, that's not a process gap — it's a compounding tax on every decision the growth org makes.
03 / 08 · The gap
Without
Each BU runs its own playbook in isolation. What Instant Rips learns doesn't reach Trading Cards before they test the same thing.
Post-launch knowledge lives in individuals. A new hire on week one inherits nothing structured.
Decisions that were coin-flips last quarter are coin-flips again this quarter.
With
Every campaign produces a structured record — what was predicted, what happened, why. Cross-referenced across BUs.
A new Instant Rips hire sees every relevant precedent in 20 minutes. Institutional memory outlasts headcount.
The org gets smarter faster than it grows. Speed and rigor compound instead of trading off.
04 / 08 · The function
Owns
The institutional memory layer for the growth org. Pre-decision framing, post-decision capture, cross-BU pattern recognition.
Builds
AI-assisted workflows that turn operational data into structured decisions — instruments that sharpen what happens next, not dashboards that summarize what already happened.
Delivers
A compounding advantage: the faster the growth org moves, the more learning it captures, the sharper each subsequent decision gets.
Is not
BI. Analytics. A COE. A process layer that slows GMs down. The GMs run their businesses — this makes the learning from those businesses work harder.
