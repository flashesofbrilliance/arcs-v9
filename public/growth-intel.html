Pitch Deck · Zach Harris
Growth Intelligence
A forward-deployed function that turns every drop, live event, and campaign into compounding organizational learning — instead of a one-time spike and a forgotten post-mortem.
PREPARED FOR FANATICS COLLECTIBLES · JUL 2026
The problem worth solving
Every drop at Instant Rips generates learning. Right now, most of that learning dies. It lives in someone's head, a Slack thread, or a post-launch debrief that nobody reads before the next one. At the speed Fanatics is growing, that's not a process gap — it's a compounding tax on every decision the growth org makes.
Current state vs. with this function
Without
Each BU runs its own playbook in isolation. What Trading Cards learns about price sensitivity doesn't reach Live Commerce before they test the same thing. What Instant Rips learned from the last three drops isn't structured in a way that Chase can hand to a new hire in week one. Learning compounds inside individuals, not inside the org.
With
Every campaign, drop, and live event produces a structured record: what was predicted, what happened, and why. Those records cross-reference across BUs. A new Instant Rips hire can see every relevant precedent in 20 minutes. A decision that was a coin-flip last quarter is a data-backed call this quarter. The org gets smarter faster than the headcount grows.
What the function actually is
Owns
The institutional memory layer for the growth org. Pre-decision framing, post-decision capture, cross-BU pattern recognition. Not a reporting layer — a decision-support layer.
Builds
Lightweight AI-assisted workflows that turn operational data into structured decisions — not dashboards that summarize what already happened, but instruments that sharpen what happens next.
Delivers
A compounding advantage: the faster the growth org moves, the more learning it captures, and the sharper each subsequent decision gets. Speed and rigor compound instead of trading off against each other.
Is not
A BI function. Not analytics. Not a COE that sets policy. Not a layer of process that slows GMs down. The GMs run their businesses — this function makes the learning from those businesses work harder.
90-day arc
01
Discovery · Days 1–30
Map the actual decision anatomy at Instant Rips. Where does learning die? What decisions get made without it? What's the highest-leverage place to start capturing?
02
First Brief · Days 31–60
Produce the first structured audit brief on a real Instant Rips campaign or drop. Show what captured learning looks like in practice — not as a concept, as a tool Chase and his team actually use.
03
Propagate · Days 61–90
Cross-reference Instant Rips findings with at least one adjacent BU. Surface the first cross-domain pattern. Start building the case for what this looks like at the org level.
