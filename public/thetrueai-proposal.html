The content strategy sprint you asked for.

On our call on July 16 you asked for a written proposal. Here it is. It is short. The terms are up front, and the first piece of work is a content strategy sprint aimed straight at your goal.

TERMS FIRST · I CHARGE FOR MY TIME, NOT ON RESULTS OR REVENUE SHARE · A FIXED FIRST PIECE OF WORK · ← BACK TO THE PRE-READ

How I work

Terms first

I charge for my time. No results-based fee, no revenue share, no equity. You buy a set number of hours. I use them as I work, and I do not go past the cap without asking you first.

$175 to $200 /hr
A rough range. The rate comes down for larger commitments. We can start as small as about 10 hours and size the rest to your budget.

The goal, in your words

Cohort 1

20 paying customers by August, from people who do not already know you. The way to get there is you, in front of your audience. Your Amazon Alexa background is what makes them stop and listen.

Why content comes first

You cannot market well until you know exactly who you are talking to and what you stand for. Writing content forces both of those decisions. So this is not one task among many. It is the one that unlocks the rest. The sprint gives you those decisions and a working system to run after.

What you get. A working system, not a memo.

  • One clear customer. We pick the single person you are selling to, and write down why it is them.
  • Your positioning, and 3 to 5 phrases you can own. The exact terms and questions you want to be the answer to.
  • A content system you can run. The recurring topics, a posting rhythm, and the first few posts actually written, not just planned.
  • A way to measure interest. A simple screening step so the right people apply, and tracking so you can watch progress toward the 20.

Timebox

5 weeks, up to the August cohort. A fixed piece of work, not an open tab.

After

We only keep going if it is working. We agree up front on what working looks like, and on a clean way to stop if it is not.

Next step

Your move

Tell me your budget and how much time you can give it. That is all I need to set the hours and start. Two fixes from the pre-read, the security headers and the Apply-button wording, are worth doing on their own, with or without me.
