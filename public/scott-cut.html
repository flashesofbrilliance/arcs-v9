LEGEND
▼
The plain meaning of every instrument term used above.

Stated preference

What someone says they want, on the record: a sector list on a site, an ask on a call, a bio line.

Revealed preference

What someone's actual choices optimized for over time: which engagements got the years, the titles, and the repeat structure.

Kairos

A peak moment in a conversation: the highest-information stretch, when someone is generating rather than reacting.

Carrier signal

The mutual momentum of a conversation. High when both people are building on each other; lost when one side goes long and the other goes short and polite.

Reverse listening

Reading a call end to start: take the actual close, then work backward to see where the real signal formed versus where it seemed to be heading.

Serialization

Joining separate artifacts (calls, sites, decks) into one cross-referenced record so patterns show up that no single piece contains.

Scar Rule

The record keeps failures and cracks visible on equal footing with wins, recorded when they opened, not smoothed over afterward.

Falsifier

The specific observation that would prove a belief wrong. The load-bearing reads here carry theirs in section 08.

Earned fidelity

Rendering claims at the resolution the evidence deserves: verified reads sharp, untested reads degraded, open questions grainy.

The hold

The calendar block I promised on Friday: where the thirty-minute working session lives if you want it. Moving it or killing it costs nothing.
