        02 · THE CONSERVATION LAW

        What no translation may drop

        LOSSLESS = THESE SURVIVE
        THE FALSIFIER

        Every payload carries what would prove it wrong. A rendering that cannot fail is flattery, not translation — the O1 distinction ("this one is built to disagree") must survive into every register.

        THE KILL / SCALE LINE

        The pre-agreed exit survives every altitude: month-one stop-loss, Thu-noon kill line, the day-30 read. Worst case travels first in every register where money is spoken.

        THE GRADE

        Evidence class is conserved even when the chip is invisible. Fidelity dials how it shows, never whether it holds — "per Jeben" stays "per Jeben" in every voice.

        THE ATTRIBUTION

        Provenance is load-bearing: the fifteenth rock is Jeben's image, credited in every register that uses it. Mango is Justin's idea, and competes fairly in every telling.

        THE SCORE-BACK

        Every translated claim keeps its date with the record — Fri 2:00 PM, day 30, day 90. A rendering with no score-back date has quietly become a pitch.

        THE FIREWALL RECEIPT

        Whatever a register may not carry is withheld and logged in the loss report. Governed redaction, visible to the operator — the strike stays on the page.

        05 · THE LOAD REGISTER

        Sizzle vs. axiom — the propaganda differential

        OPERATOR-DEFINED · JUL 25 2026

        The grade register (A–X) grades sources. This second axis grades what the words are doing. Every phrase in a rendering is either bearing evidentiary load or decorating something that does. The law: sizzle is legal only while an axiom carries the weight and the falsifier stays attached. The moment sizzle bears load, the rendering has bent.

        BandDefinitionLouisville specimen
        AXAxiomatic — survives cross-examination stripped of all styling. A/B-grade fact or labeled-E number."Built ~100%, shipped with shells, rebuilt" · the $20K/mo quote · median $628, seed 42
        SZSizzle — circumstantial flair; does no evidentiary work; legitimate while anchored to an AX and honestly framed."The fifteenth rock" · "gold seam" · "four clocks striking" · "insurance-shaped"
        BENTBent narrative / propaganda — sizzle doing load-bearing work. Aimed at others: propaganda. Aimed at yourself: magical thinking."Would have caught Pumps" (unfalsifiable counterfactual — re-cut by the gauntlet) · leading with the 8× best case · comps-as-proof at the wrong scale

        PHILOSOPHIZED THINKING (operator canon, Jul 25) — the inverse of magical thinking: the same imaginative energy, running the opposite direction. Magical thinking lets the mockup's sizzle stand in for validation; philosophized thinking lets the axiom lead, welds the label on every assumption, attaches the falsifier, and only then permits the flourish. The test is one move: strip every SZ phrase and read what remains. If the claim still stands, the rendering is philosophized. If it collapses, it was propaganda wearing style — and it gets re-cut, the way the gauntlet re-cut "would have caught it" into "a $2–5K test was available and unpurchased."
