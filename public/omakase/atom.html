Chapter 1 · 種 · The atom

A coordinate, frozen in time

Not a report and not a row in a form. A receipt is a decision's state vector: what is being bet, what would prove it wrong, where it dies or scales, and when it gets scored. Sealed before the outcome, so it cannot be quietly rewritten later.

It is meaningful standalone. Hand someone one receipt and they know the whole decision. That is the atomic test.
