What you are looking at

This is the instrument, run on my own tape. Everything scored on this tab is a read of my own performance, not yours. That is deliberate. The method crosses freely, the subject stays me. You get the whole machine, pointed at myself.

The idea is simple: a conversation is a pair of waves. When two people are on the same wavelength the signal builds, when they are not it fades. ARCS listens to a recorded call and maps that, beat by beat, into four states.

The four states it hears

In sync

You finish each other’s thoughts, the idea builds. Confidence grows.

Human

Humor and human warmth. The thing that carries a call even when the content is thin.

Useful pushback

Someone pushes back and it sharpens the other person. Disagreement that adds something, not noise.

Out of sync

Paragraphs in, one word out. The place a briefing is supposed to catch before it happens.

Same four states, run on any call, before it to prep and after it to score. The beats on The Read are those states, drawn on our real conversation.

The checks that keep a read honest

Reading energy off a transcript is easy to fake, so the read has to survive a few evals before it ships. These cross the fence. The recipe underneath does not.

  • It is traceable. Every beat traces to a real line in the transcript. If it is an inference, it is labelled one.
  • It keeps the why. The read keeps the why of a moment, not just what was said.
  • It meets you at your level. It checks the register each person was speaking at, and flags where one went over the other.
  • It admits mistakes. When the first read was wrong it says so, in writing. On The Read, the honest not-now and the PARTIAL and MISS scores are the instrument refusing to flatter me.

The pre-meeting briefing, made real

Run forward instead of backward, the same instrument becomes a briefing. Before a call: here is the person, here is the wavelength to meet them on, here is the altitude to open at. After: here is where it actually landed, and the honest diff against what you expected.

For a strategy service to founders and leaders, that is a read across a client’s calls: which moments land, where a conversation quietly falls out of phase, which relationships are trending toward trust and which are coasting. It reads recordings that already exist. Nothing to wire up to start.
