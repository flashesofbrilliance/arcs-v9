LEGEND
▼
The plain meaning of every industry term used above.

Opportunity cost

The dollar value of the return you give up by leaving cash idle instead of in a higher-yielding place. Priced live against the Mercury Treasury rate, it becomes a one-tap approved sweep.

Runway

How many months of cash a company has left at its current burn rate. Here, computed under scenarios (burn, hiring, AR timing) as a first-class Command output.

Mercury Treasury

Mercury's product that automatically sweeps idle operating cash into money-market and government funds at a stated yield (~4.47%). The execution side of parking cash, and where the engine monetizes.

Sweep

Automatically moving idle cash from checking into a higher-yielding account. Treasury already auto-sweeps; the pitch adds the pricing that says when a sweep is worth it.

AP (accounts payable)

The money a company owes suppliers. An AP calendar schedules those payments; the spend-timing engine weighs early-pay discounts against the cost to runway.

MCP

Model Context Protocol: the open standard Command uses to expose Mercury actions to AI clients. Under a read-only posture, the analyst recommends without moving money on its own.

Broker-dealer (BD)

A firm registered to transact in securities. Yield claims about swept cash must be framed to survive a partner-bank or broker-dealer compliance model.

Agentic

Driven by AI systems that take actions autonomously on a user's behalf, rather than only answering when asked. Used here for agent-driven finance tooling like Command and Ramp's agents.
