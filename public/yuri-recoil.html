The six steps it runs

This is the whole machine, the peek under the kimono. Steps 3, 4 and 6 are filled in from our actual call, further down.

1
Pre-mortem. Assume the meeting already went badly. List the ways it could, before it happens.
2
Pre-meeting brief. Orient on who you are meeting and the terrain you are walking into.
3
Ex-ante receipt. File a falsifiable bet: what you expect, your pre-committed move, and the belief most likely to be wrong.shown

Step 6, filled in · the quiet signals
