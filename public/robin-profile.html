03 · what lands, what loses you

The resonance map.

▲ LANDS
  • One narrow use case, named as a wedge (“we do X for Y”), not a platform that does everything
  • A felt, personal-pain story, ideally his own (LinkedIn can’t match me; I didn’t want to be just the CMO)
  • “Movement / category” framing over feature or mechanism framing
  • Concrete market proof (Gloat, Eightfold, Beamery, Gartner numbers), he lives in this category
  • Being asked a sharpening question, then given a crisp answer (“is it a platform or an algorithm?”, answer it in one line)
▼ LOSES YOU
  • Physics / entropy / “coherence engine” abstraction with no product on the other end
  • Everything-platform sprawl across marketing + hiring + ad-tech + M&A in one breath
  • An ask to be an internal champion before there is a single, dead-simple thing to champion
  • Long monologue / screen-share with little back-and-forth (he is a dialogue thinker)
  • Jargon that cannot be retold in a sentence to a cross-functional stakeholder

05 · the quiet signals

What the surface does not say.

Anyone can read your two registers. These are the deeper tells, computed from the pattern across your own words. This is the engine deriving meaning, not amplifying signal.

Asymmetric altitude flex
You climb, but only after the ground is nailed.

You move between heights fluently, but the flex runs one way. You ascend from a landed idea up to meaning; you never open at the sky and descend into it.

soLand one concrete thing first, then follow you up. Leading with the sky is exactly how Call 2 lost you.

Humor as a leading indicator
The jokes are a conviction tell, not filler.

When you riff and get playful, conviction is rising. The banter is the carrier wave, not a break from the signal. When the humor drops, the thread already has.

soTrack the play, not just the words. More humor means the idea landed.

The gift and return loop
You give the coaching before you are asked.

You handed me the strategy unprompted: pick one, nail it. A gift given is one you expect to see returned, sharpened. You coach, then you check whether it was heard.

soName your own gift back to you, sharper. That closes the loop you opened.

The focus and epic self-tension
You coach focus and crave scope in the same breath.

Pick one, nail it lives right next to Endless Enthusiast, Seeking The Epic. You gave me the narrowing gift, then lit up most at the movement-scale framing.

soGive you the narrow wedge and the epic it ladders into. One without the other misses half of you.

Bimodal conviction
Your conviction is a switch, not a dial.

Go all in or don’t go at all. You are either fully in or politely out, with little middle. Call 2’s collapse to one-word replies was the switch flipping, not a gradual cooling.

soRead the mode, not the temperature. A warm-but-flat exit is the off position, not a maybe.
