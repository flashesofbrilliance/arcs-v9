## 03 What's included

¶12. Every deliverable, and the time it takes.

1. Both decisions traced in writing: Relies on, can't see, date you'll know. A call that has played out sits beside the result.
2. One page: Where the build stands, what's least efficient, which hypotheses aren't holding.
3. Read against your six: Accuracy, efficiency, speed, cost, on-prem, one source of truth without bloat or tech debt.
4. Next steps, each with a success number and a check date.
5. Honest marks: What's unchecked, what's unsure, and your pushback, all on the page.
6. A stop number per decision, agreed up front: The figure and date that would call it wrong.
