Full briefing, for reference

The two problems, separated

1. Email notification. Forms sent their "someone submitted" email from an old vendor domain, notify@websitesforanything.com, which fails authentication and gets quarantined by Barracuda. Jorge unblocked one message and allow-listed that sender on Aug 14, a narrow, one-off fix.

2. Sugar delivery. Separately, and more fundamentally: Gravity Forms was never wired to create Sugar leads at all, no Zap, no native connector. Even with email fully fixed, nothing writes to the CRM. This is the actual finding behind "broken" above.

The proposed fix

Stop routing through GF-to-email-to-human entirely. Use Sugar's own Web-to-Lead form, an HTML snippet Sugar generates that posts directly to the Sugar API. No email, no SMTP, no Barracuda in the loop. The handful of real leads already tagged lead_source: "Web Site" in Sugar today arrived exactly this way, so it's not new or unproven, it's the thing that's already quietly working.

What it actually requires

UsGenerate the Web-to-Lead snippet from Sugar (Studio), map its fields to match the six required fields the chatbot delivery already targets.
Site accessAdd the snippet to the site template, replacing or running beside the current form. Likely Josh (Websites For Anything), since it's page-template editing, not Jorge's usual IT/DNS lane.
SequenceSandbox-test the snippet before it touches the live site, same discipline as the CRM routing work, then verify a real staging submission lands correctly.

What Jorge and Josh know today

Only the Barracuda ask from Aug 14. Neither has heard about the Web-to-Lead approach. That's the gap this page exists to close before anything goes to either of them.
