Intel

Amy is a genuinely warm advocate operating on a marketplace she does not own. Read the warmth as relationship equity, not as fit leverage.

  • Amy : warm, high-rapport, personally invested ('you have great experience, Zach'). Turned 50 this year, two kids engaged - personal rapport is real and worth maintaining.
  • The channel : Paraform is an algorithmic marketplace. Amy is newer to it, learning the companies; she explicitly does NOT have deep hiring-manager relationships to trade on here.
  • Throughput mechanics : 2 submissions at a time until hiring-manager feedback returns, then 2 more. Not a high-volume funnel - some reqs have only 1-2 candidates in pipeline against 4 open roles.
  • Blacksmith : already submitted (staff/senior product engineer), came back 'did not move forward.' The Paraform record still shows it open; Amy will ask why on her Wednesday call.
  • Geography : reqs cluster SF / NY, some DC/Baltimore/TX/Canada. Remote is not a filter Amy can pull (city-only); she must open each role to check. Zach is NY-workable, SF no.

Questions & landmines

The call's job was to establish who-holds-the-ball at each step and stop wasting tailoring effort on pedigree-mismatched reqs.

  • What can each side actually see/do in Paraform? · resolved live via screen-share - Zach can self-search + express interest; Amy submits.
  • Which reqs are real fits vs pedigree-locked? · Amy: hard-skill focused, but 'they're definitely out there' - needs Zach's own eyeball + stack-rank.
  • Landmine: mistaking Amy's warmth for hiring-manager leverage. The screens are pedigree/hard-skill driven; Blacksmith proves it.
  • Landmine: the 'catch-all keyword resume' Amy wants can silently de-level Zach to IC-only - must add keyword surface WITHOUT dropping leadership scope.
  • Landmine: burning a scarce 2-at-a-time slot on a Blacksmith-shaped SWE req instead of an applied-AI/FDE/product-lead fit.
