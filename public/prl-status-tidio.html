(b) Acceptance test / sign-off

What Todd (or a sales manager) runs and signs

Six hands-on tests on the safe copy, wired to the sandbox. Every test has to pass before anything goes live; a failure means we fix it on the safe copy and re-run just that test, not a re-do of the whole thing.

1.1 is accepted when all five are true:

  • 1A test lead is received in SugarCRM, complete.Down each path, arrives with first/last name, company, phone, email, and state, tagged "Chatbot," routed to the right rep.
  • 2You hear about it immediately.Rep alert lands within seconds; the prospect gets an instant acknowledgment.
  • 3The assistant never states a spec, certification, or price.It routes to a specialist and the official data sheet instead, and still captures the lead.
  • 4Nothing on the live site or team routine has changed.All testing on the safe copy; production untouched until go-live.
  • 5Todd has personally checked "received" and signs.That sign-off is what makes 1.1 done.
