Honest resumes.

Tailored to the role, built from your real evidence, with nothing you couldn't defend in the interview. It runs in Claude.ai or Claude Code today; the plugin makes Claude Code run the full method every time.

How it works

  1. Read. The posting, plus your LinkedIn export as the source of truth for titles, dates and certs. Old resumes are only your own say-so.
  2. Grade. Every claim gets its evidence and a checkability grade, in one record every draft is built from.
  3. Stress-test. Flag what you couldn't defend: the overclaim, the borrowed jargon, the NDA line. The flags become your prep.
  4. Order. Lead with what the role wants; experience newest first.
  5. Build. A print-clean PDF, a matching cover letter and a plain ATS copy, then a check of the rendered pages.
  6. Red-team. A mock hiring panel picks it apart. Only the hits your evidence confirms change a word.

Try it, no install

Attach the job posting (URL or text) and your LinkedIn export (.zip or profile PDF). Repos, a portfolio or real numbers help; mention any NDA in one line.

Tailor my resume and a cover letter for this role: [paste the job-posting URL]

My LinkedIn export is attached: [drag in the .zip or profile PDF]

Keep it honest: flag anything I couldn't defend in an interview,
keep it ATS-safe, and don't state what the evidence doesn't support.
If I have an NDA, ask before including anything specific.

You get a resume PDF, a cover letter, an ATS copy and a defend-or-drop list for prep.

Install the plugin (Claude Code, optional)

/plugin marketplace add flashesofbrilliance/arcs-skills
/plugin install resume-tailor@arcs

git clone https://github.com/flashesofbrilliance/resume-tailor-skill
cp -r resume-tailor-skill .claude/skills/resume-tailor

Three grades, enforced on every line

Documented

Public and checkable: a repo, a commit count, a live link, a credential. Said plainly.

Self-reported

Your number. Stated as yours, never past what your profile already shows.

Unverifiable

It can frame the work; it never becomes a fact with a number. Flagged for you to defend.

If something's off

  • /plugin not recognized: update Claude Code, then retry.
  • Marketplace not found: check the name flashesofbrilliance/arcs-skills and that you're online.
  • It didn't trigger: describe the job and attach your export, or say "use resume-tailor".
  • On Claude.ai: the plugin is Claude Code only. Use the prompt above; the same rules apply.

Not proven yet

  • No published measurement of interview or offer outcomes.
  • Grades are assigned by the model from your evidence; nobody independently verifies them.
  • The ATS copy is plain text; it is not tested against named ATS products.
