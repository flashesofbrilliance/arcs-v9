The Wedge

Receipts for agent spend

A hypothesis, not a pitch.

A regulated stablecoin can be frozen, but not charged back, and nothing records what the user told the agent. Policy engines log which rule allowed a send, not what the agent expected or whether it came true. That gap is the wedge.

First user, and why it is not built

Treasury or compliance leads at fintechs piloting agent payments. ARCS has the receipt and the one-way-door check, but no payment rails or design partner. Your stealth agentic payments company may already own this; I would ask first.

Kill it if

Kill rule, written before any calls.

Fewer than 3 of 15 teams piloting agent payments say the approval trail is what keeps them from going live.

Commit or Walk

I commit when an idea passes the kill criteria I wrote in week one and a named design partner agrees to pilot. If nothing survives by month six, you get the written kills. At your roughly 1 in 30, a written walk is data too.
