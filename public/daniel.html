02
Gap analysis · L1–L2

Seven gaps, ranked by what they cost him

1 · The site is invisible to search and AI — for a company that sells demand generation

Structural

100% client-side-rendered React. The HTML served to crawlers contains only "You need to enable JavaScript to run this app." The meta description is still the create-react-app default: Web site created using create-react-app. Title tag: just "Fractional." Zero structured data, zero OG tags. Every studio partner, LP, or founder who Googles him — or asks ChatGPT/Perplexity "who is Fractional.city" — gets nothing. A GTM engine whose own site generates no inbound signal is a walking counter-testimonial. This is the highest-leverage fix on the list and it's a weekend of work (prerender, or migrate to Next/Astro).

2 · Signup writes email + password directly to Firestore

Security

The bundle's login/signup path stores {email, password, createdAt} as a Firestore document rather than using Firebase Auth's credential handling. If that collection's rules are permissive (common with this pattern), client credentials are readable. Relay this privately — it's a genuine favor, and favors compound trust.

3 · No proof, despite having spectacular proof

Credibility

On the call he casually dropped a case study most agencies would kill for: touched 50% of the FreighTech market, discovered buyers recoil from the word "AI," rewrote the messaging, and interest flipped. None of that is on the site. Porsche/JB Hunt/Walton-family adjacency (via Up.Labs) appears only as naked logos. "We find truth faster than anyone" is asserted, never shown — and his exact buyer (studio partners deploying LP capital) is the most proof-demanding audience there is.

4 · Positioned as a product, rendered as a brochure

Coherence

"Not a services agency — infrastructure." But there's no screenshot, no dashboard, no sample buyer-signal report, no pricing, no onboarding shape ("up and running in 24–48 hours" — said on the call, absent from the site). The site's form contradicts its central claim. Buyers resolve that contradiction against him: it reads as a solo consultant with a landing page.

5 · The literal product is playbooks — and there's no lead magnet

Conversion

He licenses playbooks and signal reports, yet the only conversion path is a cold "book a strategy call." One redacted monthly buyer-signal report or one-page playbook behind an email gate would let the product sell itself and build the list he currently doesn't have. Single-CTA sites make every visitor a binary: book-or-bounce.

6 · The founder is the asset, and he's not on the site

Narrative

Oregon Trail ancestry, born in Alaska, dad built oil-and-gas supply in post-USSR Russia, twenty months bootstrapping a family through Costa Rica, escaped sales by inventing a repeatable first-customer machine. "Frontier truth-finding" is a brand, not small talk — and it's exactly the temperament studios want pointed at unproven markets. The About page is three sentences of category language instead.

7 · Residue and hygiene

Polish

Favicon hotlinked from a Squarespace CDN (the old site's ghost). Default theme-color. No sitemap/robots. The .city TLD is defensible but unclaimed-namespace confusion is real — worth owning fractional.city as a metaphor (see below) rather than apologizing for it.
