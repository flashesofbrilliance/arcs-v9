The short version

What this is, in your language

It turns a conversation into a time-ordered model of what each side believed and how those beliefs moved as new information arrived. Same shape as your activity schema, pointed at decisions instead of rows. Expected versus actual, joined on time.

Where our customers overlap

You put it well on the call: the customers who care about revenue and have real data problems are also the ones making expensive, hard-to-reverse decisions. That is exactly who this is for. Your data may be the less interesting part. Your customers' need is the interesting part, and it is a strong fit.

What it does

The capabilities, at a high level

Enough to see the shape. The specific model and operators are held back, so this is what it does, not how it is tuned.

  • Tracks each claim as a credence that updates over the call, plus how settled that estimate is. It can tell a belief that has locked in from one still in motion.
  • It pressure-tests its own reads before trusting them. Each conclusion gets red-teamed and pre-mortemed against itself, and only what survives the attack gets reported. The number you see is the one that already held up under fire.
  • It gets sharper from being wrong. A read that turns out false is the highest-information event in the log, so the system keeps the correction instead of smoothing it over.
  • Resolution builds as the log grows. More calls make it sharper, the same way more activities make your table more answerable.

A worked example

Two claims tracked across our call. One of them is a belief of mine that collapsed, which is the honest part. The engine caught the correction instead of hiding it.

"our customers overlap"  .60 → .90 "media pitch is his focus"  .70 → .25

The cyan line is a prior I walked in with, that your media proposal was the thing you wanted to work on. It held flat, then dropped hard when you said it was a side project and Polyform was the main event. That one correction carried most of the update, and the claim closed low and settled. The green line is the overlap between us, which started as a guess and rose to a confident read on your own framing.

Why being wrong is the point

The value is not that the model was right. When I was wrong, it caught the correction and kept it, instead of quietly editing it out. That is the same property your one table has: every activity you append makes it more answerable, not more brittle. The honest diff is worth more than a flattering summary.
