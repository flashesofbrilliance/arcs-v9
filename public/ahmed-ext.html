What it does

The capabilities, at a high level

Enough to see the shape. The specific model and operators are held back, so this is what it does, not how it is tuned.

  • Tracks each claim as a live probability that updates over the call, plus how settled that estimate is. It can tell a belief that has locked in from one still in motion.
  • It pressure-tests its own reads before trusting them. Each conclusion is red-teamed and pre-mortemed against itself. Only what survives the attack gets reported, so the number you see already held up under fire.
  • Resolution builds as the log grows. More calls make it sharper, the same way more activities make your table more answerable.

The signal under the summary

  • Context not content, one layer apart. You standardize the context AI acts on. I standardize the decision a human commits to. You said it yourself on the call: what I do for a person is what you do for the data.
