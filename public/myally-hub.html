How the buckets and the weekly rhythm work

Three hour buckets: each is spent in 1-week sprints of 20 hours ($3,000), prepaid one week at a time; stop after any week and keep what is built, and unused hours are never billed. Every number is a first guess that firms up with how much the audit can see: the more of your code, spec and data setup I can review and reuse, the closer we land to Lite.

How we stay in touch: email or text, or Slack if you set one up. Anything that changes scope, hours or dates goes in one email thread that Kelly approves. Each week has four beats. Pre-work, by Friday: you add comments, priorities and any decision needed to the thread. Monday: a 5-minute written sprint plan; reply ok or swap one item. Wednesday: a three-line check-in, with a 15-minute call only if something is blocked. Thursday: a 30-minute demo and wrap. Then a Friday note with hours used and decisions, so nothing agreed in a quick message gets lost. About 45 minutes of your time a week.
