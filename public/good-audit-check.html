Could a stranger explain your last 30 commits?

Paste an open-source GitHub repo. This page reads its recent commit history in your browser and scores how much of it carries its own reason: a message that says why, or a link to the issue that does. It is a quick sample of the full Agent Receipts Audit, and it costs nothing to run.

Open-source repos only. Nothing is stored; the request goes from your browser straight to GitHub.

    What this sample cannot see

    • It reads commit messages only. The full audit also reads diffs, artifacts and receipts, and has a cold reader try to rebuild the work and cite every claim.
    • A good message is not proof the work happened as described. Checking that is what the cold reconstruction is for.
    • It spots agent commits by their trailers (such as Co-Authored-By) and bot authors. Agents that leave no trailer count as human here.
    • It scores the record, never the people who wrote it.
