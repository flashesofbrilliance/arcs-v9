05 · where it goes

The map sharpens with every read.

A note-taker hands you a flat transcript at the same focal length every time. This is the opposite bet: a terrain that gains resolution with each conversation and each cut. A few places it goes, once there are enough reads to correlate against. Forward-looking, not built yet, but this is the direction.

Overlay the outcomes

Put closed-won and closed-lost on the same timescale as these reads. The leading and lagging indicators of account health and deal jeopardy start to surface, the ones a flat note-taker never catches because it is not looking for topography, only transcribing words.

The world is not flat

It has a terrain of ridges, fog, and weak layers. Each read and each cut sharpens it. The focal length does not stay fixed. It earns more resolution the more honest reads you feed it, so hindsight stops being the only place clarity lives.

A record versus a leading indicator

Most intel tools give you a faithful record of what was said. The compounding value is a map of the account across calls: where conviction is climbing, where a deal is quietly falling out of sync before anyone names it.
