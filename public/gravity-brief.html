How I Would Use ARCS in the Seat

An alpha engine

Built: the record and the recheck. Not shown yet: that it beats judgment alone.

Much of a studio's edge hides in what it passed on. ARCS records why each hatch was passed and checks it again as the market moves, so a pass that was wrong becomes a lead instead of a loss.

A risk-adjusted pipeline

Built: a conviction score on every decision. Not shown yet: how accurate it is (my proof page marks it not yet).

Each hatch carries what we expect, how sure we are, and what would kill it, so the pipeline reads by risk as well as by excitement.

An antifragile test bench

Built: pre-registered tests, run once, failures published (my October benchmark).

Every kill test is written down before it runs, so a failed test still pays: the next thesis starts from what it ruled out.

One Secret About Financial Services

Regulated firms keep the record of what they decided because the rules require it. Almost none keep what they expected to happen, so they cannot score their own judgment.
