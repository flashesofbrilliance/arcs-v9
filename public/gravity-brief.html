The agent-payments wedge

A stablecoin send has no chargeback, and issuers freeze on legal process, not on a customer's complaint. Spend rules record what the agent was allowed to do. Nothing records what it expected or whether that came true. That second half is the wedge.

Crypto theses pick a side. I name which signal each side rests on, and what would settle it.
