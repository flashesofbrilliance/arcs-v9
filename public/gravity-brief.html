Three theses I would test first

Hypotheses, not pitches. Each has the result that would kill it.

  • Collateral that is checked, not assumed. As borrowed positions grow, the collateral held at qualified custodians is not re-verified often enough, and clients find out when positions are forced closed. A product that proves collateral on a short, fixed cadence could sell to custodians and their institutional clients. Kill it if fewer than 3 of 15 custodian or prime-broker risk leads say the current cadence is a problem they would pay to fix.
  • A decision record for institutional crypto moves. When a bank or custodian approves a new asset, chain or counterparty, the reasoning lives in meetings and email. A record of what was expected, who signed off and what happened later serves audit and change control. Kill it if compliance leads say their existing audit and change-control tools already cover it.
  • Policy-aware screening for banks running their own custody. Platform screening works inside Anchorage or Fireblocks; a bank building its own custody has to combine vendor data with its own policies. Kill it if a company already in the portfolio covers this ground. I would ask before spending a week on it.

How I would spend the first 90 days

A proposal to test with the partners, not a plan to impose.

  • Weeks 1 to 2: agree with the partners on the studio's thesis, the ideas already in play, and written kill criteria for each idea I take on.
  • Weeks 3 to 6: about 20 conversations with possible design partners (custodians, bank digital-asset teams, prime brokers). Each idea gets a keep or kill call at week 6, in writing, against the criteria from week 1.
