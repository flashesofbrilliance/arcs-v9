The Hatch Desk

Most funds call it a deal desk. I would run it as an AI-native platform team that compounds: each memo, its kill criteria, the record of every call.

Every pass is logged with its reasons and rechecked as markets move. A wrong pass surfaces before you miss the next one like it. The call stays yours, and comes faster.

It sits on top of your Idea Engine: the agents find and draft, the desk keeps the record. Nothing gets built twice.
