Questions

What is the Lore series?

Lore is a series of the stories ARCS is built on. Each one is filed with a verdict: true, a durable myth, an old wives' tale, or undecided. An undecided story may be told but not cited, and each entry names the test that would decide it.

Why file a story with a verdict?

Because a story repeated often enough starts to feel like evidence. Filing it with a verdict and a falsifier keeps the telling honest: the story stays on the page, and the verdict changes when the test runs.
