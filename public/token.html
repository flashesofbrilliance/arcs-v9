The architectural precedent
2002 · THE BEZOS API MANDATE

Bezos mandated that every Amazon team expose its data and functionality through a service interface. All inter-team communication goes through those interfaces. No exceptions. The side effect of the mandate wasn't the mandate — it was AWS. The internal platform became the most valuable external product in history because the architecture forced composability between teams that had previously been silos.

The Fanatics version of this isn't a technical mandate. It's an intelligence interface. Each BU has growth signal trapped inside — post-mortems no one reads before the next campaign, tribal knowledge that walks out when a GM leaves, drop learnings that never reach Trading Cards. The intelligence operator creates the interface layer: consistent decision taxonomy, structured pre/post-capture, composable signal that any BU node can call.
