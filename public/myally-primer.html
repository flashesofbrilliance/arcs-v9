Technical primer

A legend for the technical words you will hear. Each one gets a plain meaning, a picture to hold, and why it matters to you. If a word is missing, ask, and I will add it.

The building blocks

Front end
The part of the app people see and tap. Picture: The shop window and the shop floor. Why it matters: Yours already works; the open questions are behind the window.
Back end
The part people do not see, where information is stored and rules are applied. Picture: The stockroom and the office. Why it matters: Where private information lives, so it is where most care is needed.
Database
An organised place where the app keeps its information. Picture: A very tidy filing cabinet. Why it matters: Supabase plays this role for you.
Lovable and Supabase
The two services your app is built and stored on. Picture: Your workshop and your storeroom. Why it matters: Each is a separate company with its own rules for what you may put in it.
AI model
The program that reads a question and writes an answer. Picture: A very well-read assistant who has never met your family. Why it matters: Alice is built on one. Which one is a decision, not a given.
Prompt
The written instructions that tell the AI how to behave. Picture: A briefing note. Why it matters: Alice's voice and her limits live here.
Vendor, provider
A company whose service you rely on. Picture: A supplier. Why it matters: Every supplier brings terms and a bill, and switching one can be easy or painful. Used in this plan: The four stages, My view on Alice.
API
A doorway one program uses to ask another for something. Picture: A shop's order window. Why it matters: Alice reaches the AI model through one.
Lock-in
When leaving a supplier is costly. Picture: A phone contract with a penalty. Why it matters: I would build so you can switch AI providers without rebuilding.

How Alice works

Token
A small chunk of text the AI reads or writes. Usage is billed by the chunk. Also called the AI usage meter. Picture: A taxi meter that counts words instead of miles. Why it matters: Longer documents and more conversations mean a bigger bill. Used in this plan: The cost, in three buckets.
Usage cap
A ceiling on how much AI use is allowed. Picture: A prepaid card with a limit. Why it matters: Stops a surprise bill. Used in this plan: The four stages.
Retrieval
Alice looking things up in the vault before answering. Picture: A librarian fetching the right folder first. Why it matters: It is why uploaded documents affect cost and privacy.
Hallucination
When an AI states something wrong in a confident voice. Picture: A fluent speaker who has not checked the facts. Why it matters: Why I test Alice and why every document gets fact-checked, mine included.
Model version, "freezing" it
Locking Alice to a specific version of the AI so it does not change behaviour under you. Picture: Printing a form and not editing it mid-year. Why it matters: Keeps results steady and lets you check them.
Redaction
Hiding private details before something is used or shared. Picture: A black marker over names. Why it matters: Automatic hiding misses things, so I would pair it with a parent review step.

Keeping the build safe

Version control, branch
A record of every change to the app, with a safe side-copy to try changes before they go live. Also called change tracking. Picture: Track changes in a document, plus a draft copy. Why it matters: You can see who changed what and undo a mistake. Used in this plan: The four stages, What is in scope, and what the audit could change.
Environment (testing, live)
Separate copies of the app for practising and for real use. Picture: A rehearsal stage and the real stage. Why it matters: Practice never touches real families' information.
Data placement
Deciding which kind of information may be kept in which service. Picture: Valuables in the safe, receipts in the drawer. Why it matters: Services differ in what they are cleared to hold; your lawyers decide.
Evidence trail
A record showing a safeguard is in place and when it was checked. Picture: Receipts for every safeguard. Why it matters: Answers "how do you know?" if anyone asks. Used in this plan: What is in scope, and what the audit could change.
Backup and restore test
A saved copy, and proof that it can be brought back. Picture: A fire drill, not just a fire exit. Why it matters: A backup nobody has tested is a hope.
Re-platforming
Moving the app or its information to a different service. Picture: Moving shop. Why it matters: Only if a service turns out not to be the right home.

Who can see what

Role-based access
Different people see different things based on who they are. Picture: Keys that open only some doors. Why it matters: Parents, children and practitioners need different views. Used in this plan: The four stages.
Audit log
A diary of who did what and when. Picture: A visitor book at the door. Why it matters: Helps with trust, questions and problems.
Retention
How long information is kept. Picture: How long a shop keeps receipts. Why it matters: Kept too long can be a risk; ask your lawyers what is right.
Synthetic data
Made-up information that looks real. Picture: A rehearsal with actors. Why it matters: I test with this, never with real families. Used in this plan: What this means for each of you, My view on Alice.
Second check at login
A second proof that it is really you. Picture: A code sent to your phone. Why it matters: A basic protection for every account.

The legal alphabet (for your lawyers, not for me)

HIPAA
United States federal privacy rules for certain health information held by certain organisations. Why it matters: Whether it applies to My Ally LD is for your lawyers.
COPPA
United States federal rules about collecting information from children online. Why it matters: Same: your lawyers decide how it applies.
BAA
A contract between an organisation that handles health information and a service company that handles it for them. Why it matters: Some AI providers offer one; whether you need one is for your lawyers.
Accessibility standard
A published set of rules for making a product usable by people with disabilities. Why it matters: You choose which one to follow, with advice.

Working together

NDA
A signed agreement that keeps what you share confidential. Picture: A sealed envelope. Why it matters: Nothing sensitive moves until it is signed. Used in this plan: What I need from you, and what you can expect from me, What happens next.
Cost model
Your estimate of what Alice will cost per family, for light, typical and heavy use. Picture: A budget with scenarios. Why it matters: I will check it against how Alice will really work, not replace it. Used in this plan: The four stages, My view on Alice, The cost, in three buckets, What I need from you, and what you can expect from me, What happens next.
Scope
What is in the work, and what is out. Picture: The edges of a campaign brief. Why it matters: Anything outside it is a separate conversation. Used in this plan: What is in scope, and what the audit could change.
Sprint
A block of work with a fixed length and something to check at the end. Picture: One episode of a series: a set length, and something delivered. Why it matters: Each stage ends with something you can check.
Roadmap
The plan in time order. Picture: A route on a map with stops. Why it matters: Stage 2 builds it with you. Used in this plan: The four stages.
Dependency
One thing that must finish before another can start. Picture: You cannot frost a cake that is not baked. Why it matters: A campaign calendar has them too.
Backlog
The to-do list, in priority order. Picture: The next items in a shopping basket. Why it matters: You decide the order.
Definition of done
A written checklist for what "finished" means. Picture: The brief a campaign is judged against. Why it matters: Ends arguments about whether something is finished.
First release, thin path
The smallest version that works from start to finish. Picture: One complete route before the whole map. Why it matters: Smaller, safer, earlier.
Trial run, spike
A small time-limited experiment. Picture: A pilot batch. Why it matters: Learn the cost before committing.
Handoff pack
Everything I made, in your hands. Picture: The binder you take if you leave the table. Why it matters: Means you lose nothing if I stop.

Money

Unit economics
What each family costs you versus what each brings in. Picture: The cost and revenue of one customer. Why it matters: I will use your model's figures, not mine.
Fixed, grows with use, hidden costs
Costs that stay the same, costs that rise with families, and costs nobody listed. Picture: Rent, ingredients, and the broken oven. Why it matters: I list all three.
Best, likely, worst case
Three versions of a number, with the cases defined before anyone fills it in. Picture: A forecast with a range. Why it matters: Keeps wishful thinking out of the numbers.

Decisions and honesty

Decision record
One page saying what was decided, why, and what would change your minds. Picture: The minutes of a meeting that people actually reread. Why it matters: Memory fades; the page does not.
"Imagine it failed" exercise
Picture the project a year from now having failed, and write the story. Picture: A reverse planning session. Why it matters: Surfaces risks while they are cheap to fix. Used in this plan: The four stages.
Looking back
Comparing what you and I set out to do with what happened. Also called a look-back. Picture: A quarterly review. Why it matters: Turns every stage into a lesson. Used in this plan: The four stages.
Checkpoint
A test set in advance that says whether reality matches the plan. Picture: A milestone in a campaign. Why it matters: Keeps everyone honest without blame. Used in this plan: Exec overview, What this means for each of you, How we will handle feedback.
Predict first, check later
Writing down what I expect before work starts. Picture: A forecast filed before the quarter. Why it matters: Lets me see if my judgement is improving.
Fact-check
Verifying each factual claim against the code or a trusted source. Picture: A copy editor with a red pen. Why it matters: Applies to every document, mine included.
Shared documents versus shared understanding
Having the same papers does not mean having the same picture. Picture: Two teams reading one budget two ways. Why it matters: The reason this engagement exists.
