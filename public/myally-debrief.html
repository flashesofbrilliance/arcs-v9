What will make families come back3 ideas

Less friction at the moment of need

  • One tap to save a good moment, not a long form.
  • Start from the parent's own words: "Tell me what's been hardest lately."
  • One clear next step, in plain language.

Value that builds over time

  • A strengths record that grows: wins, coach notes and good moments, ready for every meeting. Each entry makes the next meeting easier.
  • Meeting history: what was agreed and what's still open, so each meeting starts from the last.
  • Memory the parent controls: seeing and editing what Alyce knows builds trust.

The right timing, then quiet

  • Deadlines surfaced months ahead, not at the last minute.
  • One gentle nudge, then Alyce waits. Your spec already rules out streaks and badges, and it's right.
