Always · sometimes · never

The operating constitution for the conversation and, if it lands, for the partnership.

Always

  • Lead every answer with the number, then the story, under 60 seconds.
  • Use their units: usable hours, cost per usable hour, quality rate, task diversity.
  • Name your gaps before he does: customs, entity setup, market pay design.
  • Turn each gap into a day-30 deliverable with a number attached.
  • Frame process as instrumentation, never as governance.
  • Agree the operating rhythm: what you decide, what he decides, how often you meet.

Sometimes

  • Mention Sama’s mission. Only when it explains margin or retention.
  • Name Fortune 50 clients. Only if asked about scale, and only public ones.
  • Go deep on the annotation platform. Only if he pulls the thread.
  • Discuss equity and bonus mechanics. After the second conversation, not the first.
  • Offer Turkey or Pakistan launch ideas. Only after hearing why those two.

Never

  • Call their business annotation, labeling, or data services.
  • Claim hardware logistics or entity-setup experience you do not have.
  • Say a Starpilot number you did not hear from them.
  • Explain leaving Sama in more than one sentence.
  • Hedge on travel and in-person. Decide before the call.
  • Tell him what is or is not hard in his own business. Ask instead.
