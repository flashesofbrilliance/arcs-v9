Recoil — Why This Exists
The first draft of this idea was to stamp artifacts with a CLI that didn't exist. That is provenance theater: the first technical viewer who runs the command turns a transparency claim into a credibility hole. ADR-032 chose the inversion — build the smallest real generator first, then the stamp is free and honest.
