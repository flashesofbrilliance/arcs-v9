02 · the instrument

What this listens for.

Method, briefly

  • Reverse listening — read the call end to start; the question is always where the real signal actually formed, working backward from the close.
  • Deep recall — a slower second pass for integration after the reverse read.
  • Kairos lock — the single highest-information moment per call gets write-locked; it is not editable after minting, only falsifiable at the next call.
  • Carrier-loss lock — the clearest moment resolution dropped, held on equal footing with the Kairos moment. Peaks and drops are both signal.
  • Corpus size, stated plainly — 2 calls, confirmed as full Granola history. This instrument does not pretend more data exists than does.
