The problem worth solving
Every drop at Instant Rips generates learning: what converted, what didn't, what the fan signal looked like before and after, where the funnel leaked. Right now, most of that learning dies. It lives in someone's memory, a Slack thread, a post-mortem deck that nobody reads before the next drop. The next campaign starts slightly less informed than it could.
At the growth pace Chris described — doubling roughly every 4 months — that's a compounding problem. Each drop is a one-time experiment instead of a building block. The team gets faster at execution and stays roughly the same speed at judgment.
The function I'm proposing is the infrastructure that makes each drop make the next one better. Not a new analyst. Not another dashboard. A decision memory layer that converts campaign-level learning into org-level clarity.
What this looks like inside Instant Rips specifically
Current state
Pre-campaign: gut feel + whatever was written down from the last comparable drop. Post-campaign: metrics report, a few takeaways, filed and forgotten. Repeat.
With this function
Pre-campaign: a structured brief anchored to what worked, what failed, and why — scored for confidence, not just reported. Post-campaign: variance analysis that closes the loop. The next team that runs a similar drop starts with your data, not from scratch.
