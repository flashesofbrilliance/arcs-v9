LEGEND
▼
The plain meaning of every industry term used above.

Synthetic identity

A fabricated identity stitched together from real and fake data (often a real SSN paired with a made-up name and date of birth) to pass as a new applicant. The core fraud SentiLink was built to catch.

eCBSV

Electronic Consent Based Social Security Number Verification: an SSA service that confirms whether a name, date of birth, and SSN match Social Security records. SentiLink was first to go live on it.

FedRAMP

Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program: the standardized U.S. government process for authorizing a cloud service as secure enough for federal agency use.

ATO

Authority to Operate: the formal sign-off a federal agency gives before a system may run on or handle government data.

Improper payments

Government payments that should not have been made or were made in the wrong amount, including benefits paid out on fraudulent or ineligible enrollments. Tracked in the hundreds of billions per year.

KYA

Know Your Agent: verifying the identity and authority of an AI agent acting on a person's or business's behalf in a transaction. The commerce-side identity race Socure and Prove are chasing.

Agentic

Driven by AI systems that take actions autonomously on a user's behalf, rather than only answering when asked. Used here for both agent-driven fraud and agent-driven commerce.

HIPAA

The U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act: the federal law governing the privacy and security of protected health information.
