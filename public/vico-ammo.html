The deeper case

What the live demo showed, and the two things worth naming directly

For Joe, on the Product Engineer, Front-End seat.
The one-line version

The strongest evidence here isn't a resume line, it's what Joe already watched happen live: a working AI product, built and shipped solo, doing the same underlying job (score a decision's confidence, then check it against reality) that this role exists to do for their customers.

What Joe actually saw on the call

1

A live scoring system: an algorithm that screens incoming opportunities against weighted criteria, scores them, and reweights itself based on which outcomes were right or wrong. Same shape as a calibration/scoring-rule system, just pointed at a different problem.

2

A working automation pipeline: a tool that takes real evidence and produces a tailored, verified output, end to end, not a mockup.

3

A public status log: a live site tracking real ship velocity, day by day, since the project started. Not a claim about moving fast, a record of it.

The one thing worth naming directly

The JD calls for someone with production experience running vector databases and RAG search at scale. The honest answer: the design for that layer is real and already thought through correctly (a standard embedding-plus-similarity-search architecture), and a working local version of the embedding piece is built and running. What isn't built yet is the persistent search layer connecting the two. That's a real, nameable gap, not a hidden one, and it's the kind of gap that closes fast once there's a real system and a real team to build it inside.

On experience level: if the read anywhere is "we want someone earlier-career," the honest answer is that seniority here means moving faster with less oversight needed, not more process. Worth testing directly rather than assumed either way.

A better way to test it than a resume line

Rather than take the resume's word for the AI-native claim, watch the same live demo Joe already saw: a real, running product, not a rehearsed pitch. That's a faster, more honest signal than another round of describing it.

None of this is a reason not to talk. It's the reason to talk about the right things. Happy to go deeper on any of it, on a call or here.
