The ambition

Where you said you want this to go

Today: a manufacturer with a brochure site, paid landing pages that can't be measured, and buyers who have to already know what they want and pick up the phone.
──────────▶
Your stated end-state: an AI agent trained on PRL's product-development history that can take "I'm molding X for application Y" and recommend a formulation — a digital version of your best formulator, working the website 24/7. That's not a chatbot. That's a moat. The chatbot is step one of building it.

Nobody else in specialty resins at your scale is doing this. The PD form data you've accumulated is proprietary — a competitor can copy your website in a week, but they can't copy twenty years of formulation decisions. The roadmap tab sequences how to get there without betting everything on the endgame up front.

Instrument-by-instrument

Nine findings. Each one has a fix, an effort, and a payoff.

Ordered by severity, not by sequence — the Roadmap tab handles sequencing.
Critical1 · GA4 is dark — you're spending on PPC with no attribution

You're paying for traffic to Unbounce landers and can't see what happens after the click. At $100M revenue, even a small paid budget deserves a working read on cost-per-lead. Right now the answer to "is PPC working?" is a shrug.

Fix
Rebuild GA4 property + GTM container from scratch (faster than auditing the stale one)
Effort
1 session
Unlocks
Cost-per-lead by channel; baseline for everything else
Critical2 · No web-to-CRM path — every lead must already know to call

The website generates zero structured leads. A buyer who finds prlresins.com at 9pm has no way to start a conversation — and in a 6–12 month sales cycle, the vendor who captures the inquiry first usually keeps it. This is the gap the chatbot closes.

Fix
Tidio chatbot → SugarCRM lead via REST API, routed straight to the sales manager
Effort
3 sessions (agreed scope)
Unlocks
24/7 lead capture; the "wow" demo; Phase 5 foundation
Major3 · CS sits in the lead path as an accidental gatekeeper

Three CS reps in Farmington field inbound and relay to sales. They're good at service — but as a routing layer they add latency and lose context. You already made this call on Jul 9: route chatbot leads directly to the sales manager. This finding just makes it official for all inbound, not only the bot.

Fix
SM becomes the single routing point; CS keeps service, loses triage
Effort
Decision, not a build
Unlocks
Faster first-touch; one accountable owner for lead follow-up
Major4 · Two buyer types, one undifferentiated front door

Your buyers split cleanly: Scenario A knows the resin they need and wants to transact; Scenario B has an application problem and needs formulation help. Today both get the same brochure site and the same phone number. The A buyer wants speed; the B buyer wants expertise. Serving them identically underserves both.

Fix
Branch logic in the chatbot: A → qualify + route fast; B → capture application details for SM
Effort
Inside chatbot scope
Unlocks
Better-qualified leads; the branch design IS the real work
Major5 · 6–12 month cycles with zero re-engagement machinery

A prospect who inquires in July may not buy until next spring. Nothing today holds that thread — no captured email, no session persistence, no nurture touch. Every long-cycle inquiry decays back to zero unless a human remembers.

Fix
Chatbot captures email early; SugarCRM lead keeps the thread; simple email fallback for follow-up
Effort
Base-scope add-on
Unlocks
Pipeline that survives the sales cycle instead of leaking
Opportunity6 · The PD form archive is an unmined moat

Years of product-development forms encode which formulations solved which application problems. That's training data no competitor has. It's also the one asset that requires care: NDA first (your call, and the right one), then structure the taxonomy, then build the recommendation layer on top of a chatbot that's already working.

Fix
NDA → PD form data dump → taxonomy pass → Claude-powered recommendation engine (Phase 5)
Effort
Months, staged
Unlocks
The digital formulator — the thing you actually described wanting
Opportunity7 · Zapier free tier is a ceiling — but you don't need to raise it

The free plan can't be the automation backbone. Good news: it doesn't need to be. SugarCRM's REST API means the chatbot writes leads directly — no middleman, no monthly automation tax, one less thing that silently breaks.

Fix
Direct REST integration; keep Zapier free for incidentals
Effort
Inside chatbot scope
Unlocks
$0/mo automation cost; fewer failure points
Strength8 · The sales manager already runs close/loss analysis

Most companies your size have nobody doing win/loss discipline. Yours does it unprompted. That makes the SM the natural owner of chatbot-sourced leads — and the person whose 30 minutes of input will make the branch logic real instead of guessed. Build around this strength.

Action
30-min SM call before branch-logic session 1
Effort
One calendar invite
Unlocks
Branch logic grounded in real close/loss patterns
Strength9 · WordPress 7.0 + SugarCRM = boring, compatible, perfect

An up-to-date WordPress and a CRM with an open REST API is exactly the stack you want under this: every serious chatbot vendor supports it natively, and nothing here requires a replatform. The foundation is fine — it's the instruments bolted to it that were missing.

Action
Nothing. Don't let anyone sell you a replatform.
Effort
Zero
Unlocks
All budget goes to capability, not migration
Sequenced · chatbot first, per your call

Five phases. Each one pays for itself before the next starts.

You already re-sequenced this on Jul 9 — chatbot before the instrument-panel work. This roadmap honors that: visible win first, plumbing second, moat last.
P1
Chatbot MVP — the wow Now
Tidio on WordPress, A/B branch logic designed with the SM's close/loss input, leads written directly into SugarCRM via REST, routed to the sales manager by territory.
Effort 3 sessionsCalendar ~2 weeksNeeds SM call + WP admin access
Unlocks: first structured leads ever; the internal demo moment; proof the direct-to-SM routing works.
P2
Instrument panel — GA4 + GTM rebuild Next
Fresh GA4 property, clean GTM container, conversion events on chatbot interactions and Unbounce landers. Now PPC spend has a denominator.
Effort 1–2 sessionsNeeds GA/GTM + Unbounce access
Unlocks: cost-per-lead by channel; you can finally kill or scale PPC with numbers instead of vibes.
P3
Re-engagement layer Next
Email capture in every bot path, lead-status hygiene in SugarCRM, a simple follow-up cadence so 6–12 month buyers don't evaporate.
Effort 1–2 sessionsDepends on P1 live + real lead flow
Unlocks: pipeline that compounds across the long cycle instead of resetting every quarter.
P4
PD data foundation Later
NDA signed → PD forms + application sheets exported → taxonomy built (resin family × application × outcome). Unglamorous, and the whole moat depends on it.
Effort staged, weeksGate NDA (your call, correct)

P5
The digital formulator Later
Claude-powered recommendation engine on top of the P4 taxonomy: "I'm molding X for application Y" → suggested formulation family + routed to the right human. The Scenario-B buyer's dream, and yours.
Effort the real projectDepends on P1 traffic + P4 data
Unlocks: the thing no competitor can copy — twenty years of formulation judgment, awake 24/7 on your website.
