The ambition

Where you said you want this to go

Today: a manufacturer with a brochure site, paid landing pages that can't be measured, and buyers who have to already know what they want and pick up the phone.
──────────▶
Your stated end-state: an AI agent trained on PRL's product-development history that can take "I'm molding X for application Y" and recommend a formulation. A digital version of your best formulator, working the website 24/7. That's not a chatbot. That's a moat. The chatbot is step one of building it.

Nobody else in specialty resins at your scale is doing this. The PD form data you've accumulated is proprietary. A competitor can copy your website in a week, but they can't copy twenty years of formulation decisions. The roadmap tab sequences how to get there without betting everything on the endgame up front.

Instrument-by-instrument

Nine findings. Each one has a fix, an effort, and a payoff.

Ordered by severity, not by sequence. The Roadmap tab handles sequencing.
Critical · the move1 · The website can't capture a lead. This is the chatbot gap

Right now the site generates zero leads on its own. A buyer who finds prlresins.com at 9pm has no way to start a conversation. They have to already know to pick up the phone. In a 6–12 month sales cycle, the vendor who captures the inquiry first usually wins it. This is exactly what the chatbot fixes, and it's the piece you asked to start with.

Fix
Tidio chatbot → creates a lead in SugarCRM, routed straight to the sales manager
Effort
3 sessions · ~$1,552.50
Unlocks
24/7 lead capture; the visible win; the foundation for the AI vision
Critical2 · You're spending on ads with no way to measure them

You pay for traffic to your Unbounce landing pages but can't see what happens after the click. Your analytics (GA4) is dark and the tag setup is stale. At $100M revenue, even a small ad budget deserves a working read on what each lead costs. Right now the answer to "are the ads working?" is a shrug.

Fix
Rebuild the analytics from scratch (faster than fixing the stale setup)
Effort
1 session
Unlocks
Cost-per-lead by channel, right after the chatbot
Major3 · CS sits in the lead path as an accidental gatekeeper

Three CS reps in Farmington field inbound and relay to sales. They're good at service, but as a routing layer they add latency and lose context. You already made this call on Jul 9: route chatbot leads directly to the sales manager. This finding just makes it official for all inbound, not only the bot.

Fix
The sales manager becomes the single routing point; customer service keeps service, drops triage
Effort
Decision, not a build
Unlocks
Faster first-touch; one accountable owner for lead follow-up
Major4 · Two buyer types, one undifferentiated front door

Your buyers split cleanly: Scenario A knows the resin they need and wants to transact; Scenario B has an application problem and needs formulation help. Today both get the same brochure site and the same phone number. The A buyer wants speed; the B buyer wants expertise. Serving them identically underserves both.

Fix
Two paths in the chatbot: A → qualify + route fast; B → capture application details for the sales manager
Effort
Inside chatbot scope
Unlocks
Better-qualified leads; designing those two paths IS the real work
Major5 · 6–12 month cycles with zero re-engagement machinery

A prospect who inquires in July may not buy until next spring. Nothing today holds that thread. No captured email, no session persistence, no nurture touch. Every long-cycle inquiry decays back to zero unless a human remembers.

Fix
Chatbot captures email early; SugarCRM lead keeps the thread; simple email fallback for follow-up
Effort
Base-scope add-on
Unlocks
Pipeline that survives the sales cycle instead of leaking
Opportunity6 · The PD form archive is an unmined moat

Years of product-development forms record which formulations solved which application problems. That's training data no competitor has. The path: organize the forms, then build the recommendation AI on top of a chatbot that's already working. An NDA is optional here. Happy to sign one if you'd like, but it's not required to start.

Fix
Export the forms → organize them → build the recommendation AI on top (the later phase)
Effort
Months, staged
Unlocks
The digital formulator, the thing you actually described wanting
Opportunity7 · Zapier free tier is a ceiling, but you don't need to raise it

The free plan can't be the thing everything runs through. Good news: it doesn't need to be. SugarCRM's built-in connection lets the chatbot write leads directly. No middleman, no monthly automation fee, one less thing that silently breaks.

Fix
Connect the chatbot to SugarCRM directly; keep Zapier free for odds and ends
Effort
Inside chatbot scope
Unlocks
$0/mo automation cost; fewer failure points
Strength8 · The sales manager already runs close/loss analysis

Most companies your size have nobody doing win/loss discipline. Yours does it unprompted. That makes the sales manager the natural owner of chatbot leads, and the person whose 30 minutes of input will make the question flow real instead of guessed. Build around this strength.

Action
30-min sales-manager call before we design the question flow
Effort
One calendar invite
Unlocks
A question flow grounded in real close/loss patterns
Strength9 · WordPress 7.0 + SugarCRM = boring, compatible, perfect

An up-to-date WordPress and a CRM that connects easily is exactly the setup you want: every serious chatbot works with it out of the box, and nothing here needs rebuilding. The foundation is fine. It's the tools bolted to it that were missing.

Action
Nothing. Don't let anyone sell you a replatform.
Effort
Zero
Unlocks
All budget goes to capability, not migration
