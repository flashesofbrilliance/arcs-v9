Architecture — six layers
01
Sources
OUT →
Competitor press rooms, changelog pages, G2/Capterra reviews, SEC EDGAR filings, job posting pages (not profile data), company social accounts, your own CRM deal notes.
IN →
Feature parity gap analysis via Playwright / Stagehand / Browserbase — what competitors have shipped that Juniper hasn't. Verify ToS on target product UIs before automating; scope collaboratively, not unilaterally.
02
Triggers
Vercel edge functions + Google News Alert connectors. Fires on: new competitor post · 5+ AI/ML hires in 30 days · exec change · funding round · security incident · G2 review mentioning pricing. Trigger = signal, not noise.
03
Enrichment
Tier A (if subscriptions exist): Gartner · CB Insights · Privco. Tier B (default, no subscriptions needed): Clay enrichment + public sources — covers the majority of competitive signal at zero marginal cost. Start here, upgrade as needed.
04
Processing
Claude claude-sonnet-5 per event. Constitutional guardrails defined with non-technical stakeholders upfront. Four outputs: who · what (product/pricing/hiring/exec/security) · relevance score 1–5 · stakeholder tag (CEO/CRO/CMO/CFO/product).
05
Outputs
Presentation layer fully abstracted from backend. CEO: 30-sec Slack digest. Analyst: deep drill-down, full sourcing. Sales: auto-updated battlecard. Product: feature gap log from reviews + browser intel.
06
Evals
Deterministic and falsifiable. Recall: did we catch event X within Y hours? Precision: competitor classification correct? Grounding: summary faithful to source? Coverage: all 40 competitors monitored? Weekly eval report. Thresholds trigger human review.
