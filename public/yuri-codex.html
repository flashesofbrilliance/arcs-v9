02 · Source to target
Decompose, re-render, verify

Translated for each reader, not summarized.

Everyone gets the decision in their own language, and six evals prove nothing was lost in the move.

The same signal, spoken in each reader's language

Think of it as translation. The receipt is written once in a source register. Each reader needs it in their own target register: the exec's five bullets, the engineer's constraint. The engine decomposes the source signal and recomposes it at the target altitude through what we call the rhetorical resonance engine. What that engine does with semantic intent and inferred meaning stays on our side of the fence. What crosses to you is the set of checks that prove the translation held.

The evals that guarantee lossless decomposition and recomposition:

  • Fidelity (round-trip). The target render must reconstruct back to the source intent. If it cannot round-trip, it is not shipped.
  • Intent preservation. The why survives, not just the what. A summary that keeps the fact and drops the reason fails this.
  • Load-bearing completeness. Nothing load-bearing disappears between altitudes. What genuinely cannot cross at a given altitude is logged as withheld, never silently cut.
  • Resonance. Does the target actually land at the reader's altitude, in their register, at their resolution. This is the adaptive-altitude check, below.
  • Provenance. Every rendered claim traces to a source line. If it is a read, it is labelled a read.
  • Drift. Re-rendering the same source twice must not contradict itself. Phase lock, below, is what this eval protects.
The fence
You see the evals that prove fidelity held. How the transform reads semantic intent and inferred projected meaning off the parse is the recipe, and the recipe stays under the fence. Some of it is patentable.

06 · Adaptive altitude & phase lock
Meet each reader, keep them aligned

Meets each reader, keeps them aligned.

Renders to your altitude on its own, and re-locks every altitude the moment the source updates.

Adaptive altitude

The signal renders itself at the reader's altitude automatically. The exec gets five bullets, the engineer gets the constraint, from one source. Never dumbed down, never buried in detail the reader does not own. The resonance eval is what enforces it: a render that lands at the wrong altitude fails and is re-cut.

Phase lock

Every altitude stays pointed at the same horizon line. When the source updates, every render re-locks to it in step, so no two readers are ever holding contradictory versions. The drift eval guards this. Phase lock is the difference between one org seeing one truth at many resolutions, and five teams quietly diverging.

07 · Amplification
Two effects, not one

Agreement and disagreement both amplify.

Aligned signals harden into conviction; structured discord surfaces the blind spot. Two instruments, not one.

Constructive interference vs productive discord

When signals meet, they can amplify two different ways, and the engine treats them as different instruments. You named both on the call.

Constructive interference

Agreement amplifies into conviction. When independent readers, at different altitudes or in different departments, land on the same read from uncorrelated evidence, the waves are in phase and the amplitude grows. Confidence hardens because the confirmation was not correlated. This is how a bet earns the right to be acted on.

Productive discord

Disagreement amplifies into discovery. When readers diverge, structured discord raises signal, not noise. The surgical view sees a failure mode the exec cannot; the divergence is the information. It gets banked, not smoothed. This is the staff-of-chiefs round table converging into forward torque.

The difference is the point. Interference amplifies agreement into conviction; discord amplifies disagreement into discovery. A system that only rewards agreement is an echo chamber. One that only rewards disagreement is noise. The engine needs both, and it keeps them labelled, because uncorrelated echoes that happen to align are the strongest signal there is.

What I laid out, our call
"how do I take all these divergent perspectives and have them converge in a place where there's constructive interference and productive discord... create something generative where there's forward momentum and forward torque."
