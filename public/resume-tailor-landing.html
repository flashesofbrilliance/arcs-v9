01

The one rule

the moat

Most AI resume tools optimize for impressive. This one optimizes for defensibly true. It decomposes every claim into hard / soft / tacit, attaches the evidence and a verifiability grade, and never states what the evidence can't defend. Impressive-but-false is a ruin vector, not a rounding error — a resume goes through background and reference checks. A claim that sounds good but is false is worse than a weaker true claim. Proficiency scores are provisional hypotheses the falsifier exists to confirm, never facts asserted on your behalf.

02

Every claim is graded

honesty policy
● documented

Public and inspectable — a repo, a commit count, a live URL, a credential badge. Independently checkable. State it freely.

● self-reported

Your figure — plausible, consistent with your record, but not independently verified here. State it as yours; mirror what your public profile already discloses.

● unverifiable

Tacit or subjective. May frame the work, never stated as fact with a number. Flagged as a defend-or-drop for interview prep.

03

The method

evidence → gauntlet
1
GATHER
Render the job description; parse your LinkedIn export as ground truth for titles, dates, and certs. Prior resumes are self-reported, not authoritative.
2
GRADED SSOT
Build one competency model — every claim tagged hard/soft/tacit with its evidence, verifiability grade, and disclosure rule. One source; every render reads from it.
3
FALSIFY
Flag overclaims, single-engagement specialties, borrowed terms-of-art, and the NDA boundary (public fact stays, nature-of-the-work goes). The output is your interview-prep list.
4
PROJECT
Rank domains by JD-relevance; lead with the literal match, differentiator second. Experience reverse-chron, framing by relevance — an Exec → Surgical depth ladder.
5
RENDER
A print-safe framed PDF, a matching cover letter, and an ATS-plain companion for the upload. Then rasterize and look at the pixels — page-count is not a pass.
6
GAUNTLET
An adversarial pre-send pass: simulate the hiring panel across facets at high burden of proof, verify every flag against the evidence, apply the survivors. Subtractive, never inflating.
