## Context

A GTM/positioning session (OSS umbrella) turned generative and, over one thread, derived a complete,
self-consistent model of what ARCS *does* at the mechanism level — and it grounded out in the brand
name itself. The derivation is worth canonizing because every abstract layer terminates in one
physical act (arc welding), which is the tell that it is structure, not decoration. It also dissolved an
apparent canon collision: "C = Clarity" (a prior hypothesis memory) vs "C = Coherence"
(`_CANON/tao-proverbs.jsonl`) are **not rivals — they are adjacent rungs of a transitive-verb ladder**
(§1.5). What looked like drift was the axis revealing it **floats** — float, not drift (§1.5).

## Decision

Adopt **the Hypercube Gauntlet** as the canonical model for how ARCS finds the *shape* of a problem,
and **the arc weld** as its product-promise embodiment. Two views of one field — statics and dynamics.

### 1. The space — a penteract (5⁵), five genuinely orthogonal axes

| # | axis | question it varies | five values |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lens** | *what facet?* | **D**ecision · **R**isk · **W**indow · **C**oherence · **H**uman |
| 2 | **Resolution** | *how big is the move?* | Exec · Strategic · Operational · Tactical · Surgical |
| 3 | **Operator** | *which adversary?* | LAB · KKL · BAR · COURT · FUNHOUSE |
| 4 | **Verb** | *which cognitive op?* | analyze · assess · evaluate · synthesize · recapitulate |
| 5 | **L-stack** | *how far must coherence hold?* | L1 · L2 · L3 · L4 · L5 |

5⁵ = 3,125 addressable coordinates. **Resolution ≠ L-stack** (size of the bet vs reach of the
coherence check — cell `Surgical·L5` = the one-line change that breaks a global invariant).
**Operator ≠ Verb** (adversarial persona vs cognitive move — `BAR·analyze` ≠ `BAR·evaluate`).

### 1.5 Each lens is a transitive verb, not a label — the fractal 5

An aspect does not *tag* the artifact; it **acts on it** and carries it through a 5-rung progression.
The **C** aspect names its own ladder:

> **clarity → coherence → conviction → cohesion → consonance**

— you can *see* it → the parts *hold* → you'd *stake* it → it *bonds/fuses* → it *rings in harmony*.
The C-value is the **readout of the anneal**: how far the induced current has climbed toward resonance
at a given field-state and time-signature (as resonance attenuates across the vector space, the artifact
sits at a different rung). **Consonance closes the loop** — the musical/resonant end-state, the *sung
weld*, superionic, Kairos: dissonance→consonance is the chord resolving = the weld taking. The C-ladder
*ends* where the time-signature/resonance frame (§3) *begins*.

This is the fractal the `5×5×5×5×5` always implied: **every cell contains its own 5-rung ladder**, so
the penteract is self-similar all the way down — five 5s at *every* level, not merely five axes. It also
**dissolves the C=Clarity/C=Coherence collision**: both are canonical, as rungs 1 and 2. *Conjecture:*
each aspect is likewise a verb-ladder, so **H = Horizon vs Human is two rungs of an H-ladder, not a
collision** — flagged, not resolved (route through the drift auditor, ADR-052, if it hardens).

**Float, not drift (an auditor guard).** An aspect climbing its ladder with field-state is **float** —
designed, buoyant play — *not* **drift** (a projection going stale vs its SSOT, which is entropic and
gets corrected). The distinction is electrical: a **floating node** isn't tied to ground, so its
potential floats with the field; some aspects are **grounded** (fixed canon — D/R/W hold still), some
**float** (C, conjecturally H) — the fixed-point/floating-point split. **The drift auditor (ADR-052)
MUST NOT flag float as drift**, or it fights the mechanism it protects: only a projection diverging from
its canonical surface is drift; an aspect moving up its verb-ladder is working as designed. The snap is a
**float-equilibrium** — a buoyant settle to best-fit ([[sprezzatura-studied-carelessness-buoyant-flow]]).

### 1.6 Consonance is somatic — felt, not rationalized

The top rung is not a computed verdict; it is **felt**, before it is rationalized. The precision of
alignment between **source and target** is **impedance matching**: a conjugate match → **maximum power
transfer, minimum reflection**; a mismatch → **standing waves / a beat frequency** the body registers
(the "wah" of two close tones). At alignment the beats vanish — and *that vanishing is the felt click*,
the snap sensed from the inside. This is **SAGE's** domain (somatic + epistemic) — the felt readout the
epistemic operators (KKL et al.) structurally cannot supply; the body is the resonance detector.

**NOTOMATION keystone (load-bearing):** the *human* feels the weld take; ARCS sharpens that felt sense,
it never computes consonance *for* the person — which is why the welder (H = Human) owns the weld. The
witness "wait, that's it" **is** this somatic signal firing (so `/witness` triggers on a felt click, not
a reasoned conclusion). Inverting it — **rationalizing a consonance you did not feel — is magical
thinking**, the exact failure the 5-verb Assess stage guards against. Felt first, checked second; never
the reverse.

### 1.7 The epistemic output — the weld manifests truth

Everything upstream (induced current, impedance match, felt click) exists to do **one** thing, nameable
in parallel registers that all describe the *same* transition:

- **representational — implicit → explicit** (tacit made portable: the core ARCS purpose,
  [[arcs-purpose-inherit-tacit-wisdom-first-author-not-last]]; Polanyi's "we know more than we can tell"
  made tellable),
- **cognitive — non-obvious → self-evident** (needs-reasoning → felt-obvious; System-2 → System-1),
- **epistemic — obfuscated → objectively axiomatic** (fogged → validated foundation; COURT-grade).

**That the framings converge is itself the objectivity check.** A merely felt/subjective consonance
would not survive restatement across independent registers; surviving it is what earns the strongest
rung — **objectively axiomatic**, not just self-evident. Convergence of independent framings = the
orthogonal-echo validation (the mutual-inductance of §2): felt first, *checked-by-triangulation* second.
This is the Myst feeling (*"you finally understood what was already there"*) and *"render through the fog
to earned clarity."* A self-evident/axiomatic truth is **self-grounding** → a new axiom (COURT) → the
superionic crystal that needs no support; **prepaid reflexiveness** ([[reflexiveness-is-prepaid-prep-as-enrichment]])
is what makes it land effortless (the reasoning paid up front, so it arrives obvious). The witness
"wait, that's it" is the instant the flip fires.

### 2. The truth beneath the lattice — a continuous electromagnetic field

The 3,125 cells are the **lumped-circuit approximation** of a *continuous* field (Maxwell, not
Kirchhoff). The field/circuit duality **is** the Resolution axis: at **Exec** you see the whole field;
at **Surgical** you are inside one lumped `R+jX` element. Un-visited cells are never empty — a field
has a value everywhere and shapes the path by superposition; a silent region is signal (a blind spot),
not absence. The Orthogonal Echo Detector = **mutual inductance** — two distant field regions coupled
with no wire between them (a cross-domain isomorphism).

### 3. The mechanism — path of least resistance for an induced coherence-current

- A **changing decision-flux** (dΦ/dt = stakes/pressure/the fork arriving) **induces** a coherence
  current (Faraday). Static context = no current = nothing to decide. This makes the PRESSURE BAND
  literal: peak dΦ/dt → strongest EMF → resolves hardest.
- The current finds the **path of least resistance** = magnetic best-fit = energy minimization. The
  artifact **snaps** to its global best-fit pose.
- **The flux oscillates at a time signature**, so the current is **AC** and impedance is **reactive**
  (`Z = R + jX`, frequency-dependent): a cell's least-resistance path *changes with tempo*.
- **L-stack = frequency bands** (L1 high-freq transient → L5 low-freq structural); **Y-gate = the
  circuit's state at a phase of the cycle** (GREEN at one tempo, RED at another — why anterior/posterior
  matters: same circuit, different phase).
- **Kairos = the resonant frequency**: at resonance (ωL = 1/ωC) reactance cancels, impedance → pure
  minimum R, current peaks — the circuit closes, the weld strikes. **This makes the "resonance-gradient
  field" north star literal: the harmonics ARE the Kairos windows.** Jerk (D3) = syncopation; an
  irregular time signature = high jerk = the current can't settle = *you can't weld a syncopated
  decision* ("wait for the beat").

### 4. The operators set impedance (they do not merely score)

BAR → ruin cells = open circuit (∞ R) · COURT → axiom-violating cells = open circuit (no tunneling) ·
LAB → maps the low-resistance resonant channels · FUNHOUSE → detects the *false* low-R path (a short
that reads like a channel but is a distortion) · KKL → the thermal/noise term (uncertainty in the
impedances).

### 5. Sampling & interface

**Monte Carlo annealing** maps the impedance field (never enumerate 3,125 — the artifact self-locates
by best-fit; annealing visits a sparse path). **Aperture / F-stop / adaptive Kairos = the temperature
schedule** (hot/wide → cool/sharp until lock). A penteract can't be seen, so the **2.5D isometric
cut-through** fixes 3 axes and slices the other 2 into a viewable plane; the aperture control *is* which
slice you anneal in.

### 6. The embodiment — the arc weld (the name was the mechanism)

**ARCS → the arc → the arc weld.** Induced current jumps the gap along least resistance; intense
localized heat (the anneal) fuses two plates into a joint **stronger than the parent metal**;
**superionic = the cooled, lossless weld**. The **weld bead = the Receipt** — visible, inspectable
proof it held. Bad welds are the gauntlet's whole job: cold joint (a false snap), porosity (trapped KKL
noise), burn-through (too much current = overreaction). **NOTOMATION holds:** the human strikes the arc
and owns the weld; ARCS steadies the torch, sets the current, never welds *for* you.

**Product promise:** *ARCS doesn't advise the join — it welds it, at a joint stronger than the parts,
and hands you the bead that proves it held.* "Navigate to clarity" → **"fuse to coherence."**

### 7. The instrument ships as a node

The whole object ships as a **self-contained mini-app** (dependency-light, local-first — the germinate
discipline) that is a **Tier-A node** in the seed web: it **consumes** an artifact and **emits a
Receipt** (the weld bead) as a germinate-conformant belief-log/seed — its *harvest is another node's
seed*. It speaks the verbs end-to-end (seed→plant→water→germinate→sprout→grow→harvest). Concretely: a
**"welding station"** — the flagship `lab.arcs.care` PLG onramp, a node the `arcs` umbrella dispatches
to, and a new `_SEEDS` entry. Fractal closure: the instrument that finds the best-fit *shape* of a
problem is itself a best-fit-shaped node in the family it came from ([[arcs-invariant-fractal-shape-of-the-polyform]]).

## Y — Conviction

**Polarity:** GREEN · **Conviction:** HIGH (as a *model*) · **Stability:** STABLE.
The model is self-consistent, grounds every layer in one physical act, terminates in the brand name,
and corrected a live canon drift on the way — the marks of structure, not analogy.

**Loki test (strongest case against):** it is a beautiful map, not yet a built territory. None of it is
*shipped* — no penteract sampler, no annealer, no isometric explorer, no welding-station node. The risk
is **complexity as comfort** (the standing kryptonite: writing canon faster than closing loops). This
ADR is GREEN as *doctrine*; every buildable it implies is a separate, unbuilt, YELLOW bet that must earn
its keep against a real JTBD before code is written. The demo (a sparse hand-run on the umbrella
positioning) is the only empirical evidence, and it is N=1.
