## D — The Decision

**Sharp question:** ADR-070 named ARCS as two surfaces — Receipt (deductive) and Induction (abductive). But a two-sided object has an inside and an outside you cross between. Is that the real topology? And why is the ARCS logo a Möbius strip rather than a circle, an arc, or a two-sided ribbon?

**Decision — three claims, canon:**

1. **One surface, not two (the Möbius).** Receipt and Induction are not two sides of a ribbon; they are two *traversals of a single non-orientable surface*. You do not cross from the deductive side to the abductive side — you walk the induction surface (forge the instrument for the pre-verbal job), keep walking, and arrive at the receipt (the earned record) having been on "both" without ever lifting off. Deduction and abduction are one continuous strip with a half-twist: **the record you attest and the instrument you forged to get there are the same surface seen after half a turn.** This is why the mark is a Möbius, not an arc. The name says ARCS; the geometry says the arc closes on itself with a twist.

2. **The triune minimum is load-bearing, and it was flagged in November 2025.** The Book of ARCS (Nov 2025, earliest artifact) states it verbatim: *"Two is unstable. Three is the minimum for coherence... Minimum viable system = 3 distinct energies,"* with the failure mode explicit — *"When It Breaks: One voice dominates."* That is **none-may-null**: collapse any one of three distinct forces and the system reverts to binary conflict (A vs B). This is the same invariant drawn as the **Invariant Vectors Triad** (Red / Gold / Vermillion, none-may-null) in the OS deck and encoded as Triune Logic (NULL / OM / INFINITE). The triad is not decoration layered on later; it is the minimum-coherence structure named at the root and re-derived at every scale since.

3. **The prototype is a gravity well; drift is a displacement it pulls home.** The ARCS corpus (constitution, canon, the Phase-3 Staff-of-Chiefs gauntlet spec) has enough coherent *mass* to exert a restoring force on the live Claude Code build. On 2026-08-28 the build drifted (the Hyperstition Atlas over-claimed causation); the session *unwittingly executed the vision's own Phase-3 gauntlet* on that artifact; the correction filed (SEDIMENT tier, chip demotions). NOTOMATION-AS-GRAVITY (Jan vision) made dynamical: automation orbits the human narrative's center of mass; the well corrects drift, it does not prevent it — exactly as a mass returns to the bottom of a potential well.

The three are one object: a **non-orientable surface** (1) whose minimum stable cross-section is a **triad, none-may-null** (2), held at the bottom of a **potential well** by its own accumulated mass (3). Möbius = the topology; triad = the invariant; gravity = the dynamics.
