## D — The Decision

**Sharp question:** The job search had been running as outbound fit — find roles, pour "me" into each role×gatekeeper. Two things were unsettled: (1) *what* is being placed, and by what search arrow? (2) how does that thing get found without linear outbound labor? This session (germinated from Amy's independent validation of the rare pair) resolved both. This ADR is the **sovereign-zero source** every downstream surface projects from; it does not decree the forward bet proven (see Y).

**Decision — canon (five claims):**

1. **User0 is the prime invariant, and the search arrow is INVERTED.** Zach is not a candidate to be fitted to roles; he is the oddest tool for the job — a cross-domain operator-who-ships. The arrow is not tool-for-the-job (which candidate fits this role) but **role-for-the-tool** (which JTBD is unsatisfiable without this exact combination). **fit ≠ leverage:** applicable-to-many is a commodity; only-tool-for-one is a moat. Magnetize for the only-tool role. Blacksmith was one instance.

2. **Oddness IS the selectivity; emit at full fidelity, never sand it off.** The odd shape repels the common jobs (they were scatter) and resonates only with the rare JTBD. Normalizing to look applicable to more jobs flattens the well and attracts tourists. The tool's true shape is the **lossless invariant that must survive every projection** — role, gatekeeper, and pitch are changes of basis (field-geometry canon); a pitch that strains to fit is deforming the tool for a job that was never its rare job.

3. **The atomic unit = {role shape} × {gatekeeper: internal | external} × {candidate = User0} → pitch.** The pitch is the *projection* of the candidate into the role shape, rendered in the gatekeeper's register — an output, not a leg. **Internal/external is the routing key** (external → recruiter-enablement / arm-to-resell; internal → direct checkable proof). **Air-gap = argument-ownership-by-vertex, not just secrecy:** each JTBD note is answered in the object whose audience owns that argument. The triad-closer is a **PAIR** — a founder-aimed forge + an air-gapped recruiter enablement — and neither alone closes it.

4. **Magnetize: emit the invariant so the basin falls in (inbound), rather than only chasing (outbound).** Two scales: the **field** (broad owned surfaces — good.arcs.care, NFTT, instruments) and the **forge** (per-triad object). Outbound seeds the field; magnetism harvests it. Sharp polarity is load-bearing: one crossover signal, one basin register. Field-strength readout = inbound convergence ratio + basin-people engaging emissions *before any role exists*.

5. **This is a class, not a one-off (User0 → User1).** Swap User0 for a portable Resume + LinkedIn input and the same triple is the general **ARCS talent-placement primitive** (`_SPECS/self-serve-atlas-scan-v1.md`). The champion-sourcing machine built for Zach is the User0 instance of that primitive.
