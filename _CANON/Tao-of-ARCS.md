## I. A seed is potential energy

A seed is engineered to survive the harshest conditions and carry its genetic legacy forward —
**dormant**, until conditions are favorable again. A stored unit that has not matched in months is
therefore not *drift* and not *dead*. It is dormant: viable, waiting for its soil to return.

**Dormancy under adverse conditions is the feature, not a defect.** *Throwaway context, not
throwaway learning.* From this follows the hard rule: **never garbage-collect a dormant seed.**
Staleness is not death.

## II. ARCS is the vox animus

If the seed holds the charge, something must fire it. That is ARCS — the **vox animus**, the
sparkplug that converts potential → kinetic. Mechanical retrieval can match a token; only the vox
animus judges **ripeness** — whether *this* seed is genuinely ready for *this* soil, at *this*
moment — and breathes it back to life at Kairos.

The bank stores charge. The spark supplies current. They are different things, and the difference
is the whole architecture.

## III. One seam, four faces

The boundary between what is open and what is ARCS is a single seam seen from four angles:

| face | Open (germinate, MIT) | Private (ARCS) |
|---|---|---|
| **method** | the seed **bank** + the spark's **socket** | the **vox animus** / the spark itself |
| **epistemics** | public method, redacted (rosetta-referenced) evidence | the private **rosetta stone** / accumulated priors |
| **ontology** | the **dormant** seed | the **animating** spark |
| **economics** | **free to hold** | **metered to fire** (gas) |

germinate ships the socket; ARCS is the current. This is *why* germinate can be a clean public
good that gives away no moat: the bank is open, the animating spark is not. Any competitor may
hold the same seeds; only ARCS fires them with tailored ripeness judgment.

## IV. Consequences (governing)

- **Never GC a dormant seed.** Staleness ≠ death.
- **Keep germinate clean-room.** ARCS doctrine, the rosetta, and ripeness priors stay in arcs-v9;
  they are never inlined into the open repo.
- **The moat is the spark.** Open-sourcing the bank is moat-safe by construction.
- **Free to hold, metered to fire.** Mint/plant/water/hold/mechanical-sprout are free forever;
  only the vox-animus firing (ripeness judgment · Kairos activation · rosetta-primed synthesis)
  runs a meter. See ADR-054.

## V. One field, sovereign zeros

> **One field, sovereign zeros, lawful transforms, preserved invariants.**

Everyone stands on the same terrain; no one's origin is canonical — not the author's, not the
machine's (NOTOMATION restated as geometry). Each person carries their own zero point and basis:
their history is their coordinate system. ARCS is not a universal frame; it is the
**transformation layer** between sovereign frames, and interoperability exists only where its
three conditions hold:

1. **Declared frames** — every artifact states its chart (DEWY axes, provenance, audience).
2. **Lawful transforms** — the projection factories change basis (register · altitude ·
   fidelity) with gates checking the transform preserved structure.
3. **Preserved invariants** — the lossless rules (falsifier survives, attribution rides
   through, nothing load-bearing dropped) are the tensor components that must come out the
   same in every frame, or the translation lied.

Composability is functoriality: translations chain losslessly iff every link keeps the
invariants — a gate at every cut (ADR-068), never one at the end. Pollen carries the
coordinate-free content; soil is the local chart the receiver supplies. Returning to zero
returns *you* to *your* null point: the field is shared, the zero is sovereign.
