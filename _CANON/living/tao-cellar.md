### The wave engine (anticipatory delivery — the proactive half)

Content is surfed, not scheduled. Spot the swell → route the right surfer → drop at the right time:

1. **Wave-spotter** — Trend Surfing: velocity × acceleration × decay on a real signal, *before* it
   breaks. Deterministic on the signal; `defer` while the signal is thin (young-vine swell is a guess).
2. **Switchboard operator** — affinity-match each swelling wave to the surfer that fits it: a front
   verse, a surface, a teardown angle. Match by resonance, not by category.
3. **Kairos** — `detectKairos()` (built): hit the wave at the golden-time, one touch, not too early,
   not too late.
The surfer rides substance from the cellar/front; the board is cut to the DESIGN.md form.
